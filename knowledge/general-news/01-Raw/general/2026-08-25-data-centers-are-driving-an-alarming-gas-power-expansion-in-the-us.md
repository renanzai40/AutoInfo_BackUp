The article discusses how the rapid growth of data centers in the US is driving an alarming expansion of natural gas power projects, with many either proposed or under construction. This trend raises environmental concerns due to increased carbon emissions and reliance on fossil fuels.

- This expansion is considered alarming, likely due to its environmental impact, such as contributing to climate change.
- The data center boom reflects a broader trend of increasing energy consumption from digital technologies.
