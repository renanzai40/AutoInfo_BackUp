## Original Content
Thanks to a dwindling supply of open roles, “one-click” applications, and the rise of artificial intelligence, it’s easier than ever to apply for a job. We’re all paying the price.

## Summary
The ease of job applications, facilitated by one-click applications and AI, has increased, leading to negative consequences. This surge in easy applications is partly due to a dwindling supply of open roles. The overall job market and hiring processes are suffering as a result.

## Key Points
- One-click applications and AI have made job applying easier than ever
- A dwindling supply of open job roles contributes to the ease of application
- The ease of application has negative consequences on the job market and hiring processes
