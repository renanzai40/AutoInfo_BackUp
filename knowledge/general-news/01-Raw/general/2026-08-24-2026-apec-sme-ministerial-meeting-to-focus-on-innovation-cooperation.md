## Original Content
The 32nd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Small and Medium Enterprises (SME) Ministerial Meeting will be held on Sept. 4 in Guangzhou, south China's Guangdong Province, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) said on Monday.

## Summary
The 32nd APEC SME Ministerial Meeting will be held on September 4, 2026, in Guangzhou, China. The meeting, announced by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT), will focus on innovation and cooperation among small and medium enterprises in the Asia-Pacific region.

## Key Points
- The meeting is the 32nd edition of the APEC SME Ministerial series.
- It will take place in Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China, on September 4, 2026.
- The primary themes are innovation and cooperation for small and medium enterprises.
- The event is organized or announced by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT).
