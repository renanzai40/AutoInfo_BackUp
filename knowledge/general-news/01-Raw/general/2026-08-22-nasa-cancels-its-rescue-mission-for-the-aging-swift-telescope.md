## Summary
NASA has canceled its rescue mission for the aging Swift Telescope due to control system problems with the LINK probe. The probe was designed to use robotic arms to capture and relocate the telescope into a safe orbit.

## Key Points
- The LINK probe was intended to capture the Swift Telescope using robotic arms.
- Control system issues forced NASA to abort the rescue attempt.
- The mission aimed to move the Swift Telescope to a safer orbit.
- The cancellation highlights challenges in space telescope maintenance.
