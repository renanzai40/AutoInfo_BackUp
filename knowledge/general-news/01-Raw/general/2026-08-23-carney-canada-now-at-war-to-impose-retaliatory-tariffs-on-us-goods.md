## Original Content
Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney said on Saturday that Canada was "at war" over trade with the United States and that, starting September 8, the country would impose tariffs on imports of US steel, electronics and other products in retaliation for US President Donald Trump's new 50% tariffs that took effect at midnight.

## Summary
Canada's Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that Canada is 'at war' with the US over trade and will impose retaliatory tariffs on US steel, electronics, and other products starting September 8. This move is in response to US President Donald Trump's 50% tariffs that took effect at midnight.

## Key Points
- Canada is entering a trade conflict with the US, described as 'at war'.
- Retaliatory tariffs will be imposed on US imports such as steel and electronics.
- The tariffs are scheduled to begin on September 8.
- This action follows the US imposition of 50% tariffs by President Donald Trump.
