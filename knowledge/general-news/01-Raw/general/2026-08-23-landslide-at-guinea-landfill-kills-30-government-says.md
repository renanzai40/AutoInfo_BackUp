## Original Content
Thirty people died in Guinea's capital Conakry on Sunday when a mound of rubbish in a landfill collapsed before dawn onto neighboring homes following heavy rains, the government said.

## Summary
A landslide at a landfill in Conakry, Guinea, killed 30 people when heavy rains caused a rubbish mound to collapse onto nearby homes. The incident occurred before dawn on Sunday and was reported by the government.

## Key Points
- 30 people died in the landslide.
- The event occurred in Conakry, the capital of Guinea.
- Heavy rains triggered the collapse of a rubbish mound at a landfill.
- The collapse affected neighboring homes.
- The government confirmed the incident.
