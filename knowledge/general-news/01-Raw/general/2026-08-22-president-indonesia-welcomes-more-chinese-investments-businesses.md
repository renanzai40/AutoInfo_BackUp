## Original Content
Indonesia welcomes more Chinese enterprises to invest and do business in the country, will continue to provide a sound business environment for Chinese companies, and opposes any ill-intentioned instigation and hype, Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto has said.

## Summary
Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto has announced that Indonesia welcomes more Chinese investments and businesses, committing to a sound business environment. The statement opposes any ill-intentioned instigation and hype, emphasizing openness to international cooperation.

## Key Points
- Indonesia is inviting Chinese enterprises to invest and operate in the country.
- The government will continue to provide a supportive business environment for Chinese companies.
- President Prabowo Subianto opposes negative narratives or instigation related to these investments.
