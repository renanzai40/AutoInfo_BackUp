## Original Content
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">当日，联合国环境规划署驻华代表处在北京举办2025年世界环境日主题活动，邀请来自联合国机构、政府部门、相关企业与机构的嘉宾，探讨交流治理塑料污染的政策与实践。</p> <p style="text-indent: 2em;">6月5日是世界环境日。据介绍，今年的世界环境日将重点关注塑料污染带来的影响，推动全球形成“拒绝、减少、再利用、回收、反思”的塑料治理共识。</p>

## Summary
The 2025 World Environment Day theme event was held in Beijing, organized by the United Nations Environment Programme Office in China. It focused on discussing policies and practices for managing plastic pollution to foster global consensus on strategies like refuse, reduce, reuse, recycle, and reflect.

## Key Points
- The event focused on the impact of plastic pollution for World Environment Day 2025.
- Representatives from UN agencies, government departments, enterprises, and organizations participated.
- Global consensus is being promoted on plastic management strategies: refuse, reduce, reuse, recycle, reflect.
- The event was organized by the UN Environment Programme Office in China in Beijing.
