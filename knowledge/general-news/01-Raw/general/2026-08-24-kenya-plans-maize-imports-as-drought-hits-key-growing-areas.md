## Summary
Kenya is planning to import maize due to drought affecting key growing areas, which could lead to a poor harvest. This may put pressure on maize supplies and increase the price of maize flour, a staple for millions of households.

## Key Points
- Drought is impacting key maize growing regions in Kenya.
- This could result in a poor harvest.
- The poor harvest may strain maize supplies and raise maize flour prices.
- Maize flour is a staple food for millions of households.
- Maize imports are being considered as a mitigation strategy.
