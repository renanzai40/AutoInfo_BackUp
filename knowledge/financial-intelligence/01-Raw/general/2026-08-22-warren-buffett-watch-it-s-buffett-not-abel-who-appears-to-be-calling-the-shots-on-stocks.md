## Original Content
New Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel appears to be letting Warren Buffett and portfolio manager Ted Weschler make all the decisions on the company's equities.

## Summary
New Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel is reportedly not involved in stock decisions, with Warren Buffett and Ted Weschler making all equity choices. This indicates that Buffett continues to hold significant influence over the company's investment strategies.

## Key Points
- Greg Abel has assumed the role of CEO at Berkshire Hathaway.
- Abel is not directly making stock decisions for the company.
- Warren Buffett and Ted Weschler are handling all equity investments.
- This suggests ongoing dominance by Buffett in financial matters.
