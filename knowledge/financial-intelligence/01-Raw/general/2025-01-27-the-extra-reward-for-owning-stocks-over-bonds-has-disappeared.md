## Original Content
There is little sign of crimped demand for equities among individual investors, who remain bullish after two years of blockbuster gains.

## Summary
The article discusses the disappearance of the equity risk premium, indicating that stocks no longer offer a significant extra reward over bonds. Despite this, individual investors remain bullish and continue to show strong demand for equities after two years of substantial gains.

## Key Points
- The equity risk premium has vanished, meaning stocks do not provide additional returns compared to bonds.
- Individual investors exhibit no signs of reduced demand for equities.
- Investor optimism persists following two years of blockbuster gains in the stock market.
- This trend suggests potential shifts in market sentiment or economic outlook.
