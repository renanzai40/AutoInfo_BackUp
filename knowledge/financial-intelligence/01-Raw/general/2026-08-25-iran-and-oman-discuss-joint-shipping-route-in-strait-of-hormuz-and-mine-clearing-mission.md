Iran and Oman on Tuesday discussed a proposal to establish a jointly managed shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz.

## Summary
Iran and Oman discussed a proposal to establish a jointly managed shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz, including a mine clearing mission. The discussion, which took place on Tuesday, aims to enhance maritime cooperation and safety in the region.

## Key Points
- Proposal for a jointly managed shipping route between Iran and Oman.
- Strait of Hormuz is the key geographic location for the route.
- Inclusion of a mine clearing mission to improve maritime security.
- Focus on strengthening regional maritime cooperation.
