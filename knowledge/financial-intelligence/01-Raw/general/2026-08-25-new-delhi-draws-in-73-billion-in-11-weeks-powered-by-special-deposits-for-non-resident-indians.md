## Original Content
India has attracted inflows of $73 billion in 11 weeks, driven mostly by foreign currency bank deposits made by non-resident Indians.

## Summary
India has attracted $73 billion in financial inflows in just 11 weeks, primarily driven by special foreign currency bank deposits from non-resident Indians. This influx underscores the effectiveness of policies targeting the Indian diaspora.

## Key Points
- India received $73 billion in inflows over 11 weeks.
- The inflows are mainly from special deposits by non-resident Indians.
- The deposits are in foreign currency bank accounts.
- This reflects strong engagement with the Indian diaspora.
