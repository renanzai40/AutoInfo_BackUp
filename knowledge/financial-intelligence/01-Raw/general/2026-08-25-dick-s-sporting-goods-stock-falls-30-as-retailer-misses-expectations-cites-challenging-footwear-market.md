## Original Content
Dick's Sporting Goods reported fiscal second-quarter earnings on Tuesday that missed Wall Street expectations amid what it called a "challenging" environment.

## Summary
Dick's Sporting Goods stock fell 30% after reporting fiscal second-quarter earnings that missed Wall Street expectations. The company cited a 'challenging' footwear market as a key factor in the underperformance.

## Key Points
- Dick's Sporting Goods stock declined by 30%.
- Earnings missed Wall Street expectations for the fiscal second quarter.
- The company identified a challenging environment, particularly in the footwear market.
- The report was based on Tuesday's earnings announcement.
