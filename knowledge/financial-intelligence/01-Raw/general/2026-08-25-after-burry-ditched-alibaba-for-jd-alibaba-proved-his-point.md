## Summary
Investor Michael Burry sold his Alibaba shares due to fears of share dilution. Alibaba then priced a $10.3 billion stock offering, which caused its share prices to drop, validating Burry's concerns.

## Key Points
- Michael Burry divested from Alibaba over share dilution concerns.
- Alibaba announced a $10.3 billion offering shortly after.
- The offering led to a decline in Alibaba's share prices.
- Burry shifted investments to JD.com.
