## Summary
HP has partnered with Huawei, a U.S.-blacklisted Chinese company, to license its WiFi technology. This deal highlights the global adoption of Huawei's technology despite U.S. restrictions on collaborations with American suppliers.

## Key Points
- HP is entering a licensing agreement with Huawei for WiFi technology.
- Huawei is blacklisted by the U.S., restricting its work with American suppliers like Google.
- The partnership demonstrates increasing global acceptance of Huawei's technology outside China.
