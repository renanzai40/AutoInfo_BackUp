## Original Content
The U.S. can pressure Chinese banks over Iran through access to its financial system, while Beijing is building alternatives such as CIPS.

## Summary
China relies on U.S. dollars for its economic operations but is developing alternative financial systems like CIPS to mitigate risks from potential U.S. sanctions. The U.S. can exert pressure on Chinese banks through its financial system, particularly in the context of Iran sanctions. This strategy aims to reduce China's dependence on American-controlled financial networks.

## Key Points
- The U.S. has leverage over Chinese banks via access to its financial system, especially regarding Iran sanctions.
- China is actively building independent financial alternatives to hedge against U.S. sanctions.
- CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) is a key technology developed by China for this purpose.
- These efforts could weaken U.S. financial dominance and reduce China's vulnerability to economic coercion.
