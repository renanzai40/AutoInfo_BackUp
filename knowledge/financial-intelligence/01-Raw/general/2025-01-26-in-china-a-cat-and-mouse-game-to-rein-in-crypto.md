- Middlemen are crucial in enabling the continuation of crypto trade.
- Court cases have uncovered the mechanisms of these illicit activities.
- The situation represents a cat-and-mouse game with authorities trying to control the market.
- Cryptocurrency trade remains booming despite legal restrictions.
