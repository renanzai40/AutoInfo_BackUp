## Original Content
Canadian trade negotiators left the U.S. last week after failing to clinch a trade deal that would stop President Donald Trump's new tariffs from taking effect.

## Summary
Canada has announced retaliatory tariffs on approximately $20 billion of U.S. goods following failed trade negotiations. This action is in response to President Donald Trump's new tariffs that are now taking effect. The move escalates trade tensions between the two countries.

## Key Points
- Canada imposes retaliatory tariffs worth about $20 billion on U.S. goods.
- Trade negotiations between Canada and the U.S. have failed to reach a deal.
- President Donald Trump's tariffs are the primary cause of the retaliation.
- This marks a significant escalation in the trade dispute.
