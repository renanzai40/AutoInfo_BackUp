## Original Content
Trump last Sunday instructed the Pentagon to cut military drills because they were "costly" and send "inappropriate and hostile" signals to North Korea.

## Summary
The article discusses how Trump is redefining the U.S.-South Korea relationship from a military alliance to an economic partnership. Specifically, he instructed the Pentagon to cut costly military drills to reduce tensions with North Korea.

## Key Points
- Trump is shifting the U.S.-South Korea ties from military focus to economic partnership.
- He ordered the Pentagon to reduce military drills due to high costs.
- The reduction aims to de-escalate tensions by avoiding hostile signals to North Korea.
- This move indicates a broader redefinition of strategic alliances.
