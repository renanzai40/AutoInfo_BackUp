## Summary
The Swiss franc and Japanese yen strengthened against the U.S. dollar as investors sought safe havens following the release of a new AI model by Chinese start-up DeepSeek, which negatively impacted U.S. tech stocks.

## Key Points
- Safe-haven currencies like the Swiss franc and Japanese yen rose against the dollar.
- Investors turned to safe assets due to market uncertainty triggered by DeepSeek's AI model.
- DeepSeek, a Chinese AI start-up, launched a new model affecting U.S. tech stocks.
- The AI model's announcement led to a decline in U.S. tech stock values.
- This event highlights the influence of technology news on financial markets.
