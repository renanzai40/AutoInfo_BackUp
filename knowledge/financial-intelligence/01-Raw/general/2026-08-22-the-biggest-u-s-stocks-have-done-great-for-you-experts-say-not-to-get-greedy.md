## Summary
The article highlights that low-cost S&P 500 index funds are commonly used for wealth growth but advises investors to diversify their portfolios to reduce volatility. Experts caution against being overly greedy with high-performing stocks and stress the importance of asset diversification for risk management.

## Key Points
- S&P 500 index funds are foundational in many investment portfolios.
- These funds are low-cost and effective for growing wealth.
- Investors should add assets to diversify and lower volatility.
- Experts recommend not getting greedy with large U.S. stocks.
- Diversification is key to managing investment risk.
