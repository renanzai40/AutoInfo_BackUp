## Summary
Callaway Golf apologized for an advertisement that showed a male golfer shoving a female golfer to the ground, exposing a significant blind spot in the golf industry. This controversy arises amid women and girls driving nearly 60% of net golfer growth since 2019, underscoring the need for better gender sensitivity in marketing.

## Key Points
- Callaway Golf faced backlash for an ad depicting a male golfer pushing a female golfer to the ground.
- The company issued a public apology for the ad's inappropriate content.
- The incident highlights a major industry oversight in addressing gender representation and sensitivity.
- Women and girls contributed to nearly 60% of net golfer growth from 2019 onward, emphasizing their growing role in the sport.
