## Summary
Cash buyers are declining as a share of the housing market due to increased supply and weaker prices. These conditions are giving all buyers more purchasing power, shifting the dynamics away from cash dominance in home sales.

## Key Points
- Cash buyers are diminishing in the housing market
- Increased housing supply is driving the change
- Weaker prices are enhancing buyer purchasing power
- Market conditions favor all types of buyers equally
