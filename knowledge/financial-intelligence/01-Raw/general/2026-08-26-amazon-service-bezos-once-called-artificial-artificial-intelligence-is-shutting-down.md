## Summary
Amazon's Mechanical Turk service, described by Jeff Bezos as 'artificial artificial intelligence', is shutting down. Launched in 2005, it was designed to outsource tasks that are easy for humans but too challenging for computers.

## Key Points
- Mechanical Turk is being discontinued by Amazon.
- The service was launched in 2005 to handle tasks simple for humans but difficult for computers.
- Jeff Bezos referred to it as 'artificial artificial intelligence'.
- The shutdown indicates a shift in Amazon's approach to crowdsourcing services.
