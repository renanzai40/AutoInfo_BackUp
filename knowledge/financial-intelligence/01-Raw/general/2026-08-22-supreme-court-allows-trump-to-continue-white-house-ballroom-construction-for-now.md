President Donald Trump had the White House's East Wing demolished in 2025 to make way for his controversial planned ballroom.

## Summary
The Supreme Court has temporarily allowed President Donald Trump to proceed with constructing a ballroom at the White House. Trump demolished the East Wing in 2025 for this controversial project, and the decision permits continuation for now.

## Key Points
- Supreme Court decision temporarily allows White House ballroom construction.
- President Trump had the East Wing demolished in 2025 for the project.
- The construction project is controversial.
- The ruling is described as 'for now', indicating it may not be permanent.
