## Original Content
An OpenAI spokesperson said the company "recently reorganized our infrastructure organization to support the scale and pace of our work."

## Summary
Chris Malone, the data center chief at OpenAI, has departed from the company, marking the latest in a string of executive exits. OpenAI indicated that the departure follows a recent reorganization of their infrastructure organization to better support the scale and pace of their work.

## Key Points
- Chris Malone has left his position as data center chief at OpenAI.
- This exit is part of a broader pattern of executive departures at OpenAI.
- OpenAI recently reorganized its infrastructure organization.
- The reorganization is intended to accommodate the company's growing operational scale and pace.
