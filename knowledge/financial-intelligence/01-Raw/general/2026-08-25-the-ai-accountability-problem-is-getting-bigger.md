## Original Content
Companies and regulators are struggling to determine who is responsible when things go wrong.

## Summary
Companies and regulators are finding it increasingly difficult to assign responsibility when artificial intelligence systems fail. The issue highlights a growing gap in governance and accountability frameworks for AI technologies.

## Key Points
- Accountability for AI failures is a growing challenge for both companies and regulators
- Existing legal and regulatory frameworks are struggling to keep pace with AI development
- Clear responsibility assignment is lacking when AI causes harm or errors
- The problem intensifies as AI becomes more integrated into critical systems
