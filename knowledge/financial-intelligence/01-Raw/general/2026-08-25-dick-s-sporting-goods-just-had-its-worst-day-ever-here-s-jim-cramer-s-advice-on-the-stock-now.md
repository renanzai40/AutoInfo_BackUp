## Summary
Dick's Sporting Goods saw its stock price plummet by a record 30%, marking the worst day in the company's history. CNBC's Jim Cramer advised investors to remain confident and not sell off their shares despite the significant drop.

## Key Points
- Dick's Sporting Goods experienced a record 30% decline in its stock price.
- This decline represented the worst single-day performance for the company's stock ever.
- Jim Cramer, a prominent financial commentator, recommended that investors hold onto the stock rather than giving up.
- The advice was shared on the CNBC financial news network.
