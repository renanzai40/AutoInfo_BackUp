## Summary
Waymo, the leading robotaxi company in the U.S., has announced plans to launch driverless ride services in Germany in 2027, marking its expansion into the third international market after the U.K. and Japan.

## Key Points
- Waymo is the biggest robotaxi operator in the United States.
- Waymo has expansion plans to enter the U.K., Japan, and Germany.
- The driverless ride launch in Germany is scheduled for 2027.
- This move represents Waymo's entry into its third market outside the U.S.
