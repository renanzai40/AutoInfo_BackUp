## Original Content
Nike, Starbucks and GM have lost ground in China as domestic rivals, geopolitics and changing consumer preferences reshape the market.

## Summary
Major American brands such as Nike, Starbucks, and GM are losing ground in China. This decline is driven by competition from domestic rivals, geopolitical tensions, and evolving consumer preferences. These factors are collectively reshaping the Chinese market landscape.

## Key Points
- Nike, Starbucks, and GM are experiencing reduced market share in China.
- Domestic Chinese competitors are gaining strength and challenging American brands.
- Geopolitical issues are influencing business operations in China.
- Consumer preferences in China are shifting, affecting demand for foreign brands.
- The overall Chinese market is undergoing significant structural changes.
