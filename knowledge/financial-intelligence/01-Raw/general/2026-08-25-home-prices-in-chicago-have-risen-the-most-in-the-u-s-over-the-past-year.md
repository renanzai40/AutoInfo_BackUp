Nationally, home prices are rising at a slow pace as housing inventory remains relatively low. But some markets, particularly in the Midwest, are seeing big gains.

## Summary
Home prices in Chicago have risen the most in the U.S. over the past year, contrasting with the national trend of slow growth due to low housing inventory. Midwest markets, particularly Chicago, are seeing significant gains.
