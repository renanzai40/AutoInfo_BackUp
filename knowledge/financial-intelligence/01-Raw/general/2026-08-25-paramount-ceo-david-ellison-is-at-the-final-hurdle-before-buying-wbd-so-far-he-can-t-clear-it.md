An antitrust challenge by a group of state attorneys general has left Paramount CEO David Ellison hunting for avenues to get his deal for WBD done.

## Summary
Paramount CEO David Ellison is facing an antitrust challenge from a group of state attorneys general in his attempt to purchase WBD. This legal hurdle is preventing the deal from being finalized, and Ellison is currently seeking ways to overcome it.

## Key Points
- David Ellison serves as the CEO of Paramount.
- He is in the process of acquiring WBD.
- A group of state attorneys general has raised an antitrust challenge against the deal.
- This challenge represents the final obstacle before the acquisition can be completed.
- Ellison has not yet succeeded in finding a solution to clear the hurdle.
