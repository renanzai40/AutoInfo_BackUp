## Summary
SpaceX plans to build a $100 billion spaceport called Starbase in Vermilion Parish, Louisiana. The facility is intended to support thousands of launches annually, marking a significant expansion in space infrastructure.

## Key Points
- SpaceX is planning to establish a new spaceport named Starbase in Louisiana.
- The spaceport will be located in Vermilion Parish.
- The project is valued at $100 billion.
- The facility aims to support thousands of launches per year.
