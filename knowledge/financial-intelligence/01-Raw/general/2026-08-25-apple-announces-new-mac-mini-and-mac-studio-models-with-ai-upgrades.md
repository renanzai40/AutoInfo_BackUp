## Summary
Apple has announced new Mac Mini and Mac Studio models with AI upgrades. The announcement highlights new chips and frameworks that allow developers to run and fine-tune large AI models locally on their Mac. This advancement aims to boost on-device AI development capabilities.

## Key Points
- Apple introduced new Mac Mini and Mac Studio models with AI-focused enhancements.
- The models feature chips designed to support AI workloads.
- Developers can run and fine-tune large AI models locally on Mac, reducing cloud dependency.
- This development makes AI tools more accessible to Mac users.
