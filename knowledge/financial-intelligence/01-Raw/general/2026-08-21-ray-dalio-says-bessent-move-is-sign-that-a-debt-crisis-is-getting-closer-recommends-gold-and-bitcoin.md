Billionaire investor Ray Dalio said the the debt buyback announcement this week fits into a larger pattern that could signal a forthcoming debt crisis.

## Summary
Ray Dalio stated that a recent debt buyback announcement indicates a potential debt crisis is approaching, based on a larger pattern in debt markets. He recommends investing in gold and bitcoin as a strategy to prepare for this event.

## Key Points
- Ray Dalio interprets the debt buyback announcement as a warning sign of a forthcoming debt crisis.
- This is part of a broader pattern in debt markets that could lead to a crisis.
- He advises turning to gold and bitcoin for investment to mitigate risks.
