## Summary
Apple has launched new Mac Studio models powered by the M5 Max and the newly introduced M5 Ultra chip, which Apple calls its most powerful chip ever. This move reunifies the Mac Studio lineup under a single chip generation after over a year of a confusing split between M4 Max and M3 Ultra offerings.

## Key Points
- Apple announced new Mac Studio models featuring the M5 Max and the new M5 Ultra chip
- Apple markets the M5 Ultra as its most powerful chip ever
- The new lineup reunifies the Mac Studio under one chip generation after a confusing split between M4 Max and M3 Ultra
- The previous M3 Ultra was actually more powerful than the M4 Max it was paired with
