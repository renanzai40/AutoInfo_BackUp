## Summary
This guest column argues that contrary to widespread negative narratives, the music industry is actually in good shape. While acknowledging that today's industry has its share of problems, the author contends it is a healthier business than ever before. A key theme is that the industry has become less centralized, distributing power more broadly across the ecosystem.

## Key Points
- The music industry is healthier than commonly perceived, despite persistent negative narratives about its condition.
- The industry does face real problems, which the author acknowledges rather than dismisses.
- A major positive development is that the business has become less centralized than in previous eras.
- The column takes an optimistic, contrarian stance aimed at countering prevailing pessimism about the industry's state.
