## Summary
Janus Films and Criterion have acquired North American rights to 'Cameron Winter at Carnegie Hall,' a concert film shot and directed by Paul Thomas Anderson. The film captures a sold-out solo performance by Geese frontman Cameron Winter at Carnegie Hall in December 2025.

## Key Points
- Janus Films and Criterion acquired North American distribution rights to the concert film
- The film was shot and directed by Paul Thomas Anderson on 35mm and 16mm film
- It features a sold-out solo performance by Cameron Winter, frontman of the band Geese
- The concert took place at Carnegie Hall in December 2025
