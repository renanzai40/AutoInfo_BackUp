## Original Content
Wyze announced a new pan-and-tilt security camera today designed for indoor use. The entire Indoor Cam Pan can rotate 360 degrees, while the camera and lens on its front can tilt up and down 103 degrees. When you want privacy, the lens can tilt down until it completely disappears inside the Indoor Cam Pan's body [&#8230;]

## Summary
Wyze has announced a new $40 Indoor Cam Pan security camera featuring a retractable lens designed for privacy. The camera offers 360-degree pan rotation and 103-degree tilt range, with the lens able to fully retract inside the body.

## Key Points
- Wyze Indoor Cam Pan is priced at $40 and designed for indoor use
- The camera features 360-degree pan and 103-degree tilt capabilities
- The lens can tilt down and retract completely inside the camera body for privacy
- The retractable lens design addresses privacy concerns when monitoring is not desired
