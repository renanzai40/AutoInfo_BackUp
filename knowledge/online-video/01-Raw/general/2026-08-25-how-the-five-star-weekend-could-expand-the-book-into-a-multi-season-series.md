## Original Content
Writer and executive producer Bekah Brunstetter explains key changes and how she tried to visualize the perfect image vs. messier emotional reality theme from the Elin Hilderbrand book in the Jennifer Garner-starring Peacock series, which was recently renewed for a second season.

## Summary
Bekah Brunstetter, writer and executive producer of Peacock's 'The Five Star Weekend', discusses adapting Elin Hilderbrand's book, emphasizing the contrast between idealized images and emotional reality. The series, starring Jennifer Garner, has been renewed for a second season, indicating potential for a multi-season expansion.

## Key Points
- Bekah Brunstetter adapted Elin Hilderbrand's book 'The Five Star Weekend' for Peacock, starring Jennifer Garner.
- The adaptation focuses on contrasting perfect images with messy emotional realities.
- The series has been renewed for a second season, suggesting potential for a multi-season series.
