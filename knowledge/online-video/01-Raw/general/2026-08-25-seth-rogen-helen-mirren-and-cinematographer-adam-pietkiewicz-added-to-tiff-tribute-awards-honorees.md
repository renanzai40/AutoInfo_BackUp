## Summary
Seth Rogen, Helen Mirren, and cinematographer Adam Pietkiewicz have been named as honorees for the Toronto International Film Festival Tribute Awards. Each will receive a distinct award recognizing their career achievements and contributions to film.

## Key Points
- Adam Pietkiewicz, cinematographer of 'Angh,' will receive the TIFF Variety Artisan Award
- Helen Mirren will receive the TIFF Share Her Journey Groundbreaker Award, presented by Joanna Griffiths
- Seth Rogen will receive the TIFF Norman Jewison Career Achievement Award
- The announcements were made in connection with the upcoming Toronto International Film Festival
