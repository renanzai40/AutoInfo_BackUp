## Original Content
Neon, which will release Ryusuke Hamaguchi's French-language film in the U.S., will push Efira, who plays the director of a Paris nursing home in the film, for best actress, and Okamoto, who plays a terminally-ill Japanese playwright, for best supporting actress.

## Summary
Neon will release 'All of a Sudden' in the U.S., campaigning for Virginie Efira (Best Actress) and Tao Okamoto (Best Supporting Actress) in awards. The film, directed by Ryusuke Hamaguchi, features Efira as a Paris nursing home director and Okamoto as a terminally-ill Japanese playwright.

## Key Points
- Neon is set to release 'All of a Sudden' in the U.S.
- Virginie Efira to be campaigned for Best Actress for her role as a Paris nursing home director.
- Tao Okamoto to be campaigned for Best Supporting Actress for her role as a terminally-ill Japanese playwright.
