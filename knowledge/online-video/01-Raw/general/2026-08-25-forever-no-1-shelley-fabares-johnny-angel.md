## Summary
Shelley Fabares' song 'Johnny Angel' reached No. 1 on the Hot 100, marking the first time two female singers (following Connie Francis) had consecutive No. 1 hits. This achievement highlighted a milestone in the music industry's recognition of female artists. It signified a shift towards greater female representation at the top of the charts.
