## Summary
The EU has abandoned plans to seize Russian assets to fund support for Ukraine, as reported by EurActiv. The decision is primarily due to opposition from Belgium, which has blocked any path to revive the initiative. This marks a significant setback in utilizing frozen assets for Ukrainian aid.

## Key Points
- The EU's plan to seize Russian assets for Ukraine is no longer viable.
- Belgium's opposition is the main barrier preventing the revival of the plan.
- EU officials acknowledge there is no feasible path forward for the asset seizure proposal.
