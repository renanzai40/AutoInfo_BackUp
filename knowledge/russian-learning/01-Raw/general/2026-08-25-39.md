## Original Content
Дежурные средства ПВО с 8:00 до 20:00 (по Москве) перехватили и уничтожили 39 украинских беспилотников самолетного типа, сообщило во вторник министерство обороны РФ.

## Summary
The Russian Ministry of Defense announced that air defense systems intercepted and destroyed 39 Ukrainian drones between 8:00 and 20:00 Moscow time on Tuesday. The drones were of aircraft type and were engaged over various regions.

## Key Points
- 39 Ukrainian drones were shot down by Russian air defense systems.
- The interception occurred on Tuesday between 8:00 and 20:00 Moscow time.
- The Ministry of Defense of the RF reported the event.
- The drones were described as aircraft-type (самолетного типа).
