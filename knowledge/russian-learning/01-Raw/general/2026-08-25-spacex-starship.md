## Summary
SpaceX has announced plans to begin construction of a new spaceport in Louisiana for its Starship spacecraft in 2027. The project aims to support future missions and expand the company's operational capabilities in space exploration.

## Key Points
- SpaceX is building a new spaceport in Louisiana.
- The spaceport is specifically designed for the Starship spacecraft.
- Construction is scheduled to start in 2027.
- This development is part of SpaceX's expansion in space technology.
