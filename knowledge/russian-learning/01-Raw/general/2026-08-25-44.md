## Original Content
Производство первичного алюминия в странах Персидского залива в июле составило 293 тыс. тонн, что на 44% меньше объема за тот же месяц 2025 года, согласно предварительным расчетам Международного института алюминия (IAI).

## Summary
The article reports that primary aluminum production in the Persian Gulf countries fell by 44% year-over-year in July, reaching 293,000 tons. This data is based on preliminary estimates from the International Aluminum Institute.

## Key Points
- Aluminum production in Persian Gulf countries decreased by 44% in July compared to the same month in 2025.
- The production volume was 293,000 tons.
- Information is preliminary from the International Aluminum Institute.
- The decline indicates a significant reduction in output.
