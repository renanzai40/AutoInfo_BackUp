## Original Content
В ходе миссии, длившейся 6 часов 30 минут, Анил Менон и Софи Адено установили новую антенну для связи орбитальной станции с Центром управления полетами NASA в Хьюстоне

## Summary
NASA and ESA astronauts Anil Menon and Sophie Adeno completed a 6.5-hour spacewalk to install a new communication antenna on the orbital station. This antenna improves communication with NASA's Mission Control Center in Houston.

## Key Points
- Two astronauts from NASA and ESA performed a spacewalk.
- The mission lasted 6 hours and 30 minutes.
- A new antenna was installed for enhanced communication.
- The antenna connects the orbital station to NASA's Mission Control Center in Houston.
