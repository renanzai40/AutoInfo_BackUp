## Original Content
Топливо в среду поступит в свободную продажу и по QR-кодам в Севастополе на АЗС одной сети, сообщил губернатор города Михаил Развожаев."Продажа четырех видов топлива (Аи-95, Аи-95 NP, Аи-92 и ДТ) на АЗС "ТЭС" будет осуществляться по QR-кодам.

## Summary
Fuel in Sevastopol will be sold both freely and via QR codes at gas stations of the 'TES' network, as announced by Governor Mikhail Razvozhaev. The sale will include four fuel types: Ai-95, Ai-95 NP, Ai-92, and Diesel, starting on Wednesday.

## Key Points
- Fuel will be available for purchase using QR codes and through free sale in Sevastopol
- The sale is implemented at gas stations belonging to the 'TES' network
- Four specific fuel types are involved: Ai-95, Ai-95 NP, Ai-92, and Diesel
- Governor Mikhail Razvozhaev officially announced this change in fuel distribution methods
