## Original Content
Власти Канады вводят с 8 сентября пошлины в размере 15%, 25% и 50% в отношении ряда товаров из США, говорится в заявлении министерства финансов Канады.

## Summary
Canada has announced retaliatory tariffs against the United States, effective from September 8. The tariffs will range from 15% to 50% on various goods, as stated by the Canadian Ministry of Finance.

## Key Points
- Canada is imposing retaliatory tariffs on US goods.
- Tariff rates are set at 15%, 25%, and 50%.
- The tariffs take effect on September 8.
- The announcement was made by the Canadian Ministry of Finance.
