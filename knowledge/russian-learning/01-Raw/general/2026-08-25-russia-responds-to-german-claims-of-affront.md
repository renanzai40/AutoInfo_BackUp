## Summary
Russian diplomat Maria Zakharova ridiculed Germany's claims of being insulted by military action during the German foreign minister's visit to Kiev. The incident highlights diplomatic tensions between Russia and Germany in the context of the Ukraine conflict. Zakharova's comments were reported by the Russian state media outlet RT.com.

## Key Points
- Maria Zakharova, a Russian diplomat, mocked Germany's claims of an 'affront' regarding military action.
- The incident occurred during the German foreign minister's visit to Kiev, Ukraine.
- This response reflects ongoing diplomatic tensions between Russia and Germany.
- The article was published by RT.com, a Russian state-funded media organization.
- Germany characterized the event as an 'affront', which Russia dismissed as exaggerated.
