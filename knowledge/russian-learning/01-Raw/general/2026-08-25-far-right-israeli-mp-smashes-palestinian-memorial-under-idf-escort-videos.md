## Summary
Far-right Israeli MP Zvi Sukkot demolished a Palestinian memorial in the West Bank under IDF escort, an act that has drawn condemnation. The incident reflects ongoing regional tensions and has sparked international criticism.

## Key Points
- Zvi Sukkot, an Israeli MP, physically destroyed a Palestinian memorial.
- The operation was conducted under escort by the Israel Defense Forces (IDF).
- The event occurred in the West Bank, a contested area in the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
- The action has elicited widespread condemnation from various groups and individuals.
