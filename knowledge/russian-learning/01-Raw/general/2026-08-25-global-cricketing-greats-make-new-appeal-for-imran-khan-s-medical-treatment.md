## Summary
Twenty-two former cricket players have appealed to Pakistan's Prime Minister to provide medical treatment to Imran Khan, as ordered by the Supreme Court. This highlights ongoing efforts to ensure his access to necessary healthcare amid political and legal dimensions.

## Key Points
- A group of 22 former cricket stars has written to Pakistan's Prime Minister.
- They are urging immediate medical treatment for Imran Khan.
- The treatment has been mandated by the Supreme Court.
- This appeal underscores continued pressure for his health care rights.
