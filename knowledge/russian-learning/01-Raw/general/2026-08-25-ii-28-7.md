## Original Content
Совокупная чистая прибыль банков США во втором квартале 2026 года выросла на 28,7% относительно аналогичного периода прошлого года и составила $90,1 млрд, говорится в сообщении Федеральной корпорации по страхованию депозитов (FDIC).

## Summary
The article states that the total net profit of US banks in Q2 2026 increased by 28.7% year-over-year to $90.1 billion. This data is reported by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC).

## Key Points
- US banks experienced a 28.7% year-over-year increase in net profit in Q2 2026
- The total net profit for the quarter amounted to $90.1 billion
- The information is sourced from the FDIC, a federal agency that insures bank deposits
- This growth indicates significant improvement in the banking sector's profitability
