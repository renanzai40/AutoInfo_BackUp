## Summary
The new leader of the Democrats has requested Lee not to appoint Linton to lead the Korean Red Cross. This intervention highlights potential political influence over the leadership of a humanitarian organization. The situation raises concerns about the independence of such entities from political pressures.

## Key Points
- Democrats' new leader is actively involved in the Korean Red Cross leadership appointment process.
- The leader specifically requests Lee to refrain from appointing Linton to the position.
- The Korean Red Cross, a prominent humanitarian organization, is at the center of this political matter.
- This incident underscores the broader issue of political interference in non-political institutions.
