## Summary
From October, bus fares for intercity and express services in South Korea will increase by 9% due to rising fuel and labor costs. This adjustment is expected to impact transportation expenses for travelers.

## Key Points
- Bus fares for intercity and express buses will increase by 9%
- The fare increase is effective from October
- The reasons cited are rising fuel costs and labor expenses
- This may affect travel costs and consumer behavior in the transportation sector
