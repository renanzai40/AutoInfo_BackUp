## Summary
The article discusses how the Homeplus labor union is appealing to suppliers to agree to bond installment repayments, which is critical for the livelihoods of approximately 300,000 people. This situation highlights financial challenges at Homeplus that affect both workers and suppliers, emphasizing the need for cooperation to prevent widespread economic hardship.

## Key Points
- Homeplus union is making an urgent appeal to suppliers for consent to bond installment repayments.
- The survival rights of around 300,000 individuals are at stake due to financial distress.
- Bond installment repayment is a key procedure being negotiated to stabilize the company and its stakeholders.
- The appeal underscores the interdependence between the retail company, its suppliers, and the workforce.
