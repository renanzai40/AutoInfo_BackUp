## Summary
The Gangneung Workation Center is set to open on the 28th, providing a seaside work space for 10,000 won per day. This facility aims to support workation trends by combining work and vacation in a coastal setting.

## Key Points
- Opening of a new workation center in Gangneung on the 28th
- Affordable daily rate of 10,000 won for access
- Designed to offer a work-life balance experience near the sea
- Promotes the concept of 'workation' for remote workers
