## Summary
KT SAT has been controlling geostationary satellites at an altitude of 36,000 km for 32 years. They are now embarking on a new challenge by expanding into low Earth orbit operations.

## Key Points
- KT SAT has 32 years of experience in high-altitude satellite control.
- The company is transitioning from traditional geostationary orbits to low orbit operations.
- This move represents a significant technological and operational challenge for satellite management.
