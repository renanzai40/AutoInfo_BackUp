## Summary
The article discusses KBS executive Park Jang-beon's decision to completely remove the 'broadcast autonomy clause' that was introduced in 2003. This clause was intended to ensure independence in broadcast content, and its removal may impact editorial policies and autonomy at KBS. The action is highlighted as a significant change in the broadcasting station's operations.

## Key Points
- KBS executive Park Jang-beon erased the broadcast autonomy clause.
- The clause was originally introduced in 2003 to promote broadcast autonomy.
- This removal represents a major shift in KBS's editorial or operational framework.
