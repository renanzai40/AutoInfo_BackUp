## Summary
Chinese literary magazines have collectively rejected artificial intelligence, stating that literature is not a commercial product. This declaration aims to preserve human creativity and artistic value in literary works. The move underscores growing concerns about AI's impact on non-medical fields.

## Key Points
- 24 Chinese literary magazines issued a declaration rejecting artificial intelligence in literature.
- The statement 'Literature is not a product' emphasizes the non-commercial, artistic nature of literature.
- This collective action seeks to maintain human touch and originality in literary creation.
- It reflects broader societal concerns about AI's role in arts and culture.
- The rejection is a response to increasing AI integration across industries.
