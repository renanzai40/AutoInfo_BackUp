## Summary
A recent survey indicates that 55% of Koreans hold unfavorable views towards the United States, which is higher than the levels recorded during the 2002 Hyosun and Misun incident. This suggests a growing negative sentiment among Koreans toward the U.S. based on historical comparisons.

## Key Points
- 55% of Koreans dislike the United States according to current data.
- This percentage surpasses levels observed during the Hyosun and Misun incident, a past event.
- The incident refers to a historical case involving Korean schoolgirls and U.S. military personnel, indicating ongoing geopolitical tensions.
