## Summary
Tax authorities have initiated a recovery of 4.1 billion won from an importer who evaded taxes by declaring a lower import price for 180,000 bottles of wine, including the high-end brand 'Romanée-Conti'. The evasion involved underreporting the import value to reduce tax liability, highlighting significant tax fraud in the luxury goods sector.

## Key Points
- Underreporting of import prices for wine led to tax evasion.
- The case involves 180,000 bottles of wine, including the prestigious 'Romanée-Conti' brand.
- A recovery of 4.1 billion won is being pursued, indicating substantial financial impact.
- Tax authorities are taking legal action to address the fraud.
