## Summary
A lawsuit has been filed in Austria challenging gender discrimination in public toilet fees, where women are charged while men use them for free. This case highlights issues of gender equality and discrimination in public services.

## Key Points
- Gender-based pricing discrimination in Austrian public toilets, with women paying fees and men not.
- Lawsuit filed to address and challenge this discriminatory practice.
- Broader implications for gender equality and anti-discrimination laws in public facilities.
