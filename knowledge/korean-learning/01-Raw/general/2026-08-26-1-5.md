## Summary
The article reports that annual tobacco consumption tax revenue, amounting to 1.5 trillion won, is being redirected from the local education tax portion to fund public housing supply.

## Key Points
- Tobacco consumption tax generates 1.5 trillion won in revenue each year.
- A portion of this tax is allocated as local education tax.
- The revenue is now being used for public housing supply instead of its original educational purpose.
- This represents a significant shift in fiscal policy allocation.
- The goal is to address public housing demands using these funds.
