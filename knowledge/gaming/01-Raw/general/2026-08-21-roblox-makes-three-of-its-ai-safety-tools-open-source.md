## Summary
Roblox is open-sourcing three of its AI safety models through the Robust Open Online Safety Tools (ROOST) Model Community, making these tools available for broader use and development. This move aims to enhance online safety by sharing its safety-focused AI technologies. The open-sourcing is part of Roblox's efforts to improve safety across its platform.
