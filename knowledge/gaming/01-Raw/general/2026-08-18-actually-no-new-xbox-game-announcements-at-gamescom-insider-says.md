## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Insider <a href="https://x.com/NateTheHate2/status/2088674549428556273">NateTheHate 2</a> has amended his original statement about a potential new Xbox game announcement for Gamescom, now saying it may not happen.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"Based on recent conversations, it does not seem likely that we'll get any new Xbox game announcements at Gamescom," he wrote.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-rich is-provider-x wp-block-embed-x"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://twitter.com/NateTheHate2/status/2091935028695417221
</div></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Originally, NateTheHate2 said, "I've heard of a neat Xbox announcement that should be at Gamescom." He said it will be a "new game in a series" as opposed to a new IP. And no, it's not a new Banjo-Kazooie game."</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At the time, he said it was <a href="https://x.com/NateTheHate2/status/2089457810501251429">from a franchise that Microsoft owns</a>, but it's <a href="https://x.com/NateTheHate2/status/2089460969919426888">not being made by a studio that Microsoft owns</a>. That leaves open <em>a lot </em>of possibilities, so your guess is as good as anyone's right now as to what it may be and which studio could be behind it.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1853781" height="576" src="https://www.gamespot.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/XboxGamescom.jpg?w=636" style="cursor: zoom-in;" width="1024" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Microsoft has an announcement on tap for Gamescom, it seems.</figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"> Xbox is not holding its own dedicated showcase at Gamescom, but it's possible the announcement could come during Geoff Keighley's Opening Night Live on August 25.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The <a href="https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/07/28/xbox-gamescom-2026/">Xbox booth at Gamescom</a> will feature 25 upcoming games, including Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, Fable, Minecraft Dungeons II, Gears of War: E-Day, Metro 2039, Stranger Than Heaven, and Alien: Isolation 2.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There will also be live broadcasts from the Xbox booth August 26 and August 27, featuring interviews, guests, gameplay reveals, trailers, "and more." Perhaps one of the interview slots will be about this upcoming mystery game.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">2026 is the 25th anniversary of Xbox, and the company <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-unveils-new-25th-anniversary-edition-xbox-series-x-based-on-original-xbox-and-its-gonna-be-pricey/">has a lot planned</a>.</p>

## Summary
Insider NateTheHate2 has walked back his earlier claim that a new Xbox game announcement was expected at Gamescom, now saying it seems unlikely based on recent conversations. Xbox will not hold a dedicated showcase at Gamescom but will feature 25 upcoming games at its booth, with a possible announcement during Opening Night Live on August 25.

- Xbox is not holding its own dedicated showcase at Gamescom; any announcement might occur during Geoff Keighley's Opening Night Live on August 25
