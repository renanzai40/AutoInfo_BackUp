## Summary
Double Fine, the studio behind Psychonauts, has reclaimed the publishing rights to their games after leaving Xbox, following a similar move by Compulsion Games. CEO Tim Schafer confirmed the studio now owns their catalogue outright.

## Key Points
- Double Fine now owns the publishing rights to their own game catalogue after being a former Xbox-owned studio
- This follows a similar move by Compulsion Games, developers of South of Midnight
- Tim Schafer confirmed the studio now controls their own intellectual property
- The move comes amid the ongoing BDS boycott against Microsoft and Xbox
