## Summary
How to Fish is Steam's latest co-op party game where players control worm-like creatures fishing on cursed islands with golden sniper rifles. The game has received an update adding private lobbies, eight-player support, and anti-griefer measures.

## Key Points
- How to Fish is a co-op party game with a macabre, humorous tone featuring worm people fishing with golden sniper rifles
- The latest update adds private lobby support for more controlled multiplayer sessions
- Eight-player support has been added, expanding the multiplayer capacity
- Anti-griefer measures were included in the update to improve the player experience
- The game is marketed as a hangout/party game rather than a serious fishing simulator
