## Original Content
Discord has countered online reports about Take-Two’s hunt for the identity of the GTA 6 leaker, saying it has yet to be formally served a subpoena, and when it does receive it, it will take a long, hard look at it before responding.

## Summary
Discord states it hasn't received a formal subpoena from Take-Two regarding the GTA 6 leak and will evaluate its validity and scope before responding. The move is part of Take-Two's efforts to identify the leaker. Discord's response indicates a cautious approach to the legal request.

## Key Points
- Discord claims not to have been formally served a subpoena by Take-Two over GTA 6 leak.
- Upon receipt, Discord will evaluate the subpoena's validity and scope.
- Take-Two is actively pursuing the identity of the GTA 6 leaker.
