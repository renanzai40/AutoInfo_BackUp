## Summary
Publisher Focus Entertainment and developer Afterburner Studios, known for Dreamscaper, have announced Elta: Defy All Gods, a sci-fi action platformer coming to PS5, Xbox Series, and PC. The announcement reveals the next project from the studio behind the roguelike action-RPG Dreamscaper.

## Key Points
- Elta: Defy All Gods is a newly announced sci-fi action platformer
- The game will release on PS5, Xbox Series, and PC
- Focus Entertainment is serving as the publisher
- Afterburner Studios, the team behind Dreamscaper, is developing the title
