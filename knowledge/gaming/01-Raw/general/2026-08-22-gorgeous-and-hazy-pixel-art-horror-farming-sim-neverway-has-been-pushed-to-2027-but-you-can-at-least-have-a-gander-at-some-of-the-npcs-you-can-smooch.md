## Summary
Neverway, a horror life sim and RPG developed by Coldblood Inc. by artists behind Celeste and the composer of Fez, has been delayed to 2027. The developer shared the delay news via a Steam blog post, though no exact release date was set.

## Key Points
- Neverway has been pushed back to 2027 from its originally planned release
- The game is a horror life sim and RPG combination
- Developer Coldblood Inc. includes one of the artists behind Celeste and the composer of Fez
- The delay was announced via a Steam blog post
- No exact release date was provided
