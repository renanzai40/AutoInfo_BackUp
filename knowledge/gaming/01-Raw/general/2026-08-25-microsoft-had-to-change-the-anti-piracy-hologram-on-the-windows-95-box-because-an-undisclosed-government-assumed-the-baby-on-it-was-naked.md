## Summary
Microsoft was forced to alter the anti-piracy hologram on its Windows 95 packaging after an undisclosed government agency interpreted the baby image as nude. Microsoft referred to the government only as 'nameless for obvious reasons.'

## Key Points
- Microsoft modified the Windows 95 anti-piracy hologram due to a government's interpretation of the baby imagery
- The specific government agency was not disclosed by Microsoft
- Microsoft described the government as 'nameless for obvious reasons'
- The hologram featured a baby, which some authorities considered inappropriate or nude
