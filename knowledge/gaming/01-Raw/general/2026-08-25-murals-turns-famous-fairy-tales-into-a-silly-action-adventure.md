## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Today, during <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gamescom-opening-night-live-2026-all-the-biggest-announcements-games-and-trailers/">Gamescom Opening Night Live</a>, Disney Illusion Island developer Dlala Studios introduced its next game: Murals, which puts a new spin on some classic childhood stories, and will launch on PC in 2027. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Murals is an isometric action-adventure game set within multiple famous fairy tales, including Hansel and Gretel, The Little Mermaid, and Rapunzel, but not the way you might remember them. Rapunzel's hair, for example, is actively trying to kill her, while "Sleeping Beauty" is suffering from insomnia and hasn't slept in weeks.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D42nkf8EHMk">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D42nkf8EHMk</a></p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Players will control a nameless hero as they fight through these twisted fairy tales with help from Eeries, which the game describes as "mystical, slightly very haunted, doo-dads and whatchamacallits" that enhance the hero's abilities. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Some Eeries enhance the hero's weapon, like Strength In Misery's ability to deal more damage to enemies with status effects, while others affect the hero themselves, like Thorny's passive ability to sometimes damage any attacking enemy that hurts the hero.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Murals, which is developed and published by Dlala, is the studio's first original IP in over a decade. Its most recent projects include Battletoads and 2023's Disney Illusion Island..</p>

## Summary
Dlala Studios announces Murals, an isometric action-adventure game twisting classic fairy tales like Hansel and Gretel and Rapunzel, set for 2027 PC release. Players control a hero aided by mystical 'Eeries' that enhance abilities. The game features a unique spin on childhood stories, with altered characters and scenarios.

## Key Points
- Murals is an isometric action-adventure game set in twisted fairy tales
- Features include Eeries that enhance the hero's abilities
- Scheduled for 2027 release on PC
- Developed and published by Dlala Studios, their first original IP in over a decade
