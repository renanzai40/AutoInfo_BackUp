## Summary
Carrier Command 2 is being released on multiple console platforms including PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and Switch, through a cooperation between Atari's Infogrames, MicroProse, and developer Geometa.

## Key Points
- Carrier Command 2 is coming to PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and Switch.
- Atari's Infogrames label is the publisher.
- MicroProse and Geometa are involved in the release as original publisher and developer, respectively.
