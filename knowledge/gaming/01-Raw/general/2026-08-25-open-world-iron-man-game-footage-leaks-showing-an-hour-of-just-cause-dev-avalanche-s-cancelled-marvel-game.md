## Summary
Footage from a cancelled Iron Man game developed by Avalanche Studios, known for the Just Cause series, has leaked online. Disney had previously hired Avalanche to create an original Marvel superhero game, but the project was cancelled before any public announcement.

## Key Points
- An hour of gameplay footage from a cancelled Iron Man game has leaked
- Avalanche Studios, developer of the Just Cause series, was hired to build the game
- Marvel's owner Disney originally tapped Avalanche Studios for the project
- The game was cancelled before it was ever officially announced
- This marks another attempt at an Iron Man video game that never materialized
