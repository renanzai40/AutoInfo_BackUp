## Summary
NECHROMIA is a high-contrast survival horror game set in a castle made of memories that aren't the player's, offering a unique aesthetic in the PS1 horror throwback genre. It features chromatic aberration as a visual element and comes with a seizure warning. The game diverges from common Silent Hill or Resident Evil styles, similar to Sorry We're Closed.

## Key Points
- NECHROMIA is a high-contrast survival horror game with a unique memory-based setting
- Visually distinct with chromatic aberration, differing from typical PS1 horror aesthetics
- Seizure warning due to visual effects
- Diverges from Silent Hill/Resident Evil styles, akin to Sorry We're Closed
