## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to industry veteran Raphaël Colantonio, who worked on the Dishonored series at Arkane before starting his own studio, GTA games are "the biggest tease" and also "the biggest disappointment" for people who love immersive sim games. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"I don't mean that in an offensive way because of course who am I and who are they? They make games that sell I don't know how many thousands times more than mine and they are so incredible at doing surface simulation," he told <a href="https://www.pressboxpr.co.uk/news/raphael-colantonio-interview-dishonoured-grand-theft-auto-vi-arkane-studios">Press Box PR</a>. "But that’s such a stab in the heart for me when they don’t go more than surface level where there’s no long-term consequences to anything you do."</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">GTA games, instead, offer more of a "fantastic sandbox with incredible execution, incredible cinematography, [and] great gameplay," Colantonio added. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He went on to say Rockstar has a formula and that he "understands why they do it." At the same time, Colantonio said a game like Skyrim is more to his liking. He said in Skyrim, there is "just one instance of the world," so your actions have real, meaningful, and lasting consequences. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"You really own your consequences as a player. Everything is playable and you have to deal with your choices for the rest of the game," he said.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Colantonio was speaking about GTA games so far, and not specifically GTA 6. Rockstar has yet to share specifics on how it might have updated the formula for the new game, or if there might be further reactivity to choices players make.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He theorized that Rockstar's belief with the GTA series is that offering too much in the way of choices having real, lasting consequences confuses the player too much, so the developers take a different approach.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"What Rockstar basically say is, you know what? That part sucks. It’s too hard to do. It confuses players and most players won’t care at all anyway," he said. "Here’s what we will do instead. We put a guy with a question mark on their head. They have a mission for you. When you want to play the mission, we open an instance of the world in which nothing else matters than what's inside this instance. Then, because of that there is a trade off."</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This setup works well for GTA games, Colantonio said, because it affords Rockstar the ability to have incredible set pieces and action sequences. But, again, that's not something he's personally interested in.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"People are going to be thrilled but none of it matters. That's the thing. We don't have to deal with the consequences. The players don't have to deal with the consequences. Therefore, we can push the buttons, crank things up to 11, and I totally get the draw," he said. "I totally understand why people are into it. I understand why they would be into it as developers. I understand why it sells so much but this is not my values, not that my values are not to sell a gazillion units!"</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He went on to say that GTA games allowing players to fail missions and try them again breaks immersion. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"If you fail the mission, you find yourself back out on the street and there’s the same guy with the same question mark over his head who you click on and you’re ready to restart the mission. There’s nothing immersive to me in that," he explained.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Again, Colantonio said he makes these remarks "with the greatest respect" for Rockstar and what they do. "They do things that are amazing," he said, adding that he expects to get flamed for what he's saying about Rockstar and GTA. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"But no, I respect them a lot. It's just that it's not my bike," he said.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Asked what he would think of Rockstar making GTA 7 an immersive sim kind of game, Colantonio joked that Rockstar should do it if they want to sell fewer copies. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"It's highly respectable. It's great in so many ways. It's just not my thing, and I couldn't imagine a world where they would want me in there because I'm going to bring some bizarre stuff. They're going for more direct paraphernalia where instantly you know the gameplay. It doesn't need digestion and there isn’t anything obscure like what we do here, so yeah it's a difference like the difference between Steven Spielberg and David Lynch or whatever," he said. "One is going to sell gazillions and the other one is going to be a little more indirect and obscure and sometimes land a hit."</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Colantonio ended his thoughts by saying he is "really, really looking forward" to GTA 6 because he wants to see "how far they can push the instant simulation, surface simulation, because they do all that so incredibly well, and it's inspiring, and I love that."</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game has seen <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-leaks-a-timeline-of-the-events-so-far/">numerous gameplay leaks</a> this month, and Rockstar and Take-Two are said to be <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/microsoft-and-discord-subpoenaed-in-gta-6-leak-case/">hunting down the people or parties responsible</a>. Whatever happens, Rockstar is sticking with its plans to show the game off, in an official capacity, on August 27 via an<a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-being-paywalled-behind-netflix-is-upsetting-a-lot-of-people-but-its-a-sign-of-the-times/"> extended trailer on Netflix. </a></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As for Colantonio, he now heads up WolfEye Studios, which launched its first game, Weird West, in 2022. WolfEye is now working on a new game that hasn't been announced yet.</p>

## Summary
Raphaël Colantonio, ex-Dishonored dev, calls GTA games a 'stab in the heart' for not going beyond surface-level simulation, lacking long-term consequence, despite acknowledging their excellence in sandbox and cinematography. He prefers games like Skyrim with meaningful player consequences.

## Key Points
- Raphaël Colantonio criticizes GTA series for lacking depth in player consequences
- Colantonio prefers immersive sims (e.g., Skyrim) with lasting consequences for player actions
- Rockstar's approach prioritizes surface simulation for broad appeal and ease of play
- Colantonio respects Rockstar's success but values different design principles
- GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19, with an official trailer on August 27
