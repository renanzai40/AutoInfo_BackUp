## Summary
《骤影·绯月杀》, a Chinese asymmetric competitive game combining identity deduction and fast-paced combat, will have a 7-day public test from August 25 to 31, ahead of its September 25 release. The game offers diverse playstyles for different players, blending social deduction with RPG and combat elements.

## Key Points
- 《骤影·绯月杀》 public test: August 25-31, release: September 25
- Game combines identity deduction with fast-paced combat and RPG elements
- Diverse playstyles cater to different player preferences (e.g., combat, exploration, social deduction)
- Developed by 天命玄鸟游戏工作室 using Unreal Engine 5 with a unique medieval-fantasy-gothic art style
- Received Epic's MegaGrants and investment from 网易雷火, with over 10 million video views
