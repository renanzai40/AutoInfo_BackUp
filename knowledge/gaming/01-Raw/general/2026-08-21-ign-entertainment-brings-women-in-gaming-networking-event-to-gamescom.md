## Summary
IGN Entertainment is hosting a Women in Gaming networking breakfast at Gamescom on August 26th, supported by GamesIndustry.biz, aiming to facilitate connections among women in the gaming industry. The event will take place at the Dorint Hotel am Heumarkt Köln from 8:30-10:30. This initiative highlights the growing importance of diversity and inclusion in the gaming sector.
