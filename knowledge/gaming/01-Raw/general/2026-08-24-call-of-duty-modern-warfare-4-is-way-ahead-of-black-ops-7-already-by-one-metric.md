## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is nearing 400,000 copies sold on Steam already, and that's well ahead of where last year's Black Ops 7 stood at launch on Valve's platform. This is according to <a href="https://x.com/superhys/status/2091934770666295378">Rhys Elliott of Alinea Analytics</a>, who added that pre-sales on Steam alone work out to around $21 million in revenue.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Elliott said Black Ops 7 managed to hit around 200,000 copies pre-sold on Steam by launch. Modern Warfare 4 has doubled that, and with two months still to go before release.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1860257" height="576" src="https://www.gamespot.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/CODMW4_df79f1.jpg?w=636" style="cursor: zoom-in;" width="1024" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Modern Warfare 4 is selling well so far on Steam, it seems.</figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MW4's beta put up big numbers on Steam during its early-access period, reaching a peak of <a href="https://steamdb.info/app/4783780/charts/">more than 115,000 concurrent users</a>. Black Ops 7, meanwhile, peaked at about 100,000 concurrent users on Steam, even after it went live for everyone outside of a beta weekend, <a href="https://x.com/charlieINTEL/status/2090875216477978819">according to ChalieIntel.</a></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MW4 performing better than Black Ops 7 by some metrics already is not a total surprise, as last year's game was a commercial disappointment. In fact, its sales ranking in the US was the <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/black-ops-7-had-call-of-dutys-worst-sales-year-ranking-in-the-us-since-2008/1100-6537567/">lowest for the series since 2008</a>. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The next MW4 beta is coming up this weekend, running August 28-September 1. This will be an open beta, so anyone on any platform can play without needing to preorder. Additionally, this beta will extend to Nintendo Switch 2.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MW4 is expected to have stronger outright sales overall in part on PC because it is not included with PC Game Pass like Black Ops 7 was.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-impact-on-console-sales">Impact on console sales</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Elliott also observed that MW4 could help improve sales of PS5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles. MW4 is the first entry in the series since 2013's Call of Duty: Ghosts to skip PS4 and Xbox One, so the launch of MW4, combined with the current-gen-only GTA 6, will "for sure nudge the last-gen holdouts," Elliott said. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The newest consoles are more expensive than ever today, thanks to a variety of factors, including<a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/why-are-video-game-consoles-so-expensive-these-days-and-games-historically-cheap/"> tariffs, AI, and the capitalistic demands to always make more money</a>. As such, anyone buying a new console to play MW4 or GTA 6 who hasn't kept up with the news is in for a shock at the register or online checkout. For its part, Microsoft is encouraging people to <a href="https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/06/25/xbox-console-price-update/">buy a used console or use a Buy Now, Pay Later service</a> to help fund the cost of an expensive new console. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MW4 launches on October 23, while GTA 6 arrives at long last on November 19.</p>

## Summary
Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is outperforming Black Ops 7 in pre-sales on Steam, nearing 400,000 copies sold (doubling Black Ops 7's 200,000) with $21 million in revenue, and also surpassed it in beta concurrent users (115,000 vs 100,000). MW4's strong performance is expected to boost PC sales (not included in PC Game Pass) and potentially drive console sales for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, as it skips last-gen platforms. The game's open beta is set for August 28-September 1, extending to Nintendo Switch 2, and is scheduled to launch on October 23.

## Key Points
- Modern Warfare 4 has sold nearly 400,000 copies on Steam pre-launch, doubling Black Ops 7's pre-sale figure.
- MW4's beta reached a peak of over 115,000 concurrent Steam users, outperforming Black Ops 7's 100,000.
- MW4's success is expected to drive stronger PC sales since it's not included in PC Game Pass.
- The game may boost PS5 and Xbox Series X|S console sales as it's the first Call of Duty to skip PS4 and Xbox One.
- MW4 launches on October 23, with an open beta (August 28-September 1) also available on Nintendo Switch 2.
