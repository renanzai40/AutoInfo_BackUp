## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Yet another alleged <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-leaks-a-timeline-of-the-events-so-far/">GTA 6 leak</a> has emerged, showing off one of the game's main characters, Jason, visiting a video game store that sells physical discs. This has prompted people to point out the irony of GTA 6 being a digital-exclusive game.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the clip, we see Jason visit a store called Game Horde where he uses binoculars to zoom in on the shelves, showing various games and consoles.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"This is not about actual discs. It's about ownership. Resale rights still matter, even in a digital-only era," reads a watermark on the video.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-rich is-provider-x wp-block-embed-x"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">

</div></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">GTA 6 will be sold at brick-and-mortar retailers, but the game's physical edition only includes a code in the box. The data shows that more and more people, on a percentage basis, are buying games digitally today. But a lot of people still want physical game discs, while many have also expressed concerns about game preservation concerns in a digital-only era. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When the GTA 6 leaks began, the person claiming responsibility listed off various "commandments," including the following:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Thou Shalt Not Sell Digital Preorders</li>



<li>Thou Shalt Not Sell Fake Single-Player DLC</li>



<li>Thou Shalt Preserve Single-Player Content</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For more, check out GameSpot's <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-leaks-a-timeline-of-the-events-so-far/">timeline of the GTA 6 leaks so far</a>.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The gameplay leaks have been extensive, but they <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-leaks-dont-matter-and-arent-as-bad-as-hot-coffee-scandal-ex-rockstar-director-says/">don't matter and people should calm down</a>, a former Rockstar director has said. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rockstar and Take-Two have&nbsp;<a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-leaker-has-not-been-identified-yet-report/">yet to identify the person or people responsible</a>&nbsp;for the leak. Whoever they may be, they appear to have access to the game.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In 2022, GTA 6 suffered its first major leak of gameplay footage. The autistic 18-year-old suspect was apprehended and&nbsp;<a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-67663128" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">ordered to live in a secure hospital for life</a>. In July this year, it was reported that the person, Arion Kurtaj, left the secure hospital and is now in a&nbsp;<a href="https://bsky.app/profile/joetidy.bsky.social/post/3mqmgoyac522b" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">“normal prison”</a>&nbsp;awaiting a new trial.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While the leaks have shown a lot of new footage and revealed new insights about GTA 6, we're now just days away from <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-being-paywalled-behind-netflix-is-upsetting-a-lot-of-people-but-its-a-sign-of-the-times/">Rockstar releasing official footage of the game on Netflix on August 27</a>. </p>
