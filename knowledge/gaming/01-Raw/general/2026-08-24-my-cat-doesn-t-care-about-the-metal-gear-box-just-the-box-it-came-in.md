## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At long last,&nbsp;Metal Gear Solid fans can&nbsp;soon get their hands on the one piece of merchandise that truly matters: themed boxes for their cats. Konami has announced an official line of Metal Gear Solid cat merchandise that'll be available exclusively at CatCon 2026, to mark the release of <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/games/metal-gear-solid-master-collection-vol-2/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2</a>. We swear we're not making this up, but Konami says that it will have a booth--again, at a cat convention--where it will sell the novelty items for two days in October.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to Konami, attendees can purchase cat-scratch boxes modeled after the iconic boxes of Big Boss and Old Snake, as well as plushie pet toys and cat collars inspired by Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker and Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots. The goodies will also be sold through <a href="https://www.oksgear.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">OKS Gear Shop</a> for those two days. There'll even be a meet-and-greet with Kat Curtis and the catfluencer "One Eared Uno," and I am only now learning that this is a thing. The duo will be featured in a special playthrough video of Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-1860116" height="576" src="https://www.gamespot.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MGS-Cat.jpg?w=636" style="cursor: zoom-in;" width="1024" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Exclamation point!</figcaption></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">CatCon 2026 runs October 10-11, and if you can't make it in, please do not think about getting a giant cardboard box to sneak past security. You might fool human sense, but there's a good chance that your plans will be foiled by the actual cats in attendance when they find themselves unable to resist the siren lure of cardboard fortifications.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can stick to digital cardboard boxes in classic Metal Gear Solid games in case you don't want to take that risk, as Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 launches for PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch consoles on August 27. So far, we've found <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/metal-gear-solid-master-collection-vol-2-is-an-exciting-return-to-metal-gears-past/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">it's an exciting return to the past</a> that offers not just the enjoyable Peace Walker and Ghost Babel from the handheld eras of Metal Gear Solid, but also frees <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/games/metal-gear-solid-4-guns-of-the-patriots/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots</a> from PS3 purgatory.</p>

## Summary
Konami is releasing Metal Gear Solid-themed cat merchandise, including cat boxes, plushies, and collars, exclusively at CatCon 2026 to coincide with the release of Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2. The items will also be available online for two days. A meet-and-greet with Kat Curtis and cat influencer One Eared Uno is planned.
