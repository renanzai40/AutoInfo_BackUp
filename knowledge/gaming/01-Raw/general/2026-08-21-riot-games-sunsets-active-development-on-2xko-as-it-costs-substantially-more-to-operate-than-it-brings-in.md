## Summary
Riot Games is ending active development of its free-to-play fighting game 2XKO in December due to operational costs exceeding revenue. The game will no longer receive updates after December. This decision reflects the game's inability to generate sufficient income to sustain development.

## Key Points
- Riot Games ceases active development of 2XKO in December
- Reason: Operational costs far exceed revenue
- Game will not receive further updates post-December
