## Summary
The massive Tamriel Rebuilt Morrowind mod has released its new expansion called Poison Song, adding content to the Elder Scrolls III game. The expansion allows players to join Great House Indoril and the Temple Ordinators while exploring a new region with lush gardens and fields.

## Key Points
- Poison Song is the long-teased expansion for the Tamriel Rebuilt Morrowind mod
- Players can join Great House Indoril, a major Dunmer faction
- Players can also join the Temple Ordinators, Morrowind's lawkeeping order
- The expansion adds a new explorable region with gardens and fields
- The mod gradually expands the game world beyond Vvardenfell's original map
