## Summary
Take-Two Interactive has filed subpoenas against Microsoft, Discord, and X in connection with ongoing Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks. The legal action represents the game publisher's effort to identify sources behind the unauthorized disclosure of content related to the highly anticipated title.

## Key Points
- Take-Two Interactive initiated legal proceedings via subpoenas against three major tech platforms
- The subpoenas target Microsoft, Discord, and X in relation to GTA 6 leak investigations
- This marks an active legal response from the publisher to contain ongoing content leaks
- The move reflects the growing tension between game developers and platforms where unauthorized game content is shared
