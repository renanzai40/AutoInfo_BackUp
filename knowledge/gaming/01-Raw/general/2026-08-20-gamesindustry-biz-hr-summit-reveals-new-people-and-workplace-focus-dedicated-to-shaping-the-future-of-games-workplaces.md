## Summary
The GamesIndustry.biz HR Summit 2025, on October 1 at the Royal Institution, introduces a new People and Workplace focus. It will feature sessions for HR professionals and business leaders to discuss the modern games workplace amid industry turmoil. The event aims to shape the future of games workplaces.

## Key Points
- GamesIndustry.biz HR Summit 2025 has a new People and Workplace focus.
- Event date: October 1, location: Royal Institution.
- Targets HR professionals and business leaders in the games industry.
- Aims to address and adapt to the current turmoil in the gaming industry.
- Seeks to shape the future of workplaces in the games sector.
