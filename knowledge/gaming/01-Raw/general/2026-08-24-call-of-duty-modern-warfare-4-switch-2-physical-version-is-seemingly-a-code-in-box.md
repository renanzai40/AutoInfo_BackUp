## Summary
Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is coming to Switch 2 on the same day as other platforms, but the Switch 2 version's physical copy may only contain a download code. This is the first Call of Duty game on a Nintendo console since 2013. The game's release is anticipated but the code-in-box approach for the physical Switch 2 version may disappoint some fans.
