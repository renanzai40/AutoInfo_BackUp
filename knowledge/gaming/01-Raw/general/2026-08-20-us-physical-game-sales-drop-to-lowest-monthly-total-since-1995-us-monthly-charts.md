## Summary
US physical game sales reached a 28-year low in July, totaling $85 million, the lowest since tracking began in 1995. This decline reflects a significant drop in physical software purchases. The sales figure is based on data from Circana.

## Key Points
- US physical game sales hit $85 million in July, the lowest since 1995
- This is the lowest monthly total since Circana started tracking in 1995
- The decline indicates a sharp drop in physical software sales
