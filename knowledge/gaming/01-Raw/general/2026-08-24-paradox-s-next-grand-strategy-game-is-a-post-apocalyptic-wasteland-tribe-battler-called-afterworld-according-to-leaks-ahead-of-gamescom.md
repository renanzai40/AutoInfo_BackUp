## Summary
Paradox Interactive's next grand strategy game, reportedly titled Afterworld, is a post-apocalyptic survival strategist set in a wasteland where tribes battle for survival. The title leaked via Paradox's forums ahead of an official reveal scheduled for August 25th at Gamescom.

## Key Points
- Paradox Interactive is developing a new grand strategy game called Afterworld
- The game is set in a post-apocalyptic wasteland featuring tribe-based combat and survival mechanics
- The title was leaked through Paradox's own forums before the official Gamescom reveal on August 25th
- Paradox is known for strategy titles such as Crusader Kings 3 and Europa Universalis 4
