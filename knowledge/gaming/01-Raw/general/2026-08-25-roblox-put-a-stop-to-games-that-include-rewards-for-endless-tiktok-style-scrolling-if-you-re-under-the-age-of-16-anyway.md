## Summary
Roblox has stopped games that reward endless, TikTok-style scrolling for users under 16. A popular game, Steal an Egg, featured such a mechanic, allowing players to 'doomscroll' through TikTok videos for rewards.

## Key Points
- Roblox banned reward systems tied to endless scrolling for users under 16.
- The change affects games like 'Steal an Egg', which used TikTok-style scrolling for progression.
- The policy aims to protect younger users from potentially harmful engagement mechanisms.
