## Summary
Nvidia's ACE generative AI technology, previously used for PUBG's AI teammates, will be integrated into Aniimo, a creature-collecting RPG from Pawprint Studio, allowing players to chat with their creatures. The technology is expected to arrive early next year.

## Key Points
- Nvidia ACE tech is expanding from PUBG to support AI interactions in Aniimo
- The technology enables natural conversation between players and creature characters
- Aniimo is a Pokemon-style creature-collecting RPG developed by Chinese studio Pawprint Studio
- The integration is planned for early next year alongside Nvidia's DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction release
