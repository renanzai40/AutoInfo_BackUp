## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ahead of the first anniversary of <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/games/dispatch/">Dispatch</a>, developer AdHoc Studio&nbsp;has announced that the superhero workplace comedy-drama is finally getting a physical edition release for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch. Published in partnership with PM Studios, the release offers fans a choice between a standard and a steelbook edition, each featuring an assortment of physical and digital goodies.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The $50 Standard Deluxe Edition includes the full game on disc/cartridge, all DLC, an artbook with a digital copy, four digital comics, a Superhero Dispatch Network booklet, a download key for the game soundtrack, and a double-sided poster. If you feel like spending a little more, you can grab the Steelbook Deluxe Edition for $60. This includes all of the aforementioned extras, as well as a steelbook cover featuring box art created in-house at AdHoc Studio. The steelbook edition might also be released later due to manufacturing times, AdHoc added.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Each version will be available from retailers such as Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Target, Walmart, and more. With the&nbsp;Nintendo copy of Dispatch, AdHoc has noted that it is the Switch 1&nbsp;version of the game, and it includes&nbsp;the&nbsp;Switch 2 Edition upgrade pack--as well as some censorship, but the studio did manage to <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/dispatch-has-found-the-funniest-possible-way-to-fix-its-switch-censorship/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">apply some hilarious fixes</a> to meet Nintendo's standards.</p>

The Prime Edition&nbsp;has all three records pressed on classic black vinyl, while the Astral Edition offers the same three records, each pressed in the colors of a key Superhero Dispatch Network employee: Mecha Man blue, Invisigal translucent purple, and Blonde Blazer's red-and-yellow color scheme.</p>

## Summary
Dispatch, a superhero workplace comedy-drama, is getting physical editions (Standard Deluxe and Steelbook Deluxe) for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch, including various goodies. A physical vinyl soundtrack release is also announced in two editions (Prime and Astral) through Lost in Cult Records.

## Key Points
- Dispatch physical editions (Standard Deluxe $50, Steelbook Deluxe $60) announced for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Switch
- Editions include game, DLC, artbook, comics, soundtrack, and more; Steelbook has exclusive cover
- Nintendo Switch version includes Switch 2 Edition upgrade pack with some censorship fixes
- Physical vinyl soundtrack in two editions (Prime and Astral) available for preorder
