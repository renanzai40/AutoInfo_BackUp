## Summary
Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, has filed multiple subpoenas in a federal district court seeking broad access to user data from Discord and Microsoft in connection with the Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks. However, Discord has confirmed it has not yet been served the subpoena.

## Key Points
- Take-Two filed multiple subpoenas in a federal district court related to the GTA 6 leaks
- The subpoenas are intended to obtain a wide range of user data from Discord and Microsoft
- Discord states it has not yet been served the subpoena from Take-Two
- The legal action targets individuals or platforms involved in leaking Grand Theft Auto 6 content
