## Summary
Pearl Abyss's open-world game "红色沙漠" (Red Desert) is nominated for two Gamescom Awards 2026: "Most Epic Game" and "Best PC Game". The game will be showcased at Gamescom from Aug 26-30, with playable demos on Samsung's 6K gaming monitors.

## Key Points
- 红色沙漠" (Red Desert) nominated for "Most Epic Game" and "Best PC Game" at Gamescom Awards 2026
- Awards decided by professional jury and public voting (onsite and online)
- Pearl Abyss and Samsung to showcase the game at Gamescom (Aug 26-30) with 30 playable PCs featuring Samsung's 6K "Odyssey G8" monitors
