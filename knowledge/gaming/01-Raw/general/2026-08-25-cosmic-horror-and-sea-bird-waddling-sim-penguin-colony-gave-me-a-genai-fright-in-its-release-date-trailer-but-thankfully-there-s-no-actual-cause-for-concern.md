## Summary
Penguin Colony, a sea bird waddling sim with cosmic horror elements, revealed its release date trailer, initially alarming with mentions of 'genAI' which were later clarified as tongue-in-cheek references (e.g., 'GENeral Antarctic Isolation'). The game features RTX and DLSS, and is developed by the creators of Umurangi Generation.

## Key Points
- Penguin Colony combines sea bird simulation with cosmic horror themes.
- The game utilizes NVIDIA RTX and DLSS technologies.
- Initial 'genAI' mentions in the trailer were humorous, not indicative of actual AI integration for gameplay.
- Developed by the studio behind Umurangi Generation.
