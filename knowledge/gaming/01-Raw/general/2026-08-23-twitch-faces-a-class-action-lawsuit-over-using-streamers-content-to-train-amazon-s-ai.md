## Summary
A Twitch streamer has filed a class action lawsuit against Twitch and its parent company Amazon, alleging that streamers' content was used to train Amazon's generative AI without consent or payment. The lawsuit claims this practice affected countless creators. Legal action seeks compensation and policy change.

## Key Points
- Class action lawsuit filed against Twitch and Amazon
- Allegations of using streamer content to train Amazon's generative AI without consent
- No payment was made to the streamers for the use of their content
