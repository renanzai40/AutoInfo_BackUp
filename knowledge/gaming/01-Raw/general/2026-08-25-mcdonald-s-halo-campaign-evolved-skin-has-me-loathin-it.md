## Original Content
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Eating a Big Mac can help you obtain a special armor set for Master Chief in the UK and Ireland. Thanks to a new McDonald's collaboration with Xbox, there is a new Halo: Campaign Evolved skin that's mainly red and gray, with a yellow visor and flourishes. It costs 1,500 rewards points in the McDonald's app--a tier in the US that will get you a free apple pie, small coffee, or vanilla cone.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Now we could talk about the hilarity of a Master Chief skin for a game predominately played from the first-person perspective, though <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/halo-1-remake-lets-you-play-in-third-person-and-it-looks-great/">Halo: Campaign Evolved does let you experience the classic game in third-person</a>. Or we could talk about how this particular armor set (seen below via <a href="https://x.com/Mr_Rebs_/status/2092254489067630741">RebsGaming</a>) looks like something that should be available to just customize within the title's menu. It's really not much more than a palette swap. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-rich is-provider-x wp-block-embed-x"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But probably the biggest issue is the armor set, named Side Mission, just screams bland and boring. Like if you're going to have a McDonald's-specific skin in Halo: Campaign Evolved, give it some fun character. Put the iconic "M" arch on Chief's helmet, for instance, sort of like the added decal flair on <a href="https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Magneto%27s_Helmet">Magneto's helmet</a>. Or make the visor look like Ronald McDonald, similar to what we did with the horrifying thumbnail on this story.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Now Halo: Campaign Evolved isn't the only Xbox-owned game to get a special reward <a href="https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/newsroom/article/mcdonalds-xbox-unlock-new-level-side-missions.html">at McDonald's in the UK and Ireland</a>. The other titles involved in the collaboration are Forza Horizon 6, Fallout 76, Candy Crush Saga, World of Warcraft, and Overwatch. GameSpot has reached out to McDonald's about a similar promotion coming to other parts of the world, including North America.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We also have codes to grab <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/halo-campaign-evolved-codes-for-free-armor-sets/">free alternate armor sets in Halo: Campaign Evolved</a>. For more, check out GameSpot's <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/it-feels-wrong-for-halo-to-feel-better-than-ever-on-ps5/">Halo: Campaign Evolved review</a>. The game is available on PC, Xbox Series X|S, and for the first time ever, PS5.&nbsp;</p>

## Summary
McDonald's UK and Ireland offer a Halo: Campaign Evolved skin, 'Side Mission', for Master Chief via their app rewards. The skin is criticized for being bland. The collaboration with Xbox also includes other games like Forza Horizon 6 and Overwatch.

## Key Points
- McDonald's UK/Ireland offers Halo: Campaign Evolved 'Side Mission' skin for 1,500 rewards points
- Skin criticized for being a bland palette swap with minimal creative tie-in to McDonald's
- Collaboration includes other Xbox titles: Forza Horizon 6, Fallout 76, Candy Crush Saga, World of Warcraft, Overwatch
- Promotion's global expansion, including North America, under inquiry
