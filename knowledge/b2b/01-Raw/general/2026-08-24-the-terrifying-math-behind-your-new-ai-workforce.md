## Summary
Autonomous marketing agents promise significant labor savings, but escalating API fees and middleware costs can unexpectedly inflate technology budgets. This article examines the financial risks and challenges in deploying AI-driven marketing tools.

## Key Points
- Autonomous marketing agents can lead to substantial labor cost reductions in marketing operations.
- API fees and middleware costs are ballooning and can easily exceed initial budget projections.
- Failure to account for these hidden expenses may result in technology budget overruns.
- Proper financial planning is essential when adopting AI workforce solutions.
