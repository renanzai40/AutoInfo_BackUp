## Summary
Nvidia has evolved from solely selling chips to a comprehensive provider of financing, infrastructure guarantees, and investment for AI companies. It now supports the entire ecosystem by funding power, land, AI models, and creating financial instruments for broader investment in AI infrastructure.

## Key Points
- Nvidia has expanded its business model to finance companies that purchase its chips.
- The company guarantees the infrastructure required for housing AI systems.
- Nvidia invests in power and land resources necessary for AI operations.
- It funds the development of AI models that consume its chips.
- Nvidia creates financial instruments to enable others to invest in AI infrastructure.
