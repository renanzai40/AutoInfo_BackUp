## Summary
An empty property is not a dormant asset; it continues to incur ongoing costs such as taxes, insurance premiums, utilities, and maintenance. These expenses draw down cash regardless of occupancy, behaving like a subscription for businesses. The article highlights the financial burden of vacant houses for businesses.

## Key Points
- Vacant properties generate ongoing costs even when unused.
- Expenses include taxes, premiums, utilities, and maintenance.
- These costs are recurring and reduce cash flow for businesses.
- The situation is compared to a subscription model.
