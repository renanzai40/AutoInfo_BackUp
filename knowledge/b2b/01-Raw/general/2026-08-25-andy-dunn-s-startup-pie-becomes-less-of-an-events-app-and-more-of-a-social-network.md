## Summary
Andy Dunn's startup Pie is evolving from an events-focused app into a broader social network by introducing digital spaces for groups to connect, organize, and make plans. This expansion aims to enhance social connectivity beyond event management.

## Key Points
- Pie was founded by Andy Dunn, co-founder of Bonobos.
- The app is transitioning from an events app to a full social network.
- New features include digital homes for group interactions and planning.
- This shift represents growth in the social media and app industry.
