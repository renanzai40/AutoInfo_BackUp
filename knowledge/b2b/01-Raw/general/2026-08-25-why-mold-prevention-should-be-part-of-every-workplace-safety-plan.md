## Summary
This article emphasizes that mold prevention is often overlooked in workplace safety plans, despite mold posing similar health risks to other tracked hazards. It points out that mold can grow undetected until signs like musty smells appear, stressing the need for proactive integration into safety protocols.

## Key Points
- Mold is frequently omitted from standard workplace safety reviews.
- Unlike fire or chemical hazards, mold is not routinely assessed in safety plans.
- Mold can silently grow in buildings, leading to health issues before detection.
- Integrating mold prevention into existing safety frameworks is essential for occupational health.
