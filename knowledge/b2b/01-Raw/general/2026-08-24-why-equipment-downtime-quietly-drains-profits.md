## Summary
Facility leaders often overlook the gradual efficiency loss in mechanical systems, which accumulates over months until revealed by rising utility bills or maintenance costs. This hidden downtime quietly drains profits for organizations operating commercial buildings, plants, or campuses.

## Key Points
- Equipment downtime starts with a slow decline in efficiency rather than sudden failure.
- No alarms or immediate indicators detect the problem early.
- Financial impacts become evident only after prolonged periods through utility or maintenance expenses.
- Organizations managing commercial buildings, plants, or campuses are significantly affected by this erosion.
