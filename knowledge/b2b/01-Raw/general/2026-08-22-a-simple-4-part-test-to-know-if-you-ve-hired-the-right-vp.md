## Summary
This article presents a 4-part test to evaluate whether a newly hired VP is effective, based on their hiring decisions, key metric improvements, task delegation, and team morale. It suggests that failing any part indicates the VP is not suitable for the role.

## Key Points
- The test includes evaluating who the VP hires in the first 60 days to assess hiring quality.
- It checks if the VP's #1 key metric has improved to measure performance.
- It considers whether the VP has taken part of your job off your plate, indicating effective delegation.
- It assesses if the team believes in the VP, reflecting leadership and trust.
- Failing any part of the test means the VP is not good enough for the position.
