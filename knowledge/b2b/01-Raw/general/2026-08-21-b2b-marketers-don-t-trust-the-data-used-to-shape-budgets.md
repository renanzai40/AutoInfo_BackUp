## Summary
B2B marketers rely on data to shape budgets, but less than half fully trust its accuracy and completeness. This distrust is primarily due to disconnected reporting systems that create data gaps.

## Key Points
- Only 49% of B2B marketers fully trust the accuracy and completeness of marketing data.
- Disconnected reporting is the main cause of the confidence gap in data used for budgets.
- Marketing data influences budget decisions despite the widespread mistrust.
