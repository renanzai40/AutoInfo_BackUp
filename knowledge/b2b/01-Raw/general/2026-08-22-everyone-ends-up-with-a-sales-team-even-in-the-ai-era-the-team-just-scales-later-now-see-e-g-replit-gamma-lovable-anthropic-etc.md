## Summary
The article discusses how sales teams become essential for company scaling, even in AI-driven tech businesses, using Replit's CEO as an example of shifting from a product-focused to a sales-heavy workforce. It emphasizes that this transition occurs later in growth phases despite initial aversion to sales culture.

## Key Points
- Sales roles are inevitable for company growth, regardless of AI advancements.
- Technical founders often initially resist sales but eventually prioritize it for scaling.
- Companies like Replit, Gamma, Lovable, and Anthropic are scaling their sales teams in the AI era.
- The shift to sales-heavy teams happens later in the company lifecycle after product development.
