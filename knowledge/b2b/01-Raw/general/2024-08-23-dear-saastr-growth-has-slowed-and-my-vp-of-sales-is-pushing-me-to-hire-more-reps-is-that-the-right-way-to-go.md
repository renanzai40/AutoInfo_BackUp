## Summary
The article advises against hiring more sales reps when company growth slows, stating it is a desperation move and rarely the right solution. It indicates that this action is a key sign that the VP of Sales is struggling in their role.

## Key Points
- Hiring more sales reps is not advisable when growth slows, as it is ineffective 9.8 times out of 10.
- Such hiring is considered a desperation move in business and sales contexts.
- This action is the primary indicator that the CRO or VP of Sales is struggling.
