## Summary
Homeowners often notice that resin-based countertops and surfaces develop an amber or yellow tone over time, which they may mistakenly attribute to installation flaws. This discoloration is actually a well-documented characteristic of the material. The article explains this common oversight and its natural cause.

## Key Points
- Resin-based countertops, floors, and accent surfaces naturally yellow or develop an amber tone over time.
- This color change is a documented property of resin materials, not necessarily an indication of defects.
- Homeowners frequently misinterpret this shift as a flaw in installation or product quality.
- The phenomenon highlights the importance of understanding material characteristics before installation.
