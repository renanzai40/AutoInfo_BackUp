SpaceX has announced plans to build a second 'Starbase' spaceport in Louisiana. The $100 billion project will start construction in 2027, with the first Starship rocket potentially launching by 2029.

- SpaceX is constructing a new spaceport in Louisiana.
- The project is valued at $100 billion.
- Construction will begin in 2027.
- A Starship rocket could take flight as early as 2029.
- This expansion marks the second Starbase facility.
