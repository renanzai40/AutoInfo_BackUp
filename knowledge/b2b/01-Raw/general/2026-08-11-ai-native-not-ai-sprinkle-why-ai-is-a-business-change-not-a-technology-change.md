## Original Content
Companies unlock AI’s biggest productivity gains not by adding AI tools to existing workflows, but by redesigning teams, roles and daily work around AI. The broader lesson, argues Bob Morse, co-founder and managing partner of Strattam Capital, is that achieving transformational gains like 3x productivity requires CEO- and board-backed organizational change — not merely technology adoption.

## Summary
The article argues that companies achieve significant AI productivity gains by redesigning teams and workflows around AI rather than simply adding AI tools to existing processes. It emphasizes that transformational benefits require CEO- and board-backed organizational change, not just technology adoption.

## Key Points
- AI's biggest productivity gains come from redesigning organizational structures and daily work around AI.
- Adding AI tools to existing workflows without broader change yields limited benefits.
- CEO and board support is essential for implementing transformative AI initiatives.
- Being 'AI-native' involves a holistic business change, contrasting with superficial 'AI-sprinkle' approaches.
