## Summary
The article emphasizes that marketing effectiveness improves by not stopping at campaign reports. By asking why and testing hypotheses, marketers can turn initial results into better, data-driven decisions.

## Key Points
- Campaign reports should not be the end of analysis in marketing.
- Asking 'why' is crucial for deeper understanding of results.
- Testing hypotheses transforms data into actionable insights.
- This approach leads to improved decision-making in marketing strategies.
