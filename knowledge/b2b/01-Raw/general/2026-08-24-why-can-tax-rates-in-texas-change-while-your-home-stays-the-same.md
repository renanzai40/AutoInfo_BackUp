## Summary
Tax rates for properties in Texas can change annually even if the property remains unchanged, as they are determined by local budgets and public votes from various taxing units. The article explains that rates are set through public votes each fall and involve multiple entities like school districts.

## Key Points
- Tax rates change yearly based on local budgets, not the condition of the home.
- Every taxing unit resets its rate through a public vote each fall.
- Property tax rates in Texas come from several local units, including school districts.
