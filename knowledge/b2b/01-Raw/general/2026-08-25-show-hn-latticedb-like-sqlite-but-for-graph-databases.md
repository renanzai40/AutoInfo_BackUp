## Original Content
We have been using graph DBs more and more at work. I found them painful to work with locally and decided to try and build something better.

## Summary
The author has been using graph databases at work and found them difficult to use locally, prompting the creation of LatticeDB. LatticeDB is a graph database designed to simplify local usage, similar to SQLite for relational databases. It aims to address pain points in working with graph databases on a local scale.

## Key Points
- Graph databases are seeing increased use in workplace environments.
- Local development with graph databases is often challenging and cumbersome.
- LatticeDB is developed as a solution to improve the local usability of graph databases.
- The design of LatticeDB draws inspiration from SQLite's approach to relational databases.
- LatticeDB is introduced on Hacker News to showcase its development and gather feedback.
