## Summary
Apple has announced two new chips: the M6 and M5 Ultra, with the M5 Ultra being presented as the company's most powerful chip to date. The M6 chip is featured in a new Mac Mini model that includes updated features compared to previous versions.

## Key Points
- Apple introduced the M6 and M5 Ultra chips, expanding its line of processors.
- The M5 Ultra is marketed as Apple's most powerful chip ever, indicating significant performance improvements.
- The M6 chip is used in a new Mac Mini, which is compared to the earlier M4 Mac Mini model.
- These new chips likely offer enhanced capabilities and performance for Apple's devices.
