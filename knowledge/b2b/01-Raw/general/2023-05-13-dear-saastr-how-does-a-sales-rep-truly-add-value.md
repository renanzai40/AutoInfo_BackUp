## Summary
The article examines whether salespeople add value for customers, emphasizing that in enterprise and mid-market settings, skilled sales representatives can significantly enhance value. It distinguishes between transactional and solution sales, noting that solution sales reps make the buyer's process easier and more efficient.

## Key Points
- Skilled sales reps add substantial value in enterprise sales environments.
- Founders often overlook the importance of sales reps if they lack experience with mid-market and enterprise sales.
- Sales can be categorized into transactional and solution-oriented approaches.
- Solution sales reps improve the buyer's experience by simplifying complex processes.
