## Summary
The article argues that B2B trade shows remain more effective than digital campaigns for generating high-quality, face-to-face leads with decision-makers. It emphasizes that comparing superficial metrics like clicks to meaningful conversations is misleading and detrimental to business pipeline growth.

## Key Points
- Trade shows generate qualified conversations with individuals who have budget, authority, and decision-making power.
- Digital campaigns such as LinkedIn produce high click volumes but low-quality leads.
- In-person interactions at trade shows foster deeper engagement that digital methods cannot easily replicate.
- The value of trade shows is practical for lead generation, not merely a nostalgic preference.
