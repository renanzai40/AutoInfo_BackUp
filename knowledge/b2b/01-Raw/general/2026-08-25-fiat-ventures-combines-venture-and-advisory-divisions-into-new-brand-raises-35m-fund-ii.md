## Summary
Fiat Ventures (FGV) has combined its venture and advisory divisions under a new unified brand and has successfully raised $35 million for its second fund (Fund II). This move comes as the firm aims to differentiate its venture model to attract limited partners (LPs) in a challenging fundraising environment for emerging managers.

## Key Points
- Fiat Ventures has merged its venture and advisory operations into a single, new brand.
- The firm has closed a $35 million Fund II, signaling successful fundraising despite market challenges.
- FGV is adopting a differentiated venture model to compete for attention from limited partners (LPs) as an emerging fund manager.
