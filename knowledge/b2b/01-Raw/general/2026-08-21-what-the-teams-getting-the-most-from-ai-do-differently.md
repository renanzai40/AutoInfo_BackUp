## Summary
The article explains that teams excelling with AI are those built around autonomy, experimentation, and cross-functional collaboration. This structure allows them to leverage AI's speed for faster marketing campaigns without increasing headcount.

## Key Points
- Teams prioritizing autonomy, experimentation, and cross-functional work are most effective in utilizing AI.
- AI can significantly accelerate marketing campaign timelines.
- Improved efficiency and speed can be achieved without adding new staff.
