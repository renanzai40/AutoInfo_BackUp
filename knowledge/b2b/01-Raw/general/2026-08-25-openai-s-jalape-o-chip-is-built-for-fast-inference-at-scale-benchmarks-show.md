## Summary
OpenAI's Jalapeño chip has been benchmarked for inference performance, demonstrating superior tokens per user and energy efficiency compared to current state-of-the-art technology. The testing was conducted using SemiAnalysis' InferenceX benchmark.

## Key Points
- Jalapeño chip is designed for fast inference at scale.
- Benchmarks show it achieves more tokens per user than state-of-the-art.
- It offers higher throughput per kilowatt, improving energy efficiency.
- Testing was performed on SemiAnalysis' InferenceX benchmark.
