## Original Content
Elon Musk, le patron de SpaceX, a annoncé mardi vouloir construire en Louisiane le plus grand site de lancement au monde, capable d'accueillir des milliers de vols de sa mégafusée Starship chaque année, avec un premier tir espéré en 2029.

## Summary
Elon Musk, CEO of SpaceX, announced plans to build the world's largest space launch site in Louisiana. The facility is designed to support thousands of Starship launches annually, with the first launch expected in 2029.

## Key Points
- SpaceX plans to construct the largest launch site globally in Louisiana.
- The site will be capable of hosting thousands of Starship launches per year.
- The first launch is targeted for 2029.
