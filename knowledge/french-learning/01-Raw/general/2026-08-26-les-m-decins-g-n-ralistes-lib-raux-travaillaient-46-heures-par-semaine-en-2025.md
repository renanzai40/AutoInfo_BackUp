## Original Content
Selon une étude du ministère de la Santé, les jeunes praticiens ont des horaires, en moyenne, moins lourds que leurs aînés.

## Summary
A 2025 study by the Ministry of Health found that general practitioners in private practice worked an average of 46 hours per week. The study also revealed that younger practitioners tend to have lighter work schedules compared to their senior colleagues.

## Key Points
- General practitioners worked an average of 46 hours per week in 2025.
- Younger practitioners have lighter work schedules than older practitioners.
- The data is based on a Ministry of Health study.
