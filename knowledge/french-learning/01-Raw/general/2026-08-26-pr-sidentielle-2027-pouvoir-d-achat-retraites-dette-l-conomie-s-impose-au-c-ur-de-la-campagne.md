## Original Content
DÉCRYPTAGE - Sept candidats à l’Élysée débattront ce jeudi à la « Rencontre des Entrepreneurs de France » (REF) du Medef, où des patrons de PME les interrogeront sur leurs préoccupations.

## Summary
The article discusses an upcoming debate among seven candidates for the 2027 French presidential election at the 'Rencontre des Entrepreneurs de France' event organized by Medef. Economic issues such as purchasing power, pensions, and debt will be central topics, with SME bosses questioning the candidates, highlighting the economy's prominence in the campaign.

## Key Points
- Seven candidates for the French presidency will debate at the REF event.
- The event is hosted by Medef and focuses on economic concerns.
- Key economic issues include purchasing power, pensions, and debt.
- SME leaders will participate by questioning the candidates.
- The debate underscores the economy's central role in the 2027 presidential race.
