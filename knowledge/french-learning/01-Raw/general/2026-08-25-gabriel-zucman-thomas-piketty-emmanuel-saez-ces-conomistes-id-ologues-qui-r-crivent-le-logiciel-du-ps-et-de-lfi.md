## Original Content
Ils conseillent depuis des années les formations de gauche en Europe et aux États-Unis. Leur idée fixe : instaurer une taxe sur le patrimoine des plus riches. À court terme, leurs espoirs portent sur la Californie et la France.

## Summary
This article discusses influential economists Gabriel Zucman, Thomas Piketty, and Emmanuel Saez who have been advising left-wing political formations in Europe and the United States for years. They advocate for implementing a wealth tax on the richest individuals, with their short-term political hopes focused on California and France.

## Key Points
- Economists like Zucman, Piketty, and Saez have long provided advisory roles to left-wing parties in Europe and the US.
- Their central policy idea is the establishment of a wealth tax targeting the patrimony of the wealthiest individuals.
- Currently, their efforts and hopes for policy adoption are directed towards California and France.
