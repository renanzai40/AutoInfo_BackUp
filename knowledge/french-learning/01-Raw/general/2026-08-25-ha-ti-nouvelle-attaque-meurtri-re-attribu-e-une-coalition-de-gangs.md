## Original Content
Au moins 47 personnes ont été tuées et 22 autres blessées lors d'une nouvelle attaque de gangs en #Haïti, selon un bilan provisoire de l’#ONU. La population dénonce le manque de protection face aux #violences répétées dans un pays en proie au chaos sécuritaire.

## Summary
A deadly gang attack in Haiti has killed at least 47 people and injured 22 others, according to a preliminary UN report. The population is denouncing the lack of protection against repeated violence in the country, which is facing a severe security crisis.

## Key Points
- At least 47 deaths and 22 injuries occurred in a gang attack in Haiti.
- The attack is attributed to a coalition of gangs.
- The United Nations (UN) reported the preliminary casualty figures.
- The population criticizes inadequate protection amidst ongoing violence.
- Haiti is experiencing severe security chaos with repeated violent incidents.
