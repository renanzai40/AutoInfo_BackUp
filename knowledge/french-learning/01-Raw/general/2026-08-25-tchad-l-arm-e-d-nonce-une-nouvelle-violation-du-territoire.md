## Original Content
Le conflit au Soudan déborde de nouveau au Tchad. L'armée tchadienne affirme que des aéronefs et des drones liés aux belligérants soudanais ont poursuivi et bombardé une colonne de véhicules jusque sur le territoire tchadien. La correspondance à Ndjamena de Ghislaine Allahtaroum.

## Summary
The Chadian army has denounced a new violation of its territory, claiming that aircraft and drones linked to Sudanese belligerents pursued and bombed a vehicle column into Chad. This incident represents another spillover of the ongoing conflict in Sudan into Chadian territory. The report was provided by a correspondent in Ndjamena.

## Key Points
- The conflict in Sudan is extending into Chadian territory with repeated violations.
- Chadian army reports that aircraft and drones from Sudanese conflict parties were involved in the bombing.
- The incident occurred on Chadian soil after a pursuit from Sudanese territory.
- This is described as a 'new violation', indicating it is not an isolated event.
- The event was reported by correspondent Ghislaine Allahtaroum in Ndjamena.
