## Original Content
En Guinée, au moins trente et une personnes sont mortes dans l'éboulement d'une décharge. Le gouvernement affirme que "d'importants moyens matériels et humains ont été mobilisés" pour rechercher d'éventuelles personnes encore ensevelies. Le récit d'Emily Boyle.

## Summary
A landslide in a landfill in Guinea has caused at least 31 deaths. The government has mobilized significant material and human resources to search for people still buried. The narrative is provided by Emily Boyle.

## Key Points
- At least 31 people died in the landfill landslide in Guinea.
- The government has deployed major rescue resources for ongoing search operations.
- Authorities are looking for possible survivors still buried under debris.
- The incident highlights disaster response in a humanitarian context.
