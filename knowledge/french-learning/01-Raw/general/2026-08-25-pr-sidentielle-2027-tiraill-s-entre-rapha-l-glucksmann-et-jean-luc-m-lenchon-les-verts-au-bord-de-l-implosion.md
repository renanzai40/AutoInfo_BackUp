## Original Content
RÉCIT - Les Écologistes de Marine Tondelier sont pris en étau entre les partisans d’une alliance avec la social-démocratie, emmenés par Yannick Jadot, et ceux qui défendent un rapprochement avec les Insoumis, comme Sandrine Rousseau.

## Summary
The article highlights internal strife within the French Green party, led by Marine Tondelier, as factions debate alliances with social democrats or the Insoumis movement for the 2027 presidential election. This conflict threatens party unity, with figures like Yannick Jadot and Sandrine Rousseau representing opposing views.

## Key Points
- The Green party faces significant internal division ahead of the 2027 presidential election.
- One faction supports alliance with social democracy, led by Yannick Jadot.
- Another faction favors closer ties with the Insoumis, represented by Sandrine Rousseau.
- There is a risk of party implosion due to these tensions.
