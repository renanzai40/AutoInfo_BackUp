## Original Content
Selon les données du site Flightradar24, un avion militaire a d’abord volé dimanche de la base aérienne d’Andrews, située près de Washington et utilisée pour des déplacements de hauts responsables, jusqu’à Riga, la capitale de la Lettonie.

## Summary
American press reports suggest that the CIA director might be visiting Moscow amid the Ukraine war, based on flight tracking data. A military aircraft was observed flying from Andrews Air Force Base to Riga, Latvia, potentially indicating diplomatic or intelligence movements.

## Key Points
- CIA director's possible visit to Moscow is reported by American media.
- Flight tracking data from Flightradar24 shows an aircraft traveling from Andrews Air Force Base to Riga, Latvia.
- The context is the ongoing war in Ukraine.
- This movement could involve intelligence or diplomatic activities.
- The aircraft is a military plane from a base near Washington.
