## Original Content
Selon les médias américains CBS et Axios, le patron de la CIA, John Ratcliffe, est en visite mardi à Moscou. Une annonce que ni le gouvernement américain ni le Kremlin n'ont jusqu'ici officiellement confirmé.

## Summary
According to American media, the CIA director John Ratcliffe is reportedly visiting Moscow, but this has not been officially confirmed by the US government or the Kremlin.

## Key Points
- The CIA head John Ratcliffe is visiting Moscow.
- The information is reported by media sources CBS and Axios.
- Neither the American government nor the Kremlin have officially confirmed the visit.
