## Original Content
Cette propriété se trouve à l’un des accès de la base navale de Muskö, située à une soixantaine de kilomètres au sud de Stockholm, a indiqué l’armée dans un communiqué.

## Summary
Sweden plans to expropriate a property near the Muskö naval base that is owned by a businessman linked to Vladimir Putin. The Swedish army has confirmed the property is located at an access point to the base, about 60 km south of Stockholm. This move appears to be driven by security or geopolitical concerns.

## Key Points
- Sweden intends to expropriate property near the Muskö naval base.
- The property is owned by a businessman with ties to Russian President Vladimir Putin.
- The army has verified the property's strategic location at the base.
- This action may be motivated by security concerns due to the base's importance.
