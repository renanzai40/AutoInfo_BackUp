## Original Content
RÉCIT - Le terrain sur lequel des investisseurs saoudiens veulent construire trois parcs d’attractions a accueilli en 1987 un projet ambitieux qui voulait raconter nos contes et légendes. Il n’aura survécu que cinq ans.

## Summary
Mirapolis was the first large French amusement park, launched in 1987 with a theme based on French tales and legends. It failed after only five years, and the land is now planned for new amusement parks by Saudi investors.

## Key Points
- Mirapolis was an ambitious amusement park project in France initiated in 1987.
- It aimed to celebrate French folklore and legends through its attractions.
- The park was a fiasco and did not survive beyond five years.
- The same land is being repurposed for three new amusement parks by Saudi investors.
