## Original Content
ENTRETIEN - Le futur parc consacré au dessin animé japonais, financé par l’Arabie saoudite, est révélateur de la place qu’occupe désormais la France dans la mondialisation : une destination récréative, où les usines ferment et les touristes affluent, analyse le sondeur.

## Summary
The article features an interview with pollster Jérôme Fourquet discussing the upcoming Dragon Ball-themed park in France, which is financed by Saudi Arabia. He analyzes this as a reflection of France's role in globalization, highlighting its shift towards becoming a recreational destination amid industrial decline.

## Key Points
- A Dragon Ball-themed park is planned for France.
- The project is funded by Saudi Arabia.
- Pollster Jérôme Fourquet suggests this indicates France's evolving position in the global economy.
- France is becoming a major hub for tourism and recreation.
- The article contrasts this with the closure of factories, signaling industrial decline in France.
