## Original Content
CHRONIQUE - Alors que l’est du continent est confronté à une dangereuse montée aux extrêmes depuis quatre ans et demi, avec la guerre en Ukraine, nous devons nous poser la même question qu’en 1914.

## Summary
Renaud Girard questions whether Europe is unconsciously heading towards World War III, drawing parallels with the geopolitical tensions of 1914. The article highlights the ongoing war in Ukraine and the rise of political extremes in eastern Europe as key concerns. This comparison suggests a potential for global conflict if historical patterns repeat.

## Key Points
- The war in Ukraine has been ongoing for four and a half years, contributing to instability in eastern Europe.
- There is a dangerous rise to political extremes in the eastern part of the European continent.
- The current situation is compared to the events leading to World War I in 1914, raising alarms about potential global conflict.
- The article poses a rhetorical question about whether Europe is sleepwalking towards a major war.
