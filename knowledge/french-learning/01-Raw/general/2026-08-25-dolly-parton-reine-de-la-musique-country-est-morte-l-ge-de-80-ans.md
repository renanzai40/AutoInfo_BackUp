DISPARITION - Auteure-compositrice et interprète de Jolene ou de 9 to 5, Dolly Parton a remporté 11 Grammy Awards et écoulé plus de 100 millions de disques au cours de ses 60 ans de carrière.

## Summary
Dolly Parton, the legendary queen of country music, has passed away at the age of 80. She was a celebrated singer-songwriter with a 60-year career, known for hits like Jolene and 9 to 5, having won 11 Grammy Awards and sold over 100 million records.

## Key Points
- Dolly Parton died at 80 years old
- She was a prominent singer-songwriter with famous songs such as Jolene and 9 to 5
- She won 11 Grammy Awards during her career
- She sold over 100 million records
- Her career spanned 60 years
