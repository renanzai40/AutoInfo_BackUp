## Original Content
La cour de Cassation rappelle qu’un bailleur qui a signé un bail pour un logement non décent ne peut pas ensuite lui donner congé pour remettre en état le logement vide.

## Summary
The Court of Cassation ruled that a landlord who signed a lease for non-decent housing cannot later evict the tenant to restore the property to compliance. This decision underscores the landlord's responsibility to provide decent housing at the time of signing and protects tenants from eviction due to the landlord's initial non-compliance.

## Key Points
- The Court of Cassation issued a legal ruling on landlord-tenant eviction rights.
- A landlord cannot evict a tenant to renovate a property if the lease was originally for non-decent housing.
- This emphasizes the landlord's duty to ensure housing meets standards from the beginning of the lease.
- Tenants have legal protection against eviction for the landlord's failure to comply with housing regulations.
