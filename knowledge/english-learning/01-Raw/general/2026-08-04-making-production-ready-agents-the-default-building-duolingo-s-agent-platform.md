See how we made AI agents easy to build, run, and improve in production.

## Summary
Duolingo has developed an agent platform that simplifies the building, running, and improvement of AI agents in production environments. The platform aims to make production-ready agents the default approach for deployment.

## Key Points
- Duolingo built an agent platform specifically for AI agents.
- The platform streamlines the process of creating AI agents.
- It facilitates easy running and continuous improvement of agents in production settings.
- The goal is to standardize and default to production-ready agents.
