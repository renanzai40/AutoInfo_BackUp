## Original Content
It is believed to be one of the largest mass kidnappings in Haiti in recent years, and one gang leader is threatening to kill the hostages if authorities kill any gang members as they try to secure the community nestled in the hills above Port-au-Prince.

## Summary
A violent gang attack in Haiti has resulted in 47 deaths and the kidnapping of over 50 people, making it one of the largest mass kidnappings in recent years. A gang leader has threatened to execute the hostages if authorities harm any gang members during rescue efforts in the hills above Port-au-Prince.

## Key Points
- More than 50 people were kidnapped in a gang attack in Haiti.
- 47 people were killed in the incident.
- A gang leader threatened to kill hostages if authorities retaliate against gang members.
- This event is one of the largest mass kidnappings in Haiti recently.
- The attack occurred in the hills above Port-au-Prince.
