## Original Content
Most evacuees have been allowed to return to their homes near burn zones in Spokane, despite concerns about lingering toxins.

## Summary
Most evacuees from Spokane wildfires have been allowed to return home, but the area is still considered a hazardous materials situation due to lingering toxins. Authorities have permitted residents to return despite ongoing health concerns from the burn zones.

## Key Points
- Evacuees are returning to homes near wildfire burn zones.
- The environment remains a hazmat situation with lingering toxins.
- Health risks from toxins are a major concern for residents and authorities.
