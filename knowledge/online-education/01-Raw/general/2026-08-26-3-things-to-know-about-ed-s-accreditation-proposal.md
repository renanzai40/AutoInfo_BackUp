## Summary
The U.S. Department of Education has proposed an accreditation initiative aimed at reforming higher education standards. The administration has accused accreditors of neglecting their public trust in this process.

## Key Points
- The Department of Education is introducing a new accreditation proposal.
- The administration claims accreditors are failing to fulfill their public trust responsibilities.
- President Donald Trump is associated with this initiative.
- The proposal could lead to significant changes in how institutions are accredited.
