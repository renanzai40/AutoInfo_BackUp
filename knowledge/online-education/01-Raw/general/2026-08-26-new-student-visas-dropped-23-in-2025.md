## Summary
In 2025, new student visas issued by the United States decreased by 23%. Countries affected by Trump's travel ban and major sources of international students like China and India experienced significant declines.

## Key Points
- New student visas in the U.S. dropped by 23% in 2025.
- Countries under Trump's travel ban had the fewest visas issued.
- China and India, typically the top suppliers of international students, were also heavily impacted.
- The data was analyzed in an article published by Inside Higher Ed on August 26, 2026.
