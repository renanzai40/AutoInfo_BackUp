## Summary
A new book edited by former Columbia University President Lee Bollinger and University of Chicago legal scholar Geoffrey Stone examines how the Trump administration is threatening university autonomy, with the threat described as more serious than during the McCarthy Era. The book discusses the challenges to academic freedom and explores potential measures to safeguard it.

## Key Points
- The Trump administration poses a significant and escalating threat to university autonomy.
- This threat is compared to the McCarthy Era and is deemed more serious in its impact.
- The book addresses the erosion of academic freedom and proposes strategies to protect it.
