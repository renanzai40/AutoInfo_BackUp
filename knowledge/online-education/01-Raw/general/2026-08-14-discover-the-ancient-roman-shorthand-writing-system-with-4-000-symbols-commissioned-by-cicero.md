## Summary
The article describes an ancient Roman shorthand writing system commissioned by Cicero, which uses 4,000 symbols and was invented by Tiro. One symbol, the Tironian et, remains in use today in digital Unicode keyboards. This system highlights early innovations in writing efficiency.

## Key Points
