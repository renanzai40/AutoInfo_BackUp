## Summary
The article defends Nathan Cofnas, stressing that academic freedom must be preserved and critics should not face punishment, even when their actions lead to tragic outcomes. It argues against abandoning these principles in the face of negative consequences.

## Key Points
- Defense of Nathan Cofnas as an exemplar of academic freedom.
- Importance of not penalizing harsh critics in scholarly debates.
- Tragic results do not justify suppressing criticism or academic discourse.
- Academic principles should be upheld despite adverse outcomes.
