## Summary
The University of Chicago is shifting to an 'analog' approach for some courses, moving away from digital methods. This initiative aims to enhance learning through traditional, hands-on techniques. It represents a partial departure from technology-dependent education.

## Key Points
- University of Chicago is introducing analog courses for select subjects.
- The shift involves moving away from digital and online tools.
- The initiative focuses on traditional learning methods to improve educational outcomes.
- It applies only to some courses, not all programs.
