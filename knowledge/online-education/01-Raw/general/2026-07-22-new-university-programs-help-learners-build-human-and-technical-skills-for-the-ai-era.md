## Summary
The article announces new university programs and certifications from Coursera designed to develop human and technical skills for AI-powered futures. These initiatives are informed by findings from the QS World Future Skills Index 2027, which identifies persistent skill gaps based on employer data.

## Key Points
- Coursera is expanding its university content catalog with new certificates and Specializations.
- The programs focus on building both human and technical skills essential for the AI era.
- The QS World Future Skills Index 2027 reports skill gaps from over 92,000 employers.
- These educational offerings aim to prepare learners for future job market demands.
- The content is promoted through the Coursera Blog.
