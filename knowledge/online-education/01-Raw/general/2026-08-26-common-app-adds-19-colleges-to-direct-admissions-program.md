## Summary
Common App has added 19 colleges to its direct admissions program, aiming to simplify the college application process for students. This expansion was announced on August 26, 2026, as reported by Olivia Sanchez.

- The program is designed to streamline first-year admissions for students.
- The update was published on August 26, 2026, by author Olivia Sanchez.
- This initiative is part of efforts to increase accessibility in higher education.
