## Summary
The article examines whether traditional office hours in higher education are still effective or have become outdated. It argues that office hours retain value for student engagement but must adapt to modern learning environments, such as online and hybrid formats.

## Key Points
- Office hours are being re-evaluated for their relevance in current educational settings.
- They still hold significant value for student-faculty interaction and academic support.
- Adaptation is necessary to address challenges like student availability and digital learning trends.
