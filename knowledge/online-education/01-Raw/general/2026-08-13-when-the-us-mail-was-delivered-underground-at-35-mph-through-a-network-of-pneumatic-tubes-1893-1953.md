## Summary
This article examines the historical pneumatic tube system that was used for underground mail delivery in the United States from 1893 to 1953, achieving speeds up to 35 mph. It also highlights US Senator Ted Stevens' analogy of the internet as 'a series of tubes,' which was ridiculed but has parallels to this real-world technology.

## Key Points
- The United States operated an underground pneumatic tube network for mail delivery between 1893 and 1953.
- Mail could be transported through these tubes at speeds of up to 35 miles per hour.
- Senator Ted Stevens' comparison of the internet to 'a series of tubes' may have been influenced by historical pneumatic systems.
- Pneumatic tubes represented an early form of rapid, automated delivery infrastructure.
- The system was eventually replaced by more modern communication and delivery methods.
