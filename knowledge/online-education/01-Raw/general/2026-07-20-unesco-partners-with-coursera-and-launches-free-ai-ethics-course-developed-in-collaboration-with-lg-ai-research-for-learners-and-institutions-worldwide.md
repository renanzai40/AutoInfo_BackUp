## Original Content
<p>Today, I’m proud to welcome UNESCO, the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, to Coursera as they launch their first free course, the Global MOOC on the Ethics of AI, which they built with LG AI Research.</p>
<p>The post <a href="https://blog.coursera.org/unesco-partners-with-coursera-and-launches-free-ai-ethics-course/">UNESCO partners with Coursera and launches free AI ethics course, developed in collaboration with LG AI Research, for learners and institutions worldwide</a> appeared first on <a href="https://blog.coursera.org">Coursera Blog</a>.</p>

## Summary
UNESCO has partnered with Coursera to launch a free global MOOC on AI ethics, developed in collaboration with LG AI Research. This course is designed to educate learners and institutions worldwide on the ethical aspects of artificial intelligence.

## Key Points
- UNESCO launches its first free course on Coursera focused on AI ethics.
- The course is titled 'Global MOOC on the Ethics of AI'.
- Developed in collaboration with LG AI Research.
- Available globally for free to learners and institutions.
