## Summary
Google has expanded its AI Professional Certificate on Coursera with a new course on 'vibe coding' to help professionals become AI creators. This course enables learners to build AI applications without traditional coding skills, shifting focus from using AI to creating AI-powered software.

## Key Points
- Google launches a new vibe coding course as part of its AI Professional Certificate on Coursera.
- Vibe coding allows non-coders to build AI applications, making AI development more accessible.
- The course aims to transition learners from AI users to AI creators.
- It focuses on automating everyday tasks through AI-powered software across industries.
