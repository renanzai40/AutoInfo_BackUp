## Summary
Faculty at Texas A&M Victoria are quitting due to a new policy requiring them to be on campus five days a week, which is a response to a Texas law restricting remote work. The Texas AAUP reports that this has caused chaos and emotional distress among faculty.

## Key Points
- The policy is mandated by a Texas law limiting remote work for public university faculty.
- Faculty are required to be on campus five days a week under the new policy.
- Several faculty members have quit in response to the policy.
- The Texas AAUP describes the situation as causing chaos and tears.
