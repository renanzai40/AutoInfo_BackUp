## Original Content
A pioneering AI scientist once predicted computers would replace human radiologists. They haven't.

## Summary
The article highlights that a pioneering AI scientist's prediction about computers replacing radiologists has not occurred. Instead, AI is expected to dramatically transform the roles of radiologists, focusing on augmentation rather than full replacement.

## Key Points
- AI predictions of replacing radiologists have not materialized.
- AI technology will significantly alter the jobs of radiologists.
- The impact of AI in radiology is transformative, not substitutive.
- Medical imaging practices are evolving with AI integration.
