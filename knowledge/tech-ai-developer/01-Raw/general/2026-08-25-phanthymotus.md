## Original Content
近日，范式正式举办 PhanthyMotus 生态社区共建计划发布会，宣布其首个通用具身Agent底座从“开源”迈入“多方共建”新阶段。

## Summary
Paradigm recently held a launch conference for the PhanthyMotus ecosystem community co-building plan. They announced that their first general-purpose embodied Agent base is transitioning from an open-source model to a multi-party co-building approach, involving partners like UBTECH and other embodied technology companies.

## Key Points
- Launch of the PhanthyMotus ecosystem community co-building plan by Paradigm.
- Transition of the embodied Agent base from open-source to multi-party co-building.
- Collaboration with UBTECH and over a dozen other embodied technology giants.
- Marking a new stage in the development of general-purpose embodied agents.
- Emphasis on community-driven innovation in embodied AI technologies.
