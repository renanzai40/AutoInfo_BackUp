## Original Content
Young employment in AI-impacted fields down 19% compared to more AI-resistant occupations.

## Summary
A Stanford study reveals that AI is disproportionately affecting entry-level jobs, with a 19% decrease in employment for young workers in AI-impacted fields compared to more AI-resistant occupations. This highlights a significant impact on early-career professionals in sectors vulnerable to automation.

## Key Points
- AI has a greater adverse effect on entry-level positions than other job levels.
- Young employment in AI-impacted fields has declined by 19%.
- AI-resistant occupations experience less impact on employment.
- The study, conducted by Stanford, underscores the need for attention to workforce transitions in the AI era.
