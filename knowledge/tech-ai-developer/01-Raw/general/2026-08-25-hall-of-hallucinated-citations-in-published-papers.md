## Summary
The article addresses the problem of hallucinated citations in published research papers, which are fabricated or non-existent references that can compromise academic integrity. It is presented by Veruscite, a platform dedicated to identifying and exposing such citations to improve research quality.

## Key Points
- Hallucinated citations are made-up references that do not correspond to real sources.
- They appear in published papers, potentially eroding trust in research findings.
- Veruscite is a website that catalogs and discusses these citation issues.
- This phenomenon can affect multiple fields, including medical research.
- Awareness and detection of hallucinated citations are essential for upholding scientific standards.
