## Summary
The article discusses how Bun, a JavaScript runtime, completed a rapid code rewrite in Rust using AI in just 11 days, a task that would typically take a year, at a cost of $165K in tokens. It also covers the increasing competition in coding AI models and ongoing issues with AI-generated fake candidates in hiring, particularly from North Korea.

## Key Points
- AI can drastically accelerate software development, as seen in Bun's 11-day rewrite.
- AI-driven projects can incur significant costs, such as $165K in computational tokens.
- Competition among coding AI tools (LLM 'wars') is intensifying.
- Hiring processes face challenges from AI fakers, notably from North Korea.
