## Original Content
More companies are rolling out services to speed up shipping and delivery as the costs to deploy such systems comes down.

## Summary
The article discusses how companies are increasingly investing in same-day shipping services. This trend is driven by the decreasing costs of deploying systems to speed up shipping and delivery. It reflects a broader shift towards faster logistics solutions in the e-commerce sector.

## Key Points
- Companies are rolling out services to enable same-day shipping.
- The cost to deploy systems for faster delivery is decreasing.
- This is leading to a growing emphasis on speed in shipping and logistics.
