The IAB is developing a framework to solve a big challenge in advertising: how to measure and assign credit when ads influence AI agents.

## Summary
The Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) is developing a framework to address the challenge of measuring and assigning credit for advertisements that influence AI agents. This initiative aims to improve accountability and effectiveness in AI-driven advertising measurement.

## Key Points
- IAB is creating a framework specifically for AI advertising measurement.
- The framework focuses on solving the problem of measuring ad influence on AI agents.
- It seeks to accurately assign credit in scenarios where ads impact AI-driven decisions.
- This could enhance transparency and metrics in digital advertising.
- The initiative responds to emerging challenges in advertising with AI technology.
