## Original Content
Marketers want more than customers. They're chasing fandom to turn audiences into communities.

## Summary
Marketers are chasing fandoms to transform audiences into communities, aiming for deeper engagement beyond traditional customer relationships. This strategy leverages shared interests to build brand loyalty and foster active participation.

## Key Points
- Marketers seek to turn audiences into communities by focusing on fandoms.
- Fandoms represent a shift from transactional to relational marketing approaches.
- Building communities enhances customer loyalty and long-term brand growth.
