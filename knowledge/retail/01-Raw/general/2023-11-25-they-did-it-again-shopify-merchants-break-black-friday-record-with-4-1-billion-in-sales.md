## Summary

## Key Points
- Shopify merchants achieved $4.1 billion in Black Friday sales, a record high.
- The sales figure represents a substantial increase from previous years.
- The record highlights the growing role of e-commerce platforms in holiday shopping.
- Consumer demand remained robust despite economic uncertainties.
