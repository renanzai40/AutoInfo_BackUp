## Original Content
One of the common themes of Modern Retail's recent Leaders Dinner was just how much retail today is a battle of inches. Margins are being eroded little by little by third-party fees, tariffs, increased operations costs and a thousand other small things.

## Summary
Retail brands are experiencing a gradual erosion of profit margins due to cumulative pressures such as supply chain issues, material costs, third-party fees, tariffs, and increased operational expenses. The industry is described as a 'battle of inches,' where small cost factors add up to significant financial challenges.

## Key Points
- Profit margins in retail are under threat from multiple incremental cost pressures.
- Key contributors to margin erosion include third-party fees, tariffs, and increased operational costs.
- The retail industry is characterized as a 'battle of inches,' emphasizing the cumulative effect of small expenses.
- Supply chain pressures and rising material costs are major factors affecting brand profitability.
