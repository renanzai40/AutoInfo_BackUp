## Summary
Procter & Gamble (P&G) has acquired Thorne for $3.8 billion, signaling a continued trend of major consumer packaged goods (CPG) companies investing in science-backed wellness brands. This move reflects shifting consumer priorities towards health and scientifically supported products, driving mergers and acquisitions in the wellness sector. The M&A activity in this area shows no signs of slowing down.

## Key Points
- P&G's acquisition of Thorne for $3.8 billion exemplifies the ongoing hot streak in wellness M&A.
- Large CPG conglomerates are prioritizing science-backed, high-growth brands to meet evolving consumer demands.
- Consumer interest in wellness and scientifically credible products is fueling investment and acquisition strategies.
- The wellness M&A market remains robust with no indications of a slowdown.
