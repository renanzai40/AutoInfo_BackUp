## Summary
In the first half of the year, prestige and mass beauty retail sales in the U.S. increased, with fragrance playing a key role as consumers seek both emotional and functional value from beauty products. This trend underscores the resilience of the beauty category, according to market data from Circana.

## Key Points
- U.S. prestige and mass beauty retail sales saw growth in the first half of the year
- Fragrance is a primary driver of beauty sales increases
- Consumer demand is focused on emotional and functional value in beauty products
- Market research from Circana indicates category resilience
