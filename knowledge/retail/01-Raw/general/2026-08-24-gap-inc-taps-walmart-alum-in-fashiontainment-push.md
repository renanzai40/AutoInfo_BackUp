## Summary
Gap Inc. has hired Justin Breton, a former Walmart employee, as vice president of development. He will report to Pam Kaufman, the entertainment chief, as part of the company's strategic push into 'fashiontainment,' which integrates fashion with entertainment.

## Key Points
- Gap Inc. is advancing into 'fashiontainment' to blend fashion and entertainment.
- Justin Breton is recruited from Walmart to lead development as VP.
- Pam Kaufman, as entertainment chief, will oversee Breton's role.
