## Original Content
Dick's Sporting Goods says it remains bullish on its Foot Locker business, even as it lowered its yearly guidance for the segment, citing "challenging conditions" in the athletic footwear market.

## Summary
Dick's Sporting Goods remains optimistic about its Foot Locker business despite cutting its annual forecast due to tough conditions in the athletic footwear market. The company notes that it is still early in the turnaround for Foot Locker.

## Key Points
- Dick's Sporting Goods is bullish on its Foot Locker segment
- Yearly guidance for Foot Locker has been lowered
- Challenging conditions exist in the athletic footwear market
- The turnaround of Foot Locker is still in its early stages
