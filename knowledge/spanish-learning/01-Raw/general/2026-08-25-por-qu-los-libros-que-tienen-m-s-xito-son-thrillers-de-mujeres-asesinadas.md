## Summary
The article explores why thriller novels featuring murdered women are commercially successful. It examines the fine line between reading such violence to understand personal fears and the risk of repetition normalizing structural machismo. This raises concerns about the societal impact of violent narratives in popular literature.

## Key Points
- Thriller books with murdered women are highly successful in the market.
- Reading about violence can help individuals understand and process their fears.
- Repeated exposure to violent narratives may normalize structural machismo.
- There is a balance between the educational value and potential harm of these stories.
- The article highlights broader societal debates on gender violence in fiction.
