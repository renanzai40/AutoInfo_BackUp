## Summary
Europe has authorized a new prosthesis called PRIMA for individuals with macular degeneration. This system uses a photovoltaic implant under the retina, combined with camera-equipped glasses and an external processor, to restore the ability to read or distinguish objects.

## Key Points
- Europe has approved the PRIMA prosthesis for treating macular degeneration.
- PRIMA involves a photovoltaic implant placed under the retina.
- It pairs with glasses equipped with a camera and an external processor.
- The system aims to restore visual functionality like reading or object recognition.
