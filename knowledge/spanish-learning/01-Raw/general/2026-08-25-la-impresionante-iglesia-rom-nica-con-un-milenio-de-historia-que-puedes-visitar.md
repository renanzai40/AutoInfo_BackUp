## Summary
An ancient Romanesque church with over 1,000 years of history, hidden in the Pyrenees mountains, has recently reopened to visitors. The temple, which had been concealed for centuries, opened its doors again this summer.

## Key Points
- The church is Romanesque and dates back over a millennium.
- It is located in the peaks of the Pyrenees mountains.
- The church had been hidden for more than 1,000 years.
- It has reopened to the public this summer.
