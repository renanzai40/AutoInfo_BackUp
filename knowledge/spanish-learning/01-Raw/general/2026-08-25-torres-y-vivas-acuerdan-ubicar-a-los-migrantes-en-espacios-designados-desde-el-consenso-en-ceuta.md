## Original Content
El ministro ha anunciado que este miércoles presidirá la primera reunión del Comité de Situación para la gestión de la crisis en la ciudad autónoma.

## Summary
Torres and Vivas have agreed to place migrants in designated spaces in Ceuta through a consensus-based approach. The minister announced that he will preside over the first meeting of the Situation Committee on Wednesday to manage the crisis in the autonomous city. This initiative aims to address the migrant crisis with coordinated efforts.

## Key Points
- Torres and Vivas agree on migrant placement in designated spaces by consensus.
- Minister will lead the first Situation Committee meeting on Wednesday.
- Meeting focuses on crisis management in Ceuta.
- Emphasis on consensus for decision-making in handling the migrant situation.
