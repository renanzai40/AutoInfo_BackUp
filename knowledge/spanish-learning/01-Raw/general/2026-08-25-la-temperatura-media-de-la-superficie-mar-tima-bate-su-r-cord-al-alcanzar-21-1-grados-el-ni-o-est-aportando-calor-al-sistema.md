## Summary
The average sea surface temperature has reached a record high of 21.1 degrees, with El Niño contributing to the warming. Samantha Burgess from the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts states this record is a clear sign of increasing stress on the oceans.

## Key Points
- Record average sea surface temperature of 21.1 degrees has been achieved.
- El Niño is identified as a primary factor adding heat to the ocean system.
- The record indicates ongoing and escalating stress on the oceans due to climate factors.
- Data from the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts supports the findings.
