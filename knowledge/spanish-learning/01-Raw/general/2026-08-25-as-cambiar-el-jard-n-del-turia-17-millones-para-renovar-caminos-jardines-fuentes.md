## Summary
The article describes a renovation plan for the Turia Garden, with 17 million allocated to upgrade paths, gardens, and fountains. The project will transform sections between Pont de les Flors and Pont de l’Àngel Custodi, including a garden designed by Ricardo Bofill, while preserving its original layout.

- The garden designed by Ricardo Bofill will preserve its original layout.
- Improvements will include paths, gardens, and fountains.
- The project aims to transform the park infrastructure.
