## Summary
Austria has implemented a policy that sets a speed limit of 100 km/h for combustion vehicles on high-capacity roads, while allowing electric vehicles to drive at 130 km/h. The measure is aimed at reducing emissions from fossil fuel-powered vehicles to address environmental concerns.

## Key Points
- Austria imposes a 100 km/h speed limit for combustion vehicles on high-capacity roads.
- Electric vehicles are permitted to drive at 130 km/h under the same conditions.
- The policy differentiates speed limits based on vehicle emissions to promote environmental protection.
