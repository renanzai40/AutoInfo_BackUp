## Original Content
Una propuesta del Real Automóvil Club británico (RAC) para el Reino Unido simplificaría enormemente el paso del automático al manual para los conductores con esta tipología de carnet.

## Summary
The British Royal Automobile Club (RAC) has proposed a change in the United Kingdom to allow drivers with automatic car licenses to drive manual cars after just one hour of practice. This aims to simplify the transition between license types for drivers.

## Key Points
- The RAC proposal seeks to streamline the process for automatic license holders to drive manual vehicles in the UK.
- Only one hour of practice would be required for such drivers to operate manual cars.
- The initiative is designed to make the licensing system more efficient and accessible.
- It addresses the current barriers in transitioning from automatic to manual car licenses.
