## Summary
The article discusses how society confuses trivial internet memes with significant historical figures like Clara Campoamor, using histrionism as a platform for success in a culture termed 'burdocracia'. It critiques the superficiality and misunderstandings in contemporary social dynamics.

- The concept of 'burdocracia' highlights a system where superficiality dominates.
- Historical figures like Clara Campoamor are misrepresented in popular culture.
