## Summary
The article reports that the bank delinquency rate in Spain has reached a new low since the summer of 2008, despite an increase in interest rates. Specifically, the doubtful credit rate was 2.54% in June, which is about 0.4 percentage points lower than the previous year.

## Key Points
- Bank delinquency rate hit a new minimum since summer 2008.
- The doubtful credit rate stood at 2.54% in June.
- The rate decreased by nearly four tenths of a percentage point year-over-year.
- This decrease occurred despite rising interest rates.
