## Summary
Iván Armesto first gained attention by finishing third in the first season of the reality TV show 'Gran Hermano'. He has since transitioned into the film industry, collaborating with renowned directors Pedro Almodóvar and Alejandro Amenábar in movies such as 'Dolor y gloria' and 'El orfanato'.

## Key Points

- He used his exposure from the reality show to advance his career in entertainment.
- He has worked with prominent Spanish directors Pedro Almodóvar and Alejandro Amenábar.
- His film contributions include 'Dolor y gloria' (Pain and Glory) and 'El orfanato' (The Orphanage).
