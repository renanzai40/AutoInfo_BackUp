## Summary
Spain completes diplomatic normalization with Syria by appointing its first ambassador since 2012. The ambassador is the former special envoy, named after Al Shara overthrew the Al Assad regime in 2024.

## Key Points
- Spain normalizes diplomatic relations with Syria through ambassador appointment.
- First ambassador appointed since 2012.
- Appointment follows the overthrow of Al Assad regime by Al Shara in 2024.
- The appointed ambassador was previously the special envoy to Syria.
