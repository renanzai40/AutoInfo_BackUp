## Summary
Netflix is considering integrating other streaming platforms into its own application, similar to Prime Video's model. This would allow users to subscribe to and access third-party services without leaving the Netflix app, acting as a centralized hub for streaming content.

## Key Points
- Netflix plans to integrate third-party streaming services into its application, inspired by Prime Video.
- Users would be able to access these services directly within Netflix without switching apps.
- Netflix could serve as a showcase for other streaming platforms.
- This strategy aims to enhance user convenience and potentially change streaming consumption habits.
