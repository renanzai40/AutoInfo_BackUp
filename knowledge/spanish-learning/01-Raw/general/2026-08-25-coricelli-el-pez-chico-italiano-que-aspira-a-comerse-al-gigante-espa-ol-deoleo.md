## Summary
Coricelli, an Italian family company founded in 1939, is making a bid to acquire the Spanish company Deoleo with an offer of up to 500 million euros. The company had revenues of around 400 million euros last year.

## Key Points
- Coricelli is a third-generation Italian family company founded in 1939.
- It reported revenues of approximately 400 million euros in the previous year.
- It is pursuing an acquisition of Deoleo, described as a Spanish giant in the industry.
- The offer for Deoleo is valued at up to 500 million euros.
