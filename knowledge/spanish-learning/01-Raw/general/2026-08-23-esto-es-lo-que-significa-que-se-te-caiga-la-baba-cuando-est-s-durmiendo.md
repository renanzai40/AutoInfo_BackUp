## Original Content
Que se caiga la baba al dormir es algo habitual, aunque puede ser señal de obstrucción de nariz o boca o apnea del sueño.

## Summary
Drooling while sleeping is a common occurrence, but it can be a sign of nasal or oral obstructions or sleep apnea. While often harmless, persistent drooling may indicate underlying health issues that require medical evaluation.

## Key Points
- Drooling during sleep is generally common and not always a cause for concern.
- It can signal nasal obstruction, such as from allergies or congestion.
- It may indicate oral obstruction, possibly due to dental or jaw issues.
- Drooling can be associated with sleep apnea, a condition where breathing repeatedly stops and starts during sleep.
