## Summary
The article addresses myths and realities in the diet of captive snakes, emphasizing that offering milk or minced meat can be fatal. This is due to snakes' inability to digest lactose, which poses a serious health risk.

## Key Points
- Feeding milk to snakes can be fatal because they lack lactose digestion capabilities.
- Minced meat is highlighted as another potentially harmful dietary option for snakes.
- Captive snake diets must align with their natural digestive physiology to prevent health issues.
- Misinformation about reptile feeding can lead to severe consequences in animal care.
