## Summary
Cannabis use is associated with an earlier onset of psychosis. This conclusion is based on a comprehensive review of 149 studies involving over 71,000 individuals.

## Key Points
- Cannabis consumption is linked to an earlier appearance of psychosis.
- The findings are derived from a large-scale review of 149 studies.
- The research included a substantial sample size of more than 71,000 people.
- This association suggests potential implications for mental health and public health strategies.
