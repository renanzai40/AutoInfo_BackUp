## Summary
An outbreak of salmonellosis affected at least 32 people at a bar in Eibar, traced to eggs from Aragón. The investigation revealed deficiencies in egg traceability, utensil hygiene, food storage at room temperature, and cross-contamination prevention.

## Key Points
- At least 32 individuals were affected by the salmonellosis outbreak.
- The source was traced to eggs from the Aragón region.
- Deficiencies were found in egg traceability and food safety practices.
- Issues included improper cleaning and disinfection of utensils.
- Improper storage of tortillas at room temperature and lack of cross-contamination control were identified.
