## Original Content
La vida rara vez sigue una línea recta, y muchas veces los desvíos que tanto nos frustran terminan llevándonos a lugares que nunca habríamos alcanzado por el camino previsto.

## Summary
This article presents a fable about the unpredictability of life, suggesting that what seems like bad luck can lead to unforeseen positive outcomes. It emphasizes that life's detours, though frustrating, often take us to places we would not have reached by the predetermined path.

## Key Points
- Life rarely follows a straight line and involves many unexpected detours.
- Frustrations from deviations can lead to positive and unforeseen destinations.
- We cannot always distinguish between good and bad luck in advance.
- Alternative paths may lead to better outcomes than the original plan.
