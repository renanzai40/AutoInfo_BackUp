## Original Content
Desde el organismo dirigido por Pere Navarro se llevó a cabo una campaña de vigilancia y control de velocidad entre los días 3 y 9 de agosto con el objetivo de detectar infracciones.

## Summary
The DGT issued over 69,000 speeding tickets during a one-week campaign in early August. The campaign, conducted from August 3rd to 9th under Pere Navarro's direction, aimed to detect speed infractions.

## Key Points
- Over 69,000 speeding tickets were issued in one week
- The campaign was held by DGT from August 3rd to 9th
- Objective was to detect speed infractions
- Pere Navarro directed the campaign
