## Summary
Barcelona will completely rehabilitate the old La Escocesa factory in Poblenou, converting it into a mixed-use development with 72 homes and spaces for social economy, artistic creation, and food innovation. The project requires an investment of 51.8 million euros.

## Key Points
- The project involves full rehabilitation of the La Escocesa factory.
- 72 residential units will be constructed.
- Spaces for social economy, artistic creation, and food innovation are included.
- The investment totals 51.8 million euros.
