## Summary
Hublot, a luxury watch company, documented an 18-day research project at the Antikythera archaeological site using drones and advanced technology to study the Antikythera mechanism, considered the first astronomical computer in history.

## Key Points
- Hublot conducted extensive research on the Antikythera mechanism for 18 days.
- The study employed pioneering technologies such as drones for documentation.
- The Antikythera mechanism is recognized as the earliest known astronomical computer.
- The research was performed at the Antikythera site, a key archaeological location.
