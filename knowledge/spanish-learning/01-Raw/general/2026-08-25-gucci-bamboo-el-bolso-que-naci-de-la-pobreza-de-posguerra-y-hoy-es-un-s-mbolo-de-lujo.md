## Summary
The Gucci Bamboo bag was introduced in 1947, created during postwar poverty with a bamboo cane handle as its key feature. It has evolved into a symbol of luxury and fashion.

## Key Points
- The bag was first sold in 1947.
- It originated from postwar poverty constraints.
- The bamboo cane handle is its iconic design element.
- It is now recognized as a luxury symbol.
