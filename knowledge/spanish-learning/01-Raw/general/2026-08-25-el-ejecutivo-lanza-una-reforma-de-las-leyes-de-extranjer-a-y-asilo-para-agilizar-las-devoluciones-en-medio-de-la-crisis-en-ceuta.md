## Original Content
El Consejo de Ministros ha dado luz verde a una reforma para adaptar el marco normativo español al nuevo pacto europeo de migración y asilo, que todavía debe volver a ratificarse antes de iniciar su tramitación parlamentaria.

## Summary
The Spanish government has approved a reform to update immigration and asylum laws to align with the new European pact on migration and asylum. This measure aims to expedite deportation processes in response to the crisis in Ceuta. The reform still requires European ratification and parliamentary approval.

## Key Points
- The Council of Ministers has given approval to reform Spanish immigration and asylum laws.
- The reform adapts Spanish laws to comply with the European pact on migration and asylum.
- The European pact requires ratification before parliamentary processing can begin.
- The reform is intended to speed up returns (deportations) amid the ongoing crisis in Ceuta.
- This action is part of broader efforts to manage migration at the national and European levels.
