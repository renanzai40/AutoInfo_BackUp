## Original Content
La Generalitat levantó el lunes desde las 14.30 horas el confinamiento de los vecinos de Gátova, que permanecían en sus domicilios a causa del fuego.

## Summary
A forest fire in Segorbe burned over 630 hectares in five days before being controlled. Authorities lifted a confinement order for residents of Gátova who were sheltering at home due to the fire.

## Key Points
- The forest fire was controlled after burning more than 630 hectares.
- The fire lasted for five days.
- Confinement order for Gátova residents was lifted by Generalitat.
- The fire affected the area of Segorbe in Spain.
