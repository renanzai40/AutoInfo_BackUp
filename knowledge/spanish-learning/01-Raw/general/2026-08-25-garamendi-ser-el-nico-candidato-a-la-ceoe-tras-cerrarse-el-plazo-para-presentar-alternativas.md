## Original Content
El comité ejecutivo de la CEOE aprobó en 2023 la supresión de la limitación a dos mandatos consecutivos de cuatro años para el presidente de la patronal, organización que Antonio Garamendi dirige desde 2018.

## Summary
Antonio Garamendi is set to remain the only candidate for the presidency of the CEOE after the deadline for presenting alternatives has closed. The CEOE executive committee approved in 2023 the removal of term limits for its president, a position Garamendi has held since 2018.

## Key Points
- Garamendi is the sole candidate for CEOE leadership after the deadline closure.
- CEOE eliminated presidential term limits in 2023.
- Garamendi has been leading CEOE since 2018.
- This change allows for potential indefinite tenure in the role.
