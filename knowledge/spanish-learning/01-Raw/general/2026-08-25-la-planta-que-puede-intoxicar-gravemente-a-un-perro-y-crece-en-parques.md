## Original Content
Las semillas del ricino contienen ricina, una de las toxinas vegetales más potentes, y requieren atención veterinaria inmediata tras su ingestión.

## Summary
The article discusses the castor plant, which grows in parks and has seeds containing ricin, a highly potent toxin. Ingestion of these seeds can cause severe poisoning in dogs, requiring immediate veterinary intervention.

## Key Points
- Castor seeds contain ricin, one of the most potent plant toxins.
- Ingestion of castor seeds demands immediate veterinary attention to prevent serious harm.
- The castor plant is commonly found in parks, posing a risk to dogs.
- Ricin toxicity can lead to grave intoxication in animals.
- Awareness and quick action are crucial in cases of ingestion.
