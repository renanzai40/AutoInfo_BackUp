## Summary
An Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has resulted in over 1,200 recoveries but has already caused 2,680 deaths. The virus is spreading across multiple provinces, including Bas-Uélé, North Kivu, and South Kivu, although South Kivu has been free of new cases for 83 days.

## Key Points
- Over 1,200 people have recovered from Ebola.
- The outbreak has caused 2,680 deaths.
- The virus is affecting several provinces in DR Congo.
- South Kivu has had no new Ebola cases for 83 days.
- The situation underscores the ongoing public health crisis.
