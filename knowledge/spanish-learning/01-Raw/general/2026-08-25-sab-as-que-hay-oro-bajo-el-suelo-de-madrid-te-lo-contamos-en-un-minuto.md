## Original Content
La cámara acorazada del Banco de España (BdE) alberga unas 280 toneladas de oro, concentrando el grueso de las reservas del Estado español.

## Summary
The article reveals that the Bank of Spain's armored chamber in Madrid houses approximately 280 tons of gold, which constitutes the majority of Spain's national reserves. It highlights the existence of this substantial gold reserve located underground in Madrid.

## Key Points
- A large gold reserve is located under the city of Madrid, Spain.
- The gold is stored in the armored chamber of the Bank of Spain (BdE).
- The reserve weighs about 280 tons and represents the bulk of Spain's state reserves.
