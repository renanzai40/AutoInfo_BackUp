## Summary
UNESCO has declared 25 new sites as World Heritage, encompassing a variety of architectural and natural landmarks. The new designations include theaters in the Amazon with European architecture, modernist buildings in Uzbekistan, and Greece's highest mountain. This highlights the global effort to preserve cultural and natural heritage.

## Key Points
- 25 new sites were declared World Heritage by UNESCO.
- The sites represent diverse cultural and natural elements from around the world.
- Examples include theaters in the Amazon, modernist buildings in Uzbekistan, and a prominent mountain in Greece.
- This addition underscores the importance of heritage conservation.
