## Original Content
La pensión media de jubilación, que perciben más de dos tercios de los pensionistas, llegó 1.575 euros mensuales, un 4,5% más que en agosto de hace un año.

## Summary
In August, pension spending in Spain reached a new high, surpassing 14.470 million euros. The average retirement pension for more than two-thirds of pensioners increased to 1,575 euros per month, marking a 4.5% rise compared to the previous year.

## Key Points
- Pension expenditure hit a record maximum in August.
- Total pension spending exceeded 14.470 million euros.
- The average retirement pension is 1,575 euros monthly.
- This represents a 4.5% year-on-year increase.
- More than two-thirds of pensioners receive this average pension.
