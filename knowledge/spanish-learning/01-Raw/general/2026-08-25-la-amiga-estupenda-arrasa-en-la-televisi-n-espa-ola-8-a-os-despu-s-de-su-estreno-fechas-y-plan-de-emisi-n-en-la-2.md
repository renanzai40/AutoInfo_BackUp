The Italian TV show 'La amiga estupenda' is returning to Spanish public television, La 2, eight years after its original release on HBO in 2018. It will be broadcast three times a week as part of its emission plan, marking a significant comeback for the series.

- The broadcast schedule includes triple weekly emissions, meaning three times a week.
