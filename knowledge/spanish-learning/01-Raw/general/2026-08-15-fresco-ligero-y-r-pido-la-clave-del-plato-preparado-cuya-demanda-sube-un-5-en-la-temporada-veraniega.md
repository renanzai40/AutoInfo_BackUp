La Asociación de Platos Preparados de España asegura que la razón por la cual estos platos preparados ganan popularidad en esta época del año es por su "conveniencia".

## Summary
Demand for prepared dishes in Spain increases by 5% during the summer season, driven by their convenience. The Association of Prepared Dishes of Spain highlights that these dishes are fresh, light, and fast, contributing to their popularity.

## Key Points
- Demand for prepared dishes in Spain rises by 5% in summer.
- Convenience is the main reason for this increase, as stated by the Association of Prepared Dishes of Spain.
- Prepared dishes are characterized as fresh, light, and fast.
- This trend is seasonal, specifically occurring in the summer.
