## Summary
Amazon Prime Video is releasing a new movie in August that features a summer romance intertwined with chronic illness, set in the scenic coves of Mallorca. The film is promoted as a more mature and less toxic love story compared to the creator's previous work 'After'.

- It contrasts with the creator's past work 'After' by presenting healthier relationships.
- The promotion aims to evoke emotional engagement from the audience.
