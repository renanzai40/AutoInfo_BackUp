## Summary
Trump proposes renaming Lake Ontario to 'Lake America' amid escalating trade tensions with Canada. This comes after the US imposed 50% tariffs on Canadian automobiles and steel, symbolizing political and commercial disputes.

## Key Points
- Trump suggests changing Lake Ontario's name to 'Lake America' as a symbolic gesture.
- The proposal occurs during a trade escalation between the US and Canada.
- Washington announced 50% tariffs on Canadian automobiles and steel.
- The naming idea reflects broader political tensions in North America.
