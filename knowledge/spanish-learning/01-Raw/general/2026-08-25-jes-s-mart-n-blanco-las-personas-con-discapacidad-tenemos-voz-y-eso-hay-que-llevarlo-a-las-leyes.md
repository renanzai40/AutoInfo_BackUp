El director General de Derechos de las Personas con Discapacidad habla de las repercusiones que tendrá la reforma de las leyes de discapacidad y dependencia en casi 10 millones de personas en España.

## Summary
Jesús Martín Blanco, Director General for the Rights of People with Disabilities, discusses the impact of reforms to Spain's disability and dependency laws. The changes are expected to affect nearly 10 million people, emphasizing the need to include the voices of disabled individuals in legal frameworks.

## Key Points
- Spain is reforming its disability and dependency laws.
- The reforms will impact approximately 10 million people.
- There is a call to integrate the perspectives of people with disabilities into legislation.
- The article highlights the importance of legal recognition for disabled individuals' rights.
