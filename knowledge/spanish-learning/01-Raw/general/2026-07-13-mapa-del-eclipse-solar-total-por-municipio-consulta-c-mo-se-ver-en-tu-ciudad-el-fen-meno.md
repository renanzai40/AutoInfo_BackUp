## Summary
Spain is preparing for a total solar eclipse, with an interactive map created using data from the National Geographic Institute to show visibility across municipalities. The map allows users to see how the eclipse will appear from their specific location. This event is described as an astronomical phenomenon of the century.

## Key Points
- Spain is gearing up for a total solar eclipse, a major astronomical event.
- An interactive map is available to display the eclipse's visibility in different municipalities.
- The map is based on data from the Instituto Geográfico Nacional.
- The tool enables individuals to understand the phenomenon from their own cities.
