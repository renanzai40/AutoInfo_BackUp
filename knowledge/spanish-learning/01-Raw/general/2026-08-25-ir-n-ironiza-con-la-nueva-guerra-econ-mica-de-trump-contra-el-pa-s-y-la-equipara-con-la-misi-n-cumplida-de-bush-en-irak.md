## Summary
Iran sarcastically compared the new U.S. economic war under Trump to the premature 'mission accomplished' declaration by George W. Bush regarding the Iraq War. Iran's foreign ministry spokesperson used this historical analogy to mock and question Washington's claims about weakening Tehran.

## Key Points
- Iran's foreign ministry spokesperson, Esmaeil Baqaei, publicly mocked U.S. economic sanctions.
- The comparison draws a parallel between Trump's economic war and Bush's 2003 declaration on Iraq.
- The statement implies criticism of U.S. foreign policy as overly optimistic or premature.
- It highlights ongoing geopolitical tensions between the United States and Iran.
