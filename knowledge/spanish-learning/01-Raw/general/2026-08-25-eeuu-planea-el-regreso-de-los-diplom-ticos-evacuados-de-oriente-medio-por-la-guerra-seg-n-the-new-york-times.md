## Summary
The United States is preparing to return evacuated diplomats to its embassies in the Middle East. This decision indicates that US officials do not foresee a new escalation of the ongoing conflict. The information is reported by 'The New York Times'.

## Key Points
- The US is planning to reintroduce diplomatic staff to several embassies in the Middle East.
- This move is based on the assessment that the conflict is unlikely to escalate further.
- The report originates from 'The New York Times', a major news outlet.
- The decision involves multiple embassies in the region, reflecting a shift in diplomatic posture.
