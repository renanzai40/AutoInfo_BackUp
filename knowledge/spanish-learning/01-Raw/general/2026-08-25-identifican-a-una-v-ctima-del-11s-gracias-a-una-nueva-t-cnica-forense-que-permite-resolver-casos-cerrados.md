## Original Content
Se trata de la víctima identificada número 1.654, la primera empleando la técnica de genealogía genética forense investigativa (FIGG)

## Summary
A 9/11 victim has been identified using a new forensic technique called investigative forensic genetic genealogy (FIGG). This marks the first use of FIGG for such an identification, making it the 1,654th victim identified from the attacks.

## Key Points
- The victim is the 1,654th identified from the 9/11 attacks.
- This is the first application of investigative forensic genetic genealogy (FIGG) in this context.
- FIGG is a new technique that enables solving previously closed forensic cases.
