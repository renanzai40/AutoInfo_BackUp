## Original Content
Muchas frases atribuidas a Gandhi, Einstein, Churchill, Wilde o Twain nunca las dijeron, aunque las redes las repiten como verdades históricas.

## Summary
This article discusses how many famous quotes attributed to historical figures such as Gandhi, Einstein, and Churchill are actually misattributions. It emphasizes that social media often spreads these quotes as historical facts, even though the alleged authors never said them.

## Key Points
- Numerous famous quotes are falsely attributed to well-known personalities.
- Social media platforms perpetuate these misattributions as truths.
- Key figures like Gandhi, Einstein, Churchill, Wilde, and Twain are commonly misrepresented.
- The phenomenon underscores issues with misinformation and lack of verification in digital communication.
