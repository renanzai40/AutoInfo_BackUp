## Original Content
Unos 1.800 mozos han corrido por las calles de la localidad madrileña, que este martes a las 22.00 horas acoge el único encierro nocturno de sus fiestas.

## Summary
The first night bull run of the festivals in San Sebastián de los Reyes resulted in ten injuries, including one person with a reserved prognosis. Approximately 1,800 young people participated in the event, which is part of the local festivities.

## Key Points
- Ten people were injured during the encierro.
- One injury is described as having a reserved prognosis.
- About 1,800 young people participated in the bull run.
- The event was the only night encierro of the festivals.
- It took place in San Sebastián de los Reyes, a locality in Madrid.
