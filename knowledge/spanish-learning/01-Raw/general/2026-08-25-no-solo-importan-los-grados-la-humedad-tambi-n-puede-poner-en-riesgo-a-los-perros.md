## Original Content
Los perros apenas sudan y dependen del jadeo para refrigerarse, un mecanismo que pierde eficacia cuando aumenta la humedad ambiental.

## Summary
Dogs cool themselves primarily through panting, not sweating, and this mechanism becomes less effective in high humidity. This article emphasizes that humidity, along with temperature, is a critical factor in preventing overheating and related health risks in dogs.

## Key Points
- Dogs have limited sweat glands and depend on panting for thermoregulation.
- Increased ambient humidity reduces the efficiency of panting.
- This can lead to a higher risk of heat-related illnesses in dogs.
- Humidity is an important environmental factor to monitor for canine health.
