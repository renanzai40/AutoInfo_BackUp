## Summary
Cookies are crucial for businesses to gain insights into users' online activity, but their regulation is split between the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. This article explores the legal frameworks governing cookie usage and their implications for businesses.

## Key Points
- Cookies provide significant tracking capabilities for monitoring user behavior online.
- Regulations for cookies are divided between the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive.
- Compliance with both frameworks is necessary for businesses using cookies.
