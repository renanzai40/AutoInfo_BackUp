## Original Content
O Chega quer saber quantos acumulam pensões elevadas com salários e cargos nomeados pelo Estado. Caso de ex-ministro Correia de Campos serve de exemplo ao partido.

## Summary
The political party Chega is challenging the Portuguese government to audit the Social Security system to uncover individuals who accumulate high pensions alongside state-appointed salaries and positions. The case of former minister Correia de Campos is highlighted as an example of this issue. This move aims to address concerns about fairness and transparency in public sector benefits.

## Key Points
- Chega demands a government audit of Social Security to investigate pension and salary overlaps.
- The focus is on individuals receiving high pensions while also earning salaries from state roles.
- Former minister Correia de Campos is cited as a specific case to illustrate the problem.
- This issue raises questions about integrity and accountability in government appointments and pension systems.
- The party seeks to promote transparency and prevent potential misuse of public funds.
