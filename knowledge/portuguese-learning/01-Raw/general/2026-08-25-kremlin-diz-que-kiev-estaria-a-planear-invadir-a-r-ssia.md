## Original Content
De acordo com um assessor de Vladimir Putin, Kiev planeava invadir a Rússia antes de Moscovo ter invadido a Ucrânia. Segundo a mesma fonte, a data da operação militar seria 8 de março de 2022.

## Summary
The Kremlin claims that Ukraine was planning to invade Russia prior to Russia's invasion of Ukraine, citing an adviser to Vladimir Putin. The alleged invasion date was set for March 8, 2022. This statement is part of the ongoing geopolitical narrative between the two countries.

## Key Points
- Kremlin asserts that Ukraine had plans to invade Russia before Russia's own invasion.
- The claim is attributed to an adviser to President Vladimir Putin.
- The specified date for the alleged Ukrainian invasion was March 8, 2022.
- The assertion appears in the context of the Russia-Ukraine conflict.
- It highlights conflicting narratives and propaganda in the war.
