## Original Content
Nem soluções como a muito útil travagem de emergência merece grande compreensão e preferência dos condutores

## Summary
Modern cars are equipped with expensive technologies like emergency braking systems that are underutilized by drivers due to lack of understanding and preference. This reveals a significant gap between the availability of advanced automotive features and their adoption by users. The article suggests that even highly useful safety solutions are not fully embraced by drivers.

## Key Points
- Modern vehicles include costly equipment and technologies.
- Few drivers use or comprehend features such as emergency braking.
- There is low driver preference for advanced safety systems in cars.
- This indicates a disconnect between technological advancement and user behavior.
