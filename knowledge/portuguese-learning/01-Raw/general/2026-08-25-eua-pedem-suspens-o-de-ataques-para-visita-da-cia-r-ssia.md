## Original Content
O The Kiev Independent afirma que Kiev concordou em suspender os ataques a Moscovo e a São Petesburgo entre 24 e 26 de agosto durante a presença de "uma delegação de alto escalão" em Moscovo.

## Summary
The US has requested Ukraine to suspend attacks on Moscow and St. Petersburg during a high-level delegation visit to Moscow. According to The Kiev Independent, Kyiv has agreed to this suspension from August 24 to 26.

## Key Points
- The US is asking for a suspension of attacks on Russian cities during a CIA delegation visit.
- Kyiv has agreed to suspend attacks on Moscow and St. Petersburg from August 24 to 26.
- The suspension is due to the presence of a high-level delegation in Moscow.
- This information is reported by The Kiev Independent.
