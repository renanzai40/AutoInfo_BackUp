## Summary
Pedro Almeida Maia is an organizational psychologist born in 1979 in Ponta Delgada, holding a master's degree in Work, Organizational, and Human Resources Psychology from the Universities of Coimbra and Barcelona. His work titled 'A Escrava Açoriana' is scheduled to arrive in the United States in the autumn.
