## Original Content
O governo de Ceuta identificou até agora mais de 2.900 menores migrantes na cidade autónoma espanhola

## Summary
Spain is allocating 25 million euros to assist over 2,900 migrant minors identified in Ceuta, an autonomous Spanish city. The government of Ceuta is responsible for identifying and supporting these minors.

## Key Points
- Spain has allocated 25 million euros for assistance to minors in Ceuta.
- More than 2,900 migrant minors have been identified in Ceuta.
- The government of Ceuta is actively involved in this initiative.
