## Summary
Crime rates have increased in Porto and Lisbon, but decreased in Setúbal according to recent data. The Public Security Police (PSP) is monitoring cases that have caused significant public alarm.

## Key Points
- Crime is rising in Porto and Lisbon.
- Crime is falling in Setúbal.
- The PSP is attentive to high-profile cases causing social alarm.
