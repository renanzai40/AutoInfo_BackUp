## Original Content
O líder do Chega explica que o objetivo é concluir quantas pessoas recebem “pensões milionárias e imorais”. Ainda, Paulo Rangel acusou José Luís Carneiro de não ter capacidade para governar.

## Summary
The leader of the Chega party demands an audit of Social Security before the state budget to investigate individuals receiving large pensions deemed immoral. Additionally, Paulo Rangel accuses José Luís Carneiro of lacking the capacity to govern.

## Key Points
- Chega is calling for an audit of Social Security prior to the state budget.
- The audit aims to identify pensioners with excessively high pensions.
- Paulo Rangel has criticized José Luís Carneiro's ability to govern.
