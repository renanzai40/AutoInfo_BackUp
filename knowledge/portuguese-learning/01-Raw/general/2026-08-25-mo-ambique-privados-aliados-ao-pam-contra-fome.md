A responsável do PAM para a área de emergência, Edna Possolo, disse, no final do encontro com a CTA, que aquele organismo internacional quer agora avançar na criação de resiliência alimentar face aos choques climáticos

## Summary
The World Food Programme (PAM) in Mozambique is partnering with the private sector to address hunger, as announced by Edna Possolo after a meeting with CTA. The initiative aims to build food resilience against climate shocks. This collaboration focuses on improving food security in the region.

## Key Points
- WFP (PAM) is allied with the private sector in Mozambique to combat hunger.
- The program aims to create food resilience in the face of climate shocks.
- Edna Possolo, head of emergency at WFP, made this announcement following a meeting with CTA.
