## Original Content
"O financiamento PRR terminou, ainda estamos a aguardar as medidas nacionais para se lançar uma nova fase de construção de habitação, mas nós já adjudicamos nova construção porque fomos à banca, pedimos empréstimo", afirmou a autarca

## Summary
Matosinhos has completed 512 housing units under the PRR funding, totaling 122 million. The PRR financing has ended, and while awaiting national measures for a new construction phase, the municipality has already initiated new projects by securing a loan from the bank.

## Key Points
- 512 housing units have been completed in Matosinhos with PRR funding.
- The total funding amount is 122 million.
- PRR financing has ended, and national measures for new construction phases are pending.
- New construction projects have been awarded by obtaining a bank loan despite the end of PRR funding.
