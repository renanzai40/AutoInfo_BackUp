## Summary
The article reports that Gaia has approved a network of night guards proposed by the Socialist Party (PS). The socialists are citing examples from other municipalities such as Porto and Lisbon where similar services are active or in revitalization, indicating a focus on enhancing municipal security services.

- The socialists reference successful models from other cities, including Porto, Gondomar, Matosinhos, Amadora, Cascais, Aveiro, and Lisbon.
- These municipalities have services that are either active or undergoing revitalization.
- The decision may improve public safety or municipal management in Gaia.
