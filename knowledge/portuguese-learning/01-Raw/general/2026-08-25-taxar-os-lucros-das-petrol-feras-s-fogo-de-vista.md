## Original Content
O pedido de imposto europeu sobre lucros extraordinários tem eficácia nula na carteira e a receita no passado ficou muito aquém do previsto. A crise em Ceuta expõe a fraqueza do executivo de Madrid.

## Summary
The article argues that a proposed European tax on extraordinary profits from oil companies is ineffective and symbolic, citing past revenues that fell short of expectations. It also links this to the crisis in Ceuta, which highlights the weakness of the Spanish government's executive.

## Key Points
- The European tax on extraordinary profits is seen as ineffective and merely symbolic.
- Past revenues from similar tax measures were significantly lower than forecasted.
- The crisis in Ceuta exposes the fragility and weakness of Madrid's executive leadership.
- The proposal is critiqued as having no real impact on the financial outcomes.
