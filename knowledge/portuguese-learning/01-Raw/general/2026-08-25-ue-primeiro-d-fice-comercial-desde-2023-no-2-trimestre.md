## Original Content
Tanto as exportações como as importações vinham a diminuir desde o segundo trimestre de 2025 na UE, segundo o Eurostat

## Summary
The European Union recorded its first trade deficit since 2023 in the second quarter. Both exports and imports have been decreasing since the second quarter of 2025, according to Eurostat data.

## Key Points
- EU experienced a trade deficit in Q2, marking the first since 2023.
- Exports have been declining since the second quarter of 2025.
- Imports have also been declining since the second quarter of 2025.
- Data is provided by Eurostat, the EU's statistical office.
