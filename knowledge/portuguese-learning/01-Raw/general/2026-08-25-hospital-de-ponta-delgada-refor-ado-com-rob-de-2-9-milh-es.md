## Summary
Hospital de Ponta Delgada has acquired a robot worth 2.9 million euros. Initially, it will be used in otolaryngology, urology, general surgery, and gynecology. The robot's application will expand to other specialties as needed within certified parameters.

## Key Points
- The hospital has been reinforced with a new robot costing 2.9 million euros.
- The robot will initially be used in specific specialties: ENT, urology, general surgery, and gynecology.
- Future use of the robot will be extended to other specialties based on need and certification.
- The details were provided by Luís Bernardo.
