## Summary
Traffic on the IP3 road in Tondela will be diverted via the old EN2 route on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday nights. The diversions affect both directions of the road to accommodate road work or maintenance.

## Key Points
- IP3 road will have traffic diversions on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday nights.
- Traffic will be rerouted using the old EN2 road.
- Diversions apply in both directions on the affected road.
- The location of the disruptions is in Tondela.
