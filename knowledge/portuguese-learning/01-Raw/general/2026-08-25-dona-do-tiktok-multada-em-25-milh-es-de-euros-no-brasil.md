## Summary
The owner of TikTok has been fined 25 million euros in Brazil. The fine is due to the social network automatically applying the most restrictive privacy settings to accounts of minors under 16, which can only be altered with parental authorization.

## Key Points
- TikTok's owner fined 25 million euros in Brazil.
- Automatic restrictive privacy settings implemented for users under 16.
- Parental authorization required to change these settings.
