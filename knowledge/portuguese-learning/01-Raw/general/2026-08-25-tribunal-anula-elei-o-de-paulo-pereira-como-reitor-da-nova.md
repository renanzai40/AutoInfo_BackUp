## Summary
A tribunal has annulled the election of Paulo Pereira as rector of Nova due to detected illegalities in the scheduling of votes. This decision follows a previous order by the same tribunal to repeat the elections in March, and the process has been ongoing since 2025.

## Key Points
- The election of Paulo Pereira as rector of Nova was annulled by a tribunal.
- The annulment is justified by illegalities in the scheduling of votes.
- The tribunal had previously ordered the repetition of the elections in March.
- The entire process has been dragging on since 2025.
