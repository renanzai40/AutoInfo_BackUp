## Original Content
Mark Carney, o primeiro-ministro canadiano, já tinha prometido que o Canadá iria retaliar "dólar por dólar" a eventuais tarifas dos EUA, que se confirmaram.

## Summary
Canada has imposed retaliatory tariffs on imports from the United States in response to confirmed US tariffs. This move fulfills Prime Minister Mark Carney's earlier promise to retaliate dollar for dollar against any US trade measures.

## Key Points
- Canada implements retaliatory tariffs targeting US imports.
- The retaliation is structured to match US tariffs on a dollar-for-dollar basis.
- Prime Minister Mark Carney had pre-committed to this action before the tariffs were confirmed.
- US tariffs on Canadian goods have been officially confirmed, triggering the response.
