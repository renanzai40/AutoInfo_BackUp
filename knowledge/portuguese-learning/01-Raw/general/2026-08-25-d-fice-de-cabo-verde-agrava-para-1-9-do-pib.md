## Original Content
A retificação prevê novas medidas nas áreas económica e social, incluindo a gratuitidade do primeiro ciclo do ensino superior e dos cuidados de saúde, reforços na saúde e na proteção social e compensações à eletricidade e aos combustíveis

## Summary
Cape Verde's budget deficit has worsened to 1.9% of GDP. To address this, the government is implementing new economic and social measures, including free first-cycle higher education and health care, reinforcement in health and social protection, and subsidies for electricity and fuels.

## Key Points
- Cape Verde's deficit increased to 1.9% of GDP.
- New measures include free higher education and health care for the first cycle.
- There are reinforcements in health and social protection systems.
- Subsidies are being provided for electricity and fuels.
