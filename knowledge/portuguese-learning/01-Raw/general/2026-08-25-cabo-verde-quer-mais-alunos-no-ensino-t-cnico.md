## Original Content
O ministro da Educação considerou anormal apenas cerca de 1.400 seguirem a via técnica

## Summary
Cape Verde's Minister of Education expressed concern over the low number of students in technical education, noting that only about 1,400 students are currently enrolled. The minister considers this figure abnormal and is advocating for more students to pursue technical education to address the issue.

## Key Points
- Low enrollment in technical education in Cape Verde, with only about 1,400 students.
- The Minister of Education views this number as abnormal and unsustainable.
- There is a push to increase student participation in technical education programs.
