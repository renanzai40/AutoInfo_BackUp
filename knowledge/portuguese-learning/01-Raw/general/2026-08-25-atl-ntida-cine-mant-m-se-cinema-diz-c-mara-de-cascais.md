## Original Content
O Atlântida Cine não vai ser transformado em auditório multiusos, garante a Câmara de Cascais

## Summary
The Cascais City Council has confirmed that the Atlântida Cine will continue to operate as a cinema and will not be converted into a multi-use auditorium. This decision preserves the venue's original function.

## Key Points
- Atlântida Cine will remain a cinema.
- It will not be transformed into a multi-use auditorium.
- The Cascais City Council has guaranteed this decision.
