## Original Content
As entidades candidatas ao Programa de Apoio a Projetos - Internacionalização foram esta sexta notificadas da decisão final, estando previsto que os projetos apoiados se desenvolvam entre 1 de setembro de 2026 e 28 de fevereiro de 2028

## Summary
The DGArtes program has announced support for 115 international projects. Candidate entities were notified of the final decision this Friday, with projects expected to be developed between September 1, 2026, and February 28, 2028.

## Key Points
- DGArtes program includes 115 international projects.
- Candidate entities received final notification of selection.
- Projects are scheduled to run from September 2026 to February 2028.
