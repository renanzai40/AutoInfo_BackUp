## Summary
The 'É um encontro' festival returns to Amadora in September, focusing on gastronomy as a means to foster social connections and approximate people. It promotes food as a central theme for creating encounters and shared experiences within the community.

- Gastronomy is the core theme, used as a tool for social approximation and bringing people together.
- The event emphasizes the role of food in facilitating human interactions and community building.
