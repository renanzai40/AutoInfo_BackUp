## Summary
Queijadas de Sintra have been awarded a gold medal, recognizing their excellence as a cultural product. The municipality emphasizes their role as a key tourist attraction that promotes Sintra's name and culture worldwide.

## Key Points
- Queijadas de Sintra are distinguished with a gold medal.
- They are described as an unavoidable tourist asset.
- They export Sintra's name to remote geographies as an ambassador of excellence and culture.
