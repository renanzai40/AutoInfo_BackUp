## Summary
United Airlines will launch a flight route from Terceira to Newark starting in 2027. The operation will run from June 9 to September 5, 2027, with three weekly flights using a Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft.

## Key Points
- United Airlines is establishing a new flight connection between Terceira and Newark.
- The flight service is scheduled for the summer period from June 9 to September 5, 2027.
- The route will include three flights per week.
- The aircraft to be used is a Boeing 737 MAX 8.
