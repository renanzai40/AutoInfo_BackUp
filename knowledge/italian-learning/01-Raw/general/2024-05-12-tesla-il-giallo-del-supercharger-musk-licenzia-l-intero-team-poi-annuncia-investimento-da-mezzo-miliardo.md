## Summary
Elon Musk fired Tesla's entire Supercharger team but then announced a $500 million investment to expand the ultra-fast charging network with new sites. The announcement was made on his social media platform X, marking a significant shift in Tesla's infrastructure strategy.

## Key Points
- Tesla CEO Elon Musk dismissed the entire Supercharger team.
- A half-billion dollar investment was announced to enhance the charging network.
- The investment will fund new sites and ultra-fast charging capabilities.
- The news was shared via Musk's post on the X platform.
