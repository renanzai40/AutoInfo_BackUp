## Summary
Alessandro Baricco's debut novel 'Castelli di rabbia' is being released with the Italian newspaper 'Corriere della Sera' on March 22. This marks the second release in a series dedicated to the writer, with the book originally published in 1991 and set in an imaginary 19th-century city. The event is described as a revolutionary journey for Baricco.

## Key Points
- The release of 'Castelli di rabbia' with 'Corriere della Sera' on March 22.
- It is the second release in a series honoring Alessandro Baricco.
- The novel is Baricco's debut, first published in 1991.
- Set in an imaginary 19th-century city.
