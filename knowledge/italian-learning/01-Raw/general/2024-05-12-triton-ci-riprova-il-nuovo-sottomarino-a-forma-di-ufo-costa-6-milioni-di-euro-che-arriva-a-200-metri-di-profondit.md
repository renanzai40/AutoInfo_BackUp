## Summary
Triton has launched a new UFO-shaped submarine, the 660 AVA, priced at 6 million euros, capable of reaching depths of 200 meters. It can accommodate up to 8 passengers and operate for up to 12 hours without a bathroom, following a fatal incident in 2019.

- Triton launches the 660 AVA submarine
- It is UFO-shaped and costs 6 million euros
- Can reach 200 meters depth and hold 8 passengers
- Operates for 12 hours without a bathroom
- Launch follows a 2019 fatal incident
