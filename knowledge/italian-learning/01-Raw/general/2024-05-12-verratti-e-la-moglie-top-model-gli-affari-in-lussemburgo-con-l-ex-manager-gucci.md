## Summary
The article reports that footballer Marco Verratti and his model wife Jessica Aidi have established business affairs in Luxembourg with former Gucci manager Francesco Giliberti Birindelli. This arrangement is allegedly designed to evade taxes in Italy, highlighting the manager's expertise in financial strategies. The content repeatedly emphasizes the tax avoidance aspect, referred to as 'Il dribbling al Fisco'.

## Key Points
- Marco Verratti and Jessica Aidi are involved in business activities in Luxembourg.

- The setup aims to evade Italian taxes, as indicated by the phrase 'Il dribbling al Fisco'.
- The manager specializes in financial planning for high-net-worth individuals to avoid taxes.
