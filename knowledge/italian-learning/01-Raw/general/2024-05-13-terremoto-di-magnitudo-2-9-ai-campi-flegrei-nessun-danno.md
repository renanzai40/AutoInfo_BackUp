A 2.9 magnitude earthquake occurred in the Campi Flegrei area, with the epicenter located 2 kilometers from Pozzuoli. No damage was reported, and this event follows two other tremors that happened three days earlier.

## Key Points

- Epicenter was 2 kilometers from Pozzuoli
- No damage was reported from the earthquake
- Two other tremors occurred three days prior to this event
