## Summary
A 26-year-old ski mountaineer died in a mountain accident on the Ortles peak in South Tyrol. The incident occurred at about 3,700 meters along the steep Minnigerode couloir descending from a 3,905-meter peak.

## Key Points
- Fatal accident involving a ski mountaineer
- Location: Ortles peak in Alto Adige, South Tyrol
- Specific terrain: Minnigerode couloir at approximately 3,700 meters elevation
- Peak elevation: 3,905 meters
- Victim was 26 years old
