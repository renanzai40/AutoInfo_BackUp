## Summary
Elena Cecchettin, sister of Giulia Cecchettin who was killed in 2023, spoke at an event in Turin urging victims of violence to report their experiences. She emphasized that sharing is an act of strength and resistance.

## Key Points
- Elena Cecchettin advocates for victims of violence to speak out.
- The event was held at Salone internazionale di Torino with organizations La27ora and D.i.re.
- Giulia Cecchettin's death highlights issues of gender-based violence.
- Strength is framed as resistance through sharing experiences.
