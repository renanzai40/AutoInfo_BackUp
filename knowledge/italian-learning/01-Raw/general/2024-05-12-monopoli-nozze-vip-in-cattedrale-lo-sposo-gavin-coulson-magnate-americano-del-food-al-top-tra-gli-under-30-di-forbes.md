## Summary
A VIP wedding took place in Monopoli's cathedral, featuring Gavin Coulson, an American food magnate highlighted by Forbes as a top under-30 figure. Guests also enjoyed a visit on a $35 million super yacht named 'Crazy me' that arrived at the local port.

## Key Points
- Wedding was held in the main church of Monopoli
- Groom is Gavin Coulson, an American food magnate
- Gavin Coulson is recognized by Forbes among top under-30 figures
- Guests were treated to a tour on a super yacht called 'Crazy me'
- The super yacht is valued at $35 million
