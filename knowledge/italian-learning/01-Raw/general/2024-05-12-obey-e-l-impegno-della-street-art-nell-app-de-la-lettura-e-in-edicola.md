## Summary
The article features an interview with artist Shepard Fairey, known for the iconic Obama poster, in the supplement of 'la Lettura' publication. It emphasizes the social value of street art and graffiti, with extra content available in the app and a dedicated section for younger readers.

## Key Points
- Shepard Fairey, the author of the iconic Obama poster, is interviewed in the supplement.
- Fairey will have an exhibition in Milan.
- The 'la Lettura' app includes additional content on the social impact of graffiti.
- There is a section called 'la Lettura delle ragazze e dei ragazzi' for younger audiences.
