## Summary
Achille Occhetto, former secretary of the Italian Communist Party, revealed that three PCI leaders wanted Mikhail Gorbachev to excommunicate him, but Gorbachev refused during a 1989 meeting. He also mentioned that Enrico Berlinguer had considered changing the party's name earlier. The article highlights internal conflicts within the PCI during the late communist era.

## Key Points
- Achille Occhetto disclosed that PCI leaders Cossutta, Ingrao, and Tortorella sought Gorbachev's intervention to excommunicate him.
- Gorbachev refused the excommunication request during their 1989 meeting.
- Enrico Berlinguer had previously contemplated renaming the PCI party.
- The incident underscores internal tensions and political maneuvering within the Italian Communist Party.
