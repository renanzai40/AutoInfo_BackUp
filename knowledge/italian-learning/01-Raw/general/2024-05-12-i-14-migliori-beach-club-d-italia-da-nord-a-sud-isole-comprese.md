## Summary
The article announces the release of a guide ranking the 14 best beach clubs in Italy. It describes a rating system using umbrellas, with gold awards given to top establishments, and covers all regions including islands.

- The rating system uses umbrellas, from one to three, for evaluation.
- 14 beach clubs were awarded Gold status out of 225 evaluated.
- The guide includes establishments from all parts of Italy, including islands.
- Gold awards indicate the highest-rated beach clubs.
