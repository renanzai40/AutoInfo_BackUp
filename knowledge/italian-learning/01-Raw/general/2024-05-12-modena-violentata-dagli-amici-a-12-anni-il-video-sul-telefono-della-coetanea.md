## Summary
A 12-year-old girl in Modena was raped by her friends, and a video of the incident was found on a peer's phone. Doctors have confirmed the abuse, with suspects aged 15 and 16, and there are doubts about the purpose of the images.

## Key Points
- Victim is a 12-year-old girl in Modena.
- Abuse was committed by friends and recorded on a peer's phone.
- Medical professionals have confirmed the abuse.
- Suspects are minors aged 15 and 16.
- Uncertainty exists regarding the intent behind the video.
