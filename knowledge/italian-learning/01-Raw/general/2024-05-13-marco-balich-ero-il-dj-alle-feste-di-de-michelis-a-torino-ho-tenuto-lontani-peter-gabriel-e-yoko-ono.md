## Summary
Marco Balich, the grand master of ceremonies for the Olympics, shares anecdotes from his career including his time as a DJ at De Michelis's parties, keeping Peter Gabriel and Yoko Ono away during an event in Torino, and earning 18 million for an Indian wedding at Borgo Egnazia. He also mentions openness to becoming mayor of Venice.

## Key Points
- Marco Balich was a DJ at parties hosted by De Michelis.
- In Torino, he managed to keep Peter Gabriel and Yoko Ono away from an event.
- He is open to considering the role of mayor of Venice.
- He earned 18 million for organizing an Indian wedding at Borgo Egnazia.
