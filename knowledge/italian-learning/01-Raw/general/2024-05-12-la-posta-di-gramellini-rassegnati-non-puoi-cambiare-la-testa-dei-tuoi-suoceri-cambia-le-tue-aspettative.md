## Summary
The article advises that one cannot change the minds of in-laws and should instead adjust their own expectations. It recommends reducing contact while ensuring positive interactions when meeting.

## Key Points
- You cannot change your in-laws' behavior or opinions.
- Adjusting expectations can lead to less frustration.
- See in-laws less frequently but in a quality manner.
- The described family is intrusive, judgmental, and devaluing.
- Focus on self-management rather than trying to alter others.
