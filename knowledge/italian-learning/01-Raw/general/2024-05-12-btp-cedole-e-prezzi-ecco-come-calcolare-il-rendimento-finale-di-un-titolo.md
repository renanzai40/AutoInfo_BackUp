## Summary
The article explains that to calculate the final yield of a bond, such as Btp, one must consider not only the coupon payments but also any capital gains or losses if the bond is sold before its maturity date. It highlights the importance of a holistic assessment for accurate yield determination.

## Key Points
- Bond yield calculation involves both coupon income and capital gains/losses.
- Selling a bond before maturity can result in capital gains or losses that impact overall yield.
- Btp bonds are used as an example to illustrate these concepts.
- A comprehensive approach is necessary for assessing bond performance.
