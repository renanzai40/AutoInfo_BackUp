## Summary
Prosecutors allege that officials in Liguria, Italy, manipulated COVID-19 pandemic data, with Governor Giovanni Toti reportedly admitting to increasing infection numbers to his chief of staff Matteo Cozzani. The scandal involves tampering with official health statistics, raising concerns about data integrity.

## Key Points
- Allegations of COVID-19 data manipulation in Liguria by government officials.
- Governor Giovanni Toti accused of inflating infection counts in reports.
- Involvement of chief of staff Matteo Cozzani in the data tampering scandal.
- Prosecutors are investigating the misuse of pandemic statistics for potential misconduct.
- This incident highlights vulnerabilities in public health data accuracy and transparency.
