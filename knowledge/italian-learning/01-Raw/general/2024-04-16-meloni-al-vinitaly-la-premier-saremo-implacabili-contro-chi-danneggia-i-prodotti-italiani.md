## Summary
Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni spoke at Vinitaly, emphasizing strict measures against counterfeiting and 'Italian sounding' products that falsely claim Italian origin. She pledged to protect Italian product excellence and ensure authenticity by being implacable against offenders.

## Key Points
- Giorgia Meloni addressed counterfeiting and 'Italian sounding' issues at Vinitaly.
- The premier vowed to be implacable against those damaging Italian products.
- The focus is on protecting the integrity and excellence of Italian goods.
- This stance aims to combat misrepresentation of Italian origin in products.
