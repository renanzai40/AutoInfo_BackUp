## Summary
Italian tennis player Camila Giorgi is facing scrutiny from tax authorities over 6 million euros in earnings, excluding sponsorships. A tax folder led to a notification of seizure by the Agenzia delle Entrate riscossione in Firenze on June 26th.

## Key Points
- Camila Giorgi is under investigation by Italian tax authorities for potential unpaid taxes on her earnings.
- The focus is on 6 million euros in career earnings, not including sponsorships.
- A tax folder prompted Paolo Bracciali from the Agenzia delle Entrate riscossione in Firenze to notify a seizure on June 26th.
