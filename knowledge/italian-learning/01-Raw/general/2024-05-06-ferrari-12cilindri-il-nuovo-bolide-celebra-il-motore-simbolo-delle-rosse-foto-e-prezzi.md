## Summary
The Ferrari 12Cilindri is a new supercar from Maranello that pays tribute to the legendary V12 engine, which was originally used in the first Ferrari, the 125 S model from 1947. This latest model celebrates Ferrari's heritage with modern engineering and performance.

## Key Points
- Ferrari has launched the 12Cilindri, a new supercar model.
- The car is an homage to the iconic V12 engine that powered the first Ferrari in 1947.
- The V12 engine is a symbolic element of Ferrari's racing and engineering legacy.
- The 125 S from 1947 was the inaugural car produced by Ferrari, equipped with this V12 engine.
- The new model blends historical significance with contemporary automotive technology.
