## Summary
Xiaomi, a Chinese smartphone manufacturer, has set a new production record for its SU7 electric car by producing 10,000 units in a single month. The SU7 is a sales champion, and Xiaomi plans to expand sales to Spain.

## Key Points
- Xiaomi's SU7 electric car set a production record of 10,000 units in one month.
- The SU7 has become a sales champion in the electric vehicle market.
- Xiaomi is planning to sell the SU7 in Spain.
- Xiaomi has successfully diversified from smartphones to electric vehicles.
