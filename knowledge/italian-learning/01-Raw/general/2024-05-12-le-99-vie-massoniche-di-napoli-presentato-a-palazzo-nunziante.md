## Summary
The article announces the presentation of Antonio Piedimonte's latest work, 'Le 99 vie massoniche di Napoli,' which will be held on Monday, May 13, at the Banca Fideuram headquarters in Palazzo Nunziante. The work explores the masonic aspects of Naples.

## Key Points
- Presentation of Antonio Piedimonte's new work 'Le 99 vie massoniche di Napoli'
- Scheduled for Monday, May 13, at Palazzo Nunziante
- Hosted at the Banca Fideuram headquarters
- The work focuses on the 99 masonic roads of Naples
