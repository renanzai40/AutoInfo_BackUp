## Summary
Italy is described as challenging for mothers, with one in five women leaving their jobs after the first child. Data from Save the Children reveals that 72.8% of resignations among new parents are women, highlighting significant gender disparities in workforce participation post-childbirth.

## Key Points
- One in five Italian mothers leaves work after having their first child.
- 72.8% of resignation approvals for new parents are women.
- The findings come from the Save the Children report 'Le equilibriste'.
- This underscores systemic issues with work-life balance and gender equality in Italy.
- The data emphasizes the need for improved support systems for working mothers.
