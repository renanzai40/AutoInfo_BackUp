## Summary
The European Union is scrutinizing new baggage scanners in airports because their anti-explosive algorithm does not meet required standards. These scanners use CT technology to scan suitcases without unpacking, but they have limitations in analyzing liquids, only capable for containers up to 330 milliliters.

## Key Points
- EU authorities are questioning the effectiveness of new airport baggage scanner algorithms.
- Scanners employ CT technology for efficient, non-invasive scanning of luggage.
- The software for liquid analysis is restricted to containers with a maximum volume of 330 ml.
- This limitation may impact security protocols and passenger convenience at airports.
