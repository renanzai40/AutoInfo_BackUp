## Summary
Novak Djokovic, the top seed at the ATP Rome tournament, was eliminated in the third round by Chilean player Tabilo. This early loss could affect rankings, giving Jannik Sinner a chance to become world number one at Roland Garros.

## Key Points
- Novak Djokovic lost to Tabilo in the third round of the ATP Rome tournament.
- Djokovic was defeated in straight sets, 6-2, 6-3.
- The defeat could impact the upcoming Roland Garros tournament.
- Jannik Sinner has an opportunity to claim the number one ranking.
