## Summary
An artistic installation known as a 'visual portal' has been set up in Dublin and New York, allowing people from both cities to see each other in real-time. This street-based exhibit enables individuals to meet face to face despite the geographical distance.

## Key Points
- The installation is an artistic exhibit installed on public streets.
- It visually connects Dublin and New York.
- It allows real-time interaction between people in the two cities.
- It facilitates a face-to-face meeting experience.
