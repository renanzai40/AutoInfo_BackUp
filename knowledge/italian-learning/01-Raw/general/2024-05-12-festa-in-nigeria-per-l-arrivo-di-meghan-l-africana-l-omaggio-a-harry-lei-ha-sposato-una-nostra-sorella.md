## Summary
A celebration in Nigeria welcomed Meghan Markle, the Duchess of Sussex, acknowledging her African heritage through a DNA test revealing she is 43% Nigerian. Locals honored her as part of their community, with tributes to Prince Harry emphasizing familial bonds. The event highlighted her pride in her genetic connection to Nigeria.

## Key Points
- Meghan Markle was celebrated in Nigeria for her African roots.
- A genetic test indicated she is 43% Nigerian.
- The duchess expressed pride in her DNA and connection to Nigeria.
- Tributes were made to Prince Harry, underscoring family ties.
