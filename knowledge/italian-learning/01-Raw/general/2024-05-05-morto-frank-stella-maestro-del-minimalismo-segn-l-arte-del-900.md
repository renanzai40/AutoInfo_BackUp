## Summary
Frank Stella, a renowned American artist and master of minimalism, has passed away at the age of 88. He significantly influenced 20th-century art through his distinctive use of geometric motifs in paintings and sculptures.

## Key Points
- Frank Stella died at age 88, having worked as an artist until the end.
- He was a prominent figure in the minimalism art movement.
- His art is characterized by geometric designs on canvases and statues.
- Stella made significant contributions to American art as a painter, sculptor, and printmaker.
