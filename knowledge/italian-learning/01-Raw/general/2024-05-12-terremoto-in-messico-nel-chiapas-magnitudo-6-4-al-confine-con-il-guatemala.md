## Summary
A magnitude 6.4 earthquake struck Chiapas, Mexico, near the border with Guatemala. The quake was documented by the United States Geological Survey at a depth of 75.4 kilometers, resulting in landslides in Guatemala.

## Key Points
- Earthquake magnitude of 6.4 in Chiapas, Mexico
- Location near the border with Guatemala
- Documented by the United States Geological Survey at a depth of 75.4 kilometers
- Caused landslides in Guatemala
