## Summary
The Bugatti Bolide is a hypercar with an impressive 1,578 horsepower. However, it cannot be driven to its full potential because no existing race track has a straight long enough to unleash all its power. This limitation restricts the car's performance even on the track.

## Key Points
- The Bugatti Bolide is a hypercar developed by the historic brand Bugatti.
- It features an engine output of 1,578 horsepower.
- No circuit has a straight long enough to fully utilize the car's power.
- This means the Bugatti Bolide cannot be driven at its maximum potential even on race tracks.
