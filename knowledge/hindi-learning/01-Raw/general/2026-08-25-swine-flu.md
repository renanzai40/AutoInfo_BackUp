## Summary
Swine flu cases are rapidly increasing in several Indian states, including Delhi, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Uttar Pradesh. As a result, there has been a sudden surge in demand for a medicine that helps reduce the effects of the flu.

## Key Points
- Swine flu cases are escalating across multiple Indian states.
- Specific states affected include Delhi, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Uttar Pradesh.
- There is a sharp increase in sales of a medicine used to mitigate swine flu effects.
- The rise in cases is directly driving the demand for this medicine.
