## Original Content
मुंबई से अहमदाबाद जा रही वंदे भारत एक्सप्रेस पर सूरत के पास पथराव हुआ. उत्तराण और कोसाड के बीच हुई इस घटना के बाद एक संदिग्ध को गिरफ्तार किया गया और उससे पूछताछ की जा रही है. ट्रेन में यूपी की राज्यपाल आनंदीबेन पटेल और दमन-दीव, दादरा एवं नगर हवेली के प्रशासक प्रफुल पटेल भी सवार थे.

## Summary
The Vande Bharat Express train traveling from Mumbai to Ahmedabad was pelted with stones near Surat, between Utran and Kosad. Uttar Pradesh Governor Anandiben Patel and Praful Patel, administrator of Daman-Diu, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, were on board during the incident. A suspect has been arrested and is under interrogation for the stone-pelting.

## Key Points
- Stone-pelting incident occurred on the Vande Bharat Express near Surat.
- High-profile passengers included UP Governor Anandiben Patel and administrator Praful Patel.
- One suspect has been arrested and is being interrogated.
- The incident took place between Utran and Kosad locations.
- This highlights security concerns for premium train services in India.
