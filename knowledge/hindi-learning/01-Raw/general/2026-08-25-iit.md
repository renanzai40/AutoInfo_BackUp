## Original Content
IIT दिल्ली में 31 वर्षीय एमएससी फिजिक्स छात्र ऋषिकेश कुमार के सुसाइड केस में नया मोड़ आया है. 1 करोड़ का मुआवजा, बाहरी समिति की जांच और डीन के इस्तीफे की मांग लगातार बनी हुई है लेकिन छात्रों ने प्रशासन के जवाब को “अपर्याप्त और टालमटोल वाला” बताते हुए मांगों के पूरा न होने तक प्रदर्शन जारी रखना की बात कही है.

## Summary
A new development has emerged in the suicide case of Rishikesh Kumar, a 31-year-old MSc Physics student at IIT Delhi. Students are dissatisfied with the administration's response, labeling it inadequate and evasive, and are demanding compensation, an external investigation, and the dean's resignation. Protests will continue until these demands are met.

## Key Points
- Rishikesh Kumar, an MSc Physics student at IIT Delhi, committed suicide, leading to ongoing protests.
- Students demand 1 crore rupees compensation, an external investigation, and the dean's resignation.
- The administration's response has been criticized as insufficient and evasive by students.
- Protests are set to continue until the demands are fully addressed.
- This case marks a new twist in the incident, highlighting student dissatisfaction with institutional actions.
