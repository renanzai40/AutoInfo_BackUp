## Original Content
नेपाल के प्रधानमंत्री बालेन शाह संसद की कार्यवाही में काला चश्मा पहनकर शामिल होंगे, जबकि स्पीकर ने इसे बैन कर दिया था. पीएम शाह ने अपनी आंखों की लाइट सेंसिटिविटी और ड्राई आइज की समस्या का मेडिकल सर्टिफिकेट संसद को सौंपा है. यही वजह से कि वो अपना काला चश्मा नहीं उतार सकते हैं.

## Summary
Nepal's Prime Minister Balen Shah will wear sunglasses in parliament despite a ban imposed by the speaker. He has submitted a medical certificate citing light sensitivity and dry eyes as reasons for needing to wear them. This highlights a conflict between parliamentary rules and medical accommodations.

## Key Points
- Balen Shah, Nepal's PM, insists on wearing sunglasses during parliament sessions.
- The speaker of parliament had previously banned sunglasses in sessions.
- Shah provided a medical certificate for light sensitivity and dry eyes to justify his actions.
- This situation underscores the intersection of political procedures and health-related needs.
- The incident involves a debate on rule enforcement versus individual medical exceptions.
