राजस्थान PTI भर्ती-2022 के फर्जी B.P.Ed डिग्री मामले में SOG ने यूनिवर्सिटी के कुलपति समेत 3 लोगों को गिरफ्तार किया है. इस सिलसिले में 66 अभ्यर्थियों की डिग्रियां जांच के घेरे में हैं. जानें क्या है ये पूरा मामला.

## Summary
In the Rajasthan PTI Recruitment-2022 case, three individuals including a university Vice Chancellor were arrested for using fake B.P.Ed degrees to secure government jobs. The SOG is investigating, with 66 candidates' degrees under scrutiny.

- Arrest of three people, including the Vice Chancellor, for using fake B.P.Ed degrees
- Fake degrees involved in Rajasthan PTI Recruitment-2022 for government positions
- 66 candidates' degrees are under investigation
- SOG is leading the probe into the fraud
