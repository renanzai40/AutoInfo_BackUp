## Summary
Swine flu cases are rapidly increasing in several Indian states, leading the health ministry to alert and order hospitals to prepare isolation beds. ICMR has clarified that this is not a new H1N1 strain but a common seasonal flu.

## Key Points
- Rapid increase in swine flu cases across multiple states in India.
- Health ministry is on alert and has mandated preparation of isolation beds in hospitals.
- ICMR states the outbreak is not caused by a new H1N1 strain.
- It is identified as a typical form of seasonal flu.
