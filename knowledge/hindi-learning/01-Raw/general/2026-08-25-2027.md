## Summary
A super El Niño has broken previous heat records, and scientists warn it has not yet reached its peak. Its increasing strength in 2026-27 could significantly affect global temperatures, weather patterns, and rainfall in countries including India.

## Key Points
- Super El Niño has caused record-breaking increases in heat levels.
- Scientists state that El Niño has not yet peaked in intensity.
- The growing strength of El Niño in 2026-27 may impact global temperature, weather, and rain patterns in multiple countries, particularly India.
