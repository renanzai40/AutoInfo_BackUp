## Summary
The article discusses the introduction of a new anti-paper leak law following Gen-Z protests at Jantar Mantar, which aims to make paper leaks a serious crime. It raises concerns about whether the law will effectively stop leaks or if the paper leak mafia will still evade it.

## Key Points
- Gen-Z protested at Jantar Mantar against paper leaks.
- The government introduced an anti-leak law as a response to protests.
- The law makes paper leaks a more serious criminal offense.
- There is speculation on the law's effectiveness in deterring the paper leak mafia.
