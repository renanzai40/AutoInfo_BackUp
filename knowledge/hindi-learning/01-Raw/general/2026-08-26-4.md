## Summary
The US has imposed sanctions on four India-based companies and three Indian citizens for their involvement in Iran's oil and petrochemical trade, aiming to pressure Iran through secondary sanctions affecting third-country entities.

## Key Points
- US sanctions target four India-based companies linked to Iran's oil and petrochemical trade.
- Three Indian individuals have been specifically sanctioned by the US.
- This action is part of broader US efforts to enforce restrictions on Iran's energy sector.
- Sanctions impact international trade dynamics between India, US, and Iran.
