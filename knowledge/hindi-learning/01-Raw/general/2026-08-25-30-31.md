## Original Content
भाद्रपद कृष्ण पक्ष की तृतीया तिथि 30 अगस्त 2026 को रात 9 बजकर 39 मिनट पर शुरू होगी और 31 अगस्त को रात 8 बजकर 53 मिनट पर समाप्त होगी. उदया तिथि की मान्यता के चलते कजरी तीज का पर्व 31 अगस्त दिन सोमवार को मनाया जाएगा.

## Summary
The article specifies that Kajri Teej festival in 2026 will be celebrated on 31 August, based on the Tritiya tithi of Krishna Paksha in Bhadrapada. The tithi starts on 30 August at 9:39 PM and ends on 31 August at 8:53 PM, with the festival date determined by the Udaya tithi principle.

## Key Points
- Kajri Teej is observed on Tritiya tithi in Krishna Paksha of the Bhadrapada month.
- In 2026, the Tritiya tithi begins on 30 August at 9:39 PM and ends on 31 August at 8:53 PM.
- Due to the belief in Udaya tithi, the festival date is 31 August 2026.
- 31 August 2026 is a Monday, which is mentioned as the day of celebration.
