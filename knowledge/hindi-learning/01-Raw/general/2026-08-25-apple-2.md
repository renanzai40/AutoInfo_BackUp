आने वाले समय में ऐपल का फ्लैगशिप 2 लाख रुपये से शुरू हो तो हैरानी की बात नहीं होगी. जिस तेजी से स्मार्टफोन की कीमतें बढ़ रही हैं उसे देख कर ऐसा ही लगता है. ताजा रिपोर्ट के मुताबिक ऐपल अपने आईफोन को और भी महंगा कर सकता है.

## Summary
Apple is likely to increase the price of its iPhones in the future, with flagship models potentially starting at over 2 lakh rupees. This is based on recent reports indicating Apple's plans to make iPhones more expensive amid the rising trend in smartphone prices.

## Key Points
- Apple plans to raise iPhone prices, with flagship models possibly starting at 2 lakh rupees.
- The increase is driven by the rapid rise in smartphone prices globally.
- Recent reports suggest Apple's strategy to enhance iPhone pricing.
- This could lead to higher costs for consumers in the future.
