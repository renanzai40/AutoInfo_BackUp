## Summary
Akhilesh Yadav aims to change the Samajwadi Party's old image in Uttar Pradesh. After the 2022 assembly elections, the party was still seen through its traditional social base, but it is now attempting to adopt a more inclusive approach.

## Key Points
- Akhilesh Yadav is focused on rebranding the Samajwadi Party's image.
- SP has been traditionally viewed through its specific social and demographic base.
- The party is now conveying a broader, more inclusive message to appeal to a wider audience.
- This shift is occurring post the 2022 UP assembly elections.
- The effort aims to modernize SP's perception beyond historical associations.
