## Summary
Canada has imposed retaliatory tariffs on approximately $20 billion worth of American goods, including steel, dairy products, and other items, with some tariffs reaching up to 50%. This move is in response to trade policies by the United States, likely under the Trump administration.

## Key Points
- Canada imposed tariffs on $20 billion of American goods as a retaliatory measure.
- Tariffs cover steel, dairy products, household appliances, agricultural equipment, and everyday items.
- Some tariffs are as high as 50%, indicating a significant trade dispute.
- The action reflects ongoing trade tensions between Canada and the US.
