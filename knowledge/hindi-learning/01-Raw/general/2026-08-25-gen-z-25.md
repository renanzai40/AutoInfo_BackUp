## Original Content
यूपी में Gen-Z युवाओं को योगी सरकार 25 लाख मुफ्त टैबलेट देने की तैयारी में है. इसके लिए वित्तीय वर्ष 2026-27 में 2,374.60 करोड़ रुपये का बजट प्रावधान किया गया है. स्वामी विवेकानंद युवा सशक्तीकरण योजना के तहत स्नातक, स्नातकोत्तर, डिप्लोमा, ITI और कौशल विकास से जुड़े युवाओं को इसका लाभ मिलेगा.

## Summary
The Yogi government in Uttar Pradesh is preparing to distribute free tablets to 25 lakh Gen-Z students under the Swami Vivekananda Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana. A budget of 2374.60 crore rupees has been allocated for the financial year 2026-27 to fund this initiative targeting graduates, post-graduates, diploma holders, ITI students, and those in skill development programs. This scheme aims to empower young people through digital tools.

## Key Points
- 25 lakh free tablets will be distributed to Gen-Z youth in Uttar Pradesh.
- A budget of 2374.60 crore rupees is allocated for the financial year 2026-27.
- The scheme is part of the Swami Vivekananda Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana.
- It benefits graduates, post-graduates, diploma holders, ITI students, and skill development participants.
- The initiative aims to enhance education and skill development through technology.
