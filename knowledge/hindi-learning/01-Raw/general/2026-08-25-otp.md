## Summary
The article explains eSIM swap scams, where gullible individuals are targeted to have their SIM cards swapped, leading to unauthorized access and draining of their bank accounts without their knowledge. It emphasizes the need for awareness to prevent such frauds.

## Key Points
- SIM swap scams exploit unsuspecting people by swapping their SIM cards.
- Victims' bank accounts can be emptied without their awareness until it is too late.
- Understanding the details of this scam is crucial for prevention and protection.
