मध्य प्रदेश के मंदसौर जिला अस्पताल से करीब 4 लाख रुपये के गहनों और नकदी से भरा बैग गायब हो गया। CCTV में चौंकाने वाला खुलासा हुआ कि बैग एक कुत्ता मुंह में दबाकर ले गया था। पुलिस ने CCTV के जरिए कुत्ते की गतिविधियां ट्रेस कर बैग बरामद कर लिया। महिला ने राहत की सांस ली।

## Summary
A bag containing jewelry and cash worth approximately 400,000 rupees was stolen from Mandsaur district hospital in Madhya Pradesh. CCTV footage revealed that a dog had taken the bag in its mouth, and police used the footage to trace and recover it. The woman who owned the bag expressed relief upon its recovery.

## Key Points
- The theft occurred at Mandsaur district hospital in Madhya Pradesh.
- CCTV footage showed a dog as the thief, taking the bag with jewelry and cash.
- Police utilized CCTV to track the dog's activities and successfully recovered the bag.
- The incident involved valuable items, including jewelry and cash, leading to initial concern.
- The owner of the bag was relieved after the recovery, highlighting the resolution of the incident.
