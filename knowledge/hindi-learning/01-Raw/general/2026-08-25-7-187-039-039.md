## Original Content
पिछले 7 दिनों में 4.5 या उससे ज्यादा तीव्रता के 187 भूकंप दर्ज हुए. इनमें कई झटके पैसिफिक रिंग ऑफ फायर में आए. जापान, इंडोनेशिया, अलास्का और अमेरिका में हुई इस हलचल ने फिर भूकंपों पर ध्यान खींचा है.

## Summary
Over the past 7 days, 187 earthquakes with magnitudes of 4.5 or higher have been recorded globally. A significant number of these tremors occurred in the Pacific Ring of Fire, with notable activity in Japan, Indonesia, Alaska, and the United States. This increased seismic activity has renewed attention on earthquake risks.

## Key Points
- 187 earthquakes of magnitude 4.5 or greater occurred in the last 7 days.
- Many tremors were concentrated in the Pacific Ring of Fire region.
- Earthquake activity was reported in Japan, Indonesia, Alaska, and the United States.
- The surge in earthquakes has heightened public and scientific focus on seismic events.
