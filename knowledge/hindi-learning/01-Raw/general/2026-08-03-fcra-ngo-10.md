## Summary
The article discusses the FCRA bill, which aims to regulate foreign contributions to NGOs by imposing stricter controls on organizations receiving foreign funds. Opposition parties are expected to oppose the bill, fearing it could restrict the operational freedom of NGOs.

## Key Points
- The FCRA bill targets NGOs with foreign funding and introduces regulatory changes.
- The bill includes 10 specific modifications to existing regulations.
- Opposition parties plan to oppose the bill due to concerns over NGO independence.
- The aim is to tighten oversight and control of foreign contributions to NGOs.
