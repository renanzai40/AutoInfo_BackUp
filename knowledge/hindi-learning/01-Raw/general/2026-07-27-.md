## Summary
Assam experiences annual floods due to the Brahmaputra river's vast water flow, heavy silt from the Himalayas, the valley's mountainous geography, simultaneous heavy rainfall in the northeast, and climate change. Experts state that completely preventing these floods is not possible.

## Key Points
- The Brahmaputra river's massive water flow is a primary cause of flooding in Assam.
- Heavy silt deposits from the Himalayas contribute to the severity of floods.
- The geographical feature of Assam being a valley surrounded by mountains intensifies water accumulation.
- Simultaneous heavy rainfall in the northeast region exacerbates flood conditions.
- Climate change is a significant factor influencing the frequency and impact of floods.
