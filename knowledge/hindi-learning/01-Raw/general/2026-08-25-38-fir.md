## Summary
In Ranchi, Jharkhand, protests erupted against alleged irregularities in recruitment exams, during which a female activist was mistreated. An FIR has been filed against 38 individuals in connection with the incident. The matter has led to a political confrontation between BJP and JMM.

## Key Points
- Protests in Ranchi, Jharkhand, were held against alleged irregularities in recruitment exams.
- A female activist was allegedly mistreated during the protests.
- FIRs have been filed against 38 people related to the incident.
- BJP and JMM are opposing each other over the political fallout from the event.
