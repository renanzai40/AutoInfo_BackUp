## Content

### The Basics of News Analysis

News articles serve as primary sources for learning about current events, and analyzing them helps students grasp real-world developments. For example, sports news can report on a tennis victory at the US Open mixed doubles (Source: https://www.sport-interfax.ru/1111239), while social news highlights human interest stories, such as a veteran celebrating his 100th birthday (Source: https://tass.ru/obschestvo/28044341). Recognizing these categories is essential for efficient information processing.

Effective news analysis begins with checking sources and verifying facts to ensure reliability. Many articles provide citations or references to official bodies, like updates on a spacewalk by NASA and ESA astronauts (Source: https://tass.ru/kosmos/28044321). Students should always look for these details to distinguish credible reporting from potential misinformation, fostering habits of media literacy from an early stage.



### Geopolitical Events and International Relations

Geopolitical news focuses on international relations, conflicts, and diplomacy, which shape global affairs. ### Economic and Business News

Economic news provides insights into market trends, trade policies, and financial health. Oil prices, for example, have declined significantly, with Brent crude falling to $88.33 per barrel (Source: https://www.interfax.ru/business/1111215), reflecting broader global economic shifts. Canada's new tariffs on US goods worth nearly $28 billion aim to address trade imbalances (Source: https://tass.ru/ekonomika/28044009), while the EU reported a 16.9% year-over-year increase in automotive fuel prices (Source: https://www.interfax.ru/business/1111196).
