## Context

What is the situation that forces the decision? What problem are we solving?

backstory needed to understand the choice. Keep it factual — the *why*.

## Decision

wording that belongs in `AGENTS.md` Architecture Rules or a spec section.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative**: what it was
- **Why rejected**: the specific reason it lost (cost, throughput, correctness,

## Consequences
