## Executive Summary

The knowledge base spans a broad clinical and translational landscape, with reproductive medicine and assisted reproductive technology (ART) representing the largest evidence cluster. Large retrospective cohorts drive many of the most robust signals: a population-based study of 99,484 IVF cycles linked benzodiazepine use during the cycle to increased miscarriage risk, while a national cohort of 21,004 clomiphene citrate cycles demonstrated dose-dependent perinatal risks. In 2,571 women with PMOS/PCOS, preconception 1-hour plasma glucose emerged as an early predictor of gestational diabetes, and machine-learning analysis of 4,300 vitrified-warmed single euploid embryo transfers identified modifiable factors associated with clinical pregnancy. Ancillary ART work clarified that active smoking worsens reproductive outcomes, higher psychological resilience correlates with better blastocyst quality in 126 ICSI couples, and expanded carrier screening panels do not fully align with conditions that ultimately drive PGT-M use.

Surgical and interventional evidence is dominated by systematic reviews and meta-analyses, many with actionable conclusions. Adjunctive platelet-rich plasma improves healing in non-union lower extremity fractures; intraoperative CT reduces the intraoperative revision rate in maxillofacial fracture surgery; and combined monoclonal antibody–chemoradiotherapy showed efficacy in 776 Chinese patients with locally advanced cervical cancer across 18 studies. In neonatology, a meta-analysis of 12 trials (2,344 extremely preterm infants) found active pharmacotherapy for patent ductus arteriosus alters key outcomes, and an overview of 20 systematic reviews (137 RCTs, 18,941 participants) found only low-to-moderate certainty evidence supporting prehabilitation in adult cancer patients. Several important topics remain protocol-stage only—including non-pharmacological interventions for pregnancy-related lumbopelvic pain, pelvic physiotherapy effects on uterine blood flow, and postpartum hand expression—indicating that evidence generation is still in progress.

Cross-cutting themes include health equity, technology evaluation, and methodology. A mixed-methods study of 176 Black women found over a quarter felt under-supported in infertility care, citing specific gaps in support. The Ukraine casualty management paper illustrates how military and civilian health systems operationalize the Eight Domains of Mass Casualty Management under extreme strain. Emerging technologies—dynamic SPECT/CT, microfluidics for sperm selection, AI for infectious disease surveillance, molecular profiling for cancer of unknown primary, and surface-engineered nanocarriers for HSV—were consistently framed as promising but requiring further validation and implementation safeguards.


## Key Findings


- **Benzodiazepine exposure during IVF is a miscarriage risk factor**: In 99,484 women, benzodiazepine use during the IVF cycle was associated with an increased risk of miscarriage and adverse pregnancy outcomes, supporting avoidance or caution in cycle management.

- **Clomiphene citrate has dose-dependent perinatal risks**: Analysis of 21,004 fresh autologous IVF cycles in the US (2004–2021) showed that higher clomiphene citrate doses were associated with increased perinatal risks, suggesting the lowest effective dose should be used.

- **Preconception glucose predicts GDM in PMOS/PCOS IVF patients**: In 2,571 women, a 1-hour plasma glucose measurement before conception served as an early indicator of gestational diabetes risk, offering a simple screening opportunity prior to IVF.

- **Machine learning can stratify euploid transfer success**: Analysis of 4,300 vitrified-warmed single euploid embryo transfer cycles identified factors associated with clinical pregnancy, demonstrating the potential of ML-based decision support in ART.

- **Oocyte vitrification alters aneuploidy patterns in advanced maternal age**: Compared with fresh oocytes, vitrified-warmed oocytes showed chromosome-specific differences in aneuploidy, with implications for counseling older patients. Midway PGT-A—testing residual vitrified-thawed embryos after repeated biopsy—showed clinical validity comparable to traditional PGT-A.

- **Smoking adversely affects ART outcomes**: A systematic review and meta-analysis restricted to active smokers versus never-smokers found that active smoking compromises reproductive outcomes in women undergoing assisted reproduction.

- **Psychological resilience is linked to blastocyst quality**: In a prospective study of 126 infertile couples undergoing ICSI, higher resilience scores—in both partners, with sex-specific patterns—were associated with better blastocyst quality.

- **PRP improves non-union fracture healing**: Adjunctive platelet-rich plasma in non-union lower extremity fractures was associated with improved healing rates, supporting its use as a surgical adjunct.

- **Intraoperative CT reduces maxillofacial revision surgery**: A systematic review and meta-analysis found iCT warranted in specific maxillofacial fracture patterns, lowering the intraoperative revision rate.

- **Monoclonal antibodies plus chemoradiotherapy benefit cervical cancer patients**: Across 18 studies with 776 Chinese patients, combination therapy improved efficacy endpoints with a manageable safety profile in locally advanced disease.

- **Pharmacotherapy for PDA in preterm infants shifts outcomes**: A meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis of 12 trials (2,344 extremely preterm infants) found active pharmacotherapy changes clinical outcomes compared with expectant management, though certainty and effect size should guide judicious use.

- **Prehabilitation evidence is only low-to-moderate certainty**: Twenty systematic reviews encompassing 137 RCTs and 18,941 participants showed benefits of prehabilitation in adult cancer patients, but the overview emphasizes the need for higher-certainty evidence.

- **Second-hand smoke exposure in pregnancy is a modifiable ASD risk factor**: Maternal exposure to second-hand smoke during pregnancy was associated with an increased risk of autism spectrum disorder in offspring.

- **Expanded carrier screening does not fully predict PGT-M conditions**: A real-world cohort found misalignment between ECS panel content and the conditions that actually lead to PGT-M, arguing for broader or more targeted panels.

- **Fertility care is inequitable for Black women**: 176 Black women seeking infertility care reported feeling under-supported, with specific recommendations for culturally competent, patient-centered support services.

- **Dynamic SPECT/CT extends beyond static imaging**: Systematic review evidence supports expanding dynamic SPECT/CT from research into clinical applications across cardiology, nephrology, and oncology, adding functional and pharmacokinetic data.

- **Military and civilian systems can operationalize mass casualty domains**: The Ukraine experience demonstrates adaptation of the Eight Domains framework under high casualty loads, providing lessons for system resilience.

- **Mechanistic insights into embryonic genome activation**: High-resolution transcriptomic mapping of mouse EGA revealed a decoupling of transcription activation from precocious H3K4me3 removal, refining the epigenetic model of EGA.




## Recommendations


- **Adopt risk-screening and mitigation in ART protocols**: Integrate preconception glucose testing, smoking-cessation counseling, and benzodiazepine deprescribing into routine IVF care, given the large-cohort associations with miscarriage and perinatal outcomes. Use the lowest effective clomiphene citrate dose.

- **Leverage machine learning as a clinical decision-support tool**: Validate and implement ML-based prediction models—such as those developed for euploid transfer success and IVF outcome factors—to personalize embryo transfer and stimulation strategies, with prospective evaluation before widespread adoption.

- **Apply surgical adjuncts where evidence is strongest**: Expand use of platelet-rich plasma for non-union lower extremity fractures and intraoperative CT for select maxillofacial fractures, while ensuring resource allocation matches demonstrated revision-rate benefits.

- **Strengthen equity and support structures in fertility care**: Act on the documented support needs of Black women seeking infertility care by redesigning patient navigation, education, and psychosocial services to be culturally responsive and inclusive.

- **Complete and publish pending protocols to close evidence gaps**: Prioritize the systematic reviews on non-pharmacological interventions for pregnancy-related lumbopelvic pain, pelvic physiotherapy and uterine blood flow, postpartum hand expression, and traditional Chinese medicine for diabetes-related pruritus, so that current protocol-stage work can inform clinical guidance.

- **Encourage rigorous evaluation of emerging technologies**: For dynamic SPECT/CT, microfluidics-based sperm selection, AI-based infectious disease surveillance, molecular profiling for cancer of unknown primary, and nanocarrier HSV therapies, require comparative effectiveness, cost, and safety data before routine clinical adoption.

- **Translate mass casualty lessons into structured preparedness**: Apply the Eight Domains framework validated in Ukraine to civilian and military health systems, focusing on surge adaptation, triage, and interoperable command structures.

- **Screen and counsel for environmental exposures in pregnancy**: Given the second-hand smoke–ASD association, incorporate household exposure screening into prenatal and preconception care and couple it with targeted cessation interventions for partners and household members.
